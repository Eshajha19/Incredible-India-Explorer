HISTORY

Harike Ki Kahani

Harike Wetland 1953 mein Beas aur Sutlej dariyaon par ek barrage bana kar cheate ke roop mein wajood mein aaya — jiska maqsad tha Rajasthan Canal (ab Indira Gandhi Canal) ke liye paani supply karna. Jaise-jaise saalon mein paani jama hota gaya, ye ek bada, hara-bhara wetland ban gaya jo dheere-dheere ek prime bird habitat ban gaya. Aaj ye Punjab ka sabse bada wetland hai aur North India ke sabse important biodiversity hotspots mein se ek.

GEOGRAPHY

Beas–Sutlej Sangam

Harike wetland uss jagah par sthit hai jahan Beas aur Sutlej dariya aapas mein mil jaate hain — Punjab ke Tarn Taran aur Ferozepur zilon ki border par. Ye sangam wetland ko nutrient-rich paani dono taraf se deta hai, jisse yahan ka ecosystem itna rich aur diverse ban paata hai. Confluence ke aas-paas kai chhote-chhote river islands bhi hain jo wildlife ke liye safe breeding grounds ka kaam karte hain.

CONSERVATION STATUS

Ramsar Site

1990 mein Harike Wetland ko Ramsar Convention ke tehat ek "Wetland of International Importance" ghoshit kiya gaya. Ye designation isse global level par protection aur recognition deta hai, aur India ke top wetlands ki conservation strategy ka ek important hissa banata hai.

PROTECTING THE WETLAND

Conservation

Harike Wetland ko kai challenges ka samna hai — pollution, invasive species (jaise water hyacinth), aur siltation (dariyaon se aane wali gaad wetland ko dheere-dheere bhar rahi hai). Punjab Wildlife Department aur various NGOs mil kar regular water quality monitoring, invasive species removal, aur local communities ke saath awareness programs chala rahe hain taaki ye wetland aane wale saalon mein bhi migratory birds ka safe ghar bana rahe.
