Sri Venkateswara National Park

Explore the rugged beauty of the Eastern Ghats. Trek through dry deciduous forests, discover majestic cascading waterfalls, and uncover a landscape deeply entwined with spiritual heritage.

Explore Wildlife View Map
Landscape

Eastern Ghats & Flora

Mountain Terrain

Located in the Seshachalam Hills, the park features deep gorges, rugged ridges, and steep valleys characteristic of the ancient Eastern Ghats geological formation.

Majestic Waterfalls

Home to the spectacular Talakona waterfall (highest in AP) and Gundalakona. These cascades create micro-habitats crucial for amphibians and unique flora.

Flora & Red Sanders

The tropical dry deciduous forest is globally significant as one of the last strongholds of the highly endangered and valuable Red Sanders (Pterocarpus santalinus) tree.

Fauna

Wildlife of the Ghats

Discover the elusive species navigating the rocky terrain.

Indian Leopard

Vulnerable

The apex predator of these hills, highly adapted to the rocky outcrops and dry scrub forests.

Sloth Bear

Vulnerable

Frequently found foraging for termites and fruits in the lower valleys and forested slopes.

Indian Giant Squirrel

Least Concern

A striking, large arboreal squirrel often seen leaping through the canopy in the denser, moister valleys.

Indian Pangolin

Endangered

A highly reclusive, scale-covered mammal that relies on the park's ant and termite mounds.

Chital

Least Concern

Graceful spotted deer that form the primary prey base for the park's leopard population.

Jungle Cat

Least Concern

A medium-sized feline adapted to the dry scrublands and tall grass patches of the Ghats.

Adventure

Trekking & Waterfalls

The park offers excellent eco-tourism and hiking opportunities.

  • Talakona Trek: A scenic trail leading to the stunning 270-foot Talakona Waterfall, passing through dense canopy and medicinal plants.
  • Canopy Walk: Experience the forest from above on the suspended canopy walkway near Talakona.
  • Best Season: Post-monsoon (October to February) when the waterfalls are at their most vibrant.
Heritage

Sacred Forests

The ecosystem is intertwined with the spiritual heart of Southern India.

  • Tirumala Connection: The park encompasses the sacred Tirumala Hills, home to the world-renowned Sri Venkateswara Temple.
  • Pilgrimage Routes: Ancient footpaths cross through the park's forests, used by millions of devotees annually.
  • Conservation: The religious significance has historically aided in protecting patches of old-growth forest.
