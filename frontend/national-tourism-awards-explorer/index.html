📜 History of the Awards

The National Tourism Awards are presented annually by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India to various segments of the travel, tourism, and hospitality industry. The awards have been given since the early 1990s, emerging over the years as a prestigious recognition of achievements in the sector.

Genesis and Vision

The awards were instituted to recognize and encourage healthy competition among State Governments and Union Territories, classified hotels, heritage hotels, approved travel agents, tour operators, tourist transport operators, individuals, and other private organizations — with the overarching aim of promoting tourism across the country.

Evolution Over the Years

Tourism is a dynamic industry, and every year the categories of awards are reviewed, with new categories added to recognize excellence in diverse fields in accordance with the requirements of changing times. Notable additions include "Best Civic Management of a Tourist Destination" (2010), "Tour Operators Promoting Niche Segments" (2011), and "Best Heritage City" and "Best Heritage Walk" (2012).

Significance and Impact

The awards are presented on the occasion of World Tourism Day (27 September), by eminent dignitaries including the Hon'ble President of India. The date was selected to coincide with World Tourism Day to foster awareness of the importance of tourism and its social, cultural, political, and economic value.

Institutional Framework

The awards are administered by the Ministry of Tourism, with selection made by committees constituted for the purpose. The decision of the Ministry of Tourism is final and binding on all applicants.

The National Tourism Awards serve as a cornerstone of India's efforts to build a world-class tourism industry that drives economic growth, employment, and cultural exchange.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

The National Tourism Awards are open to a wide range of participants from across the tourism ecosystem:

🏛️ State Governments & UTs

State Governments and Union Territories are eligible for awards such as "Best State/UT for Comprehensive Development of Tourism" and "Most Film Promotion Friendly State/UT".

🏨 Hotels & Heritage Hotels

Classified hotels from 1-star to 5-star deluxe categories (one award in each category) and heritage hotels in Basic, Classic, and Grand categories are eligible.

✈️ Travel Agents & Tour Operators

Approved inbound tour operators (categorized by foreign exchange earnings), domestic tour operators promoting various regions, and adventure/MICE tour operators.

🚌 Tourist Transport Operators

Recognized tourist transport operators, categorized by business earnings, with awards based on business transacted through agents, operators, hoteliers, and FITs.

🍽️ Hospitality Professionals

Bed & Breakfast establishments, stand-alone restaurants, convention centres, and chefs are eligible for recognition in the hospitality segment.

👥 Individuals & Organizations

Individuals and private organizations contributing to civic management of destinations, heritage walks, responsible tourism, accessible tourism, and niche segments.

How to Apply

Applications must be made in the proper forms, wherever specified, and submitted to the Ministry of Tourism. Unless otherwise mentioned, the activities undertaken during the specified financial year form the basis for evaluation. The decision of the Ministry of Tourism is final and binding.

What Winners Receive

Recipients are presented trophies and scrolls of honour by eminent dignitaries — including the Hon'ble President of India — at a function organized by the Ministry of Tourism on World Tourism Day (27 September).

⚙️ Selection Process

The selection process is designed to be transparent, merit-based, and rigorous, ensuring that only the most deserving contributors to Indian tourism receive the honour.

1

Category Review

The Ministry of Tourism reviews and updates the award categories every year, adding new categories to recognize excellence in diverse and emerging fields of tourism.

2

Application Submission

Eligible entities submit applications in the proper prescribed forms, covering activities undertaken during the specified financial year (e.g., April to March of the award period).

3

Evidence Verification

Claims such as foreign exchange earnings are verified through statutory auditors' certificates of the service provider, with due weightage given to growth in earnings.

4

Committee Evaluation

The selection of awardees is made by committees constituted for the purpose, which evaluate each entry against the criteria for its category.

5

Ministry Approval

The recommendations are finalized by the Ministry of Tourism, whose decision is final and binding. Winners are announced and felicitated at the awards ceremony.

6

Presentation Ceremony

The awards are presented on World Tourism Day (27 September) by eminent dignitaries, including the Hon'ble President of India, with tourism ministers of states and industry leaders in attendance.

Evaluation Themes

  • Performance: Tangible results in tourism promotion, visitor growth, and revenue generation
  • Service Excellence: Highest standards of service quality and visitor experience
  • Innovation: Creative promotional campaigns and novel tourism products
  • Sustainability: Eco-friendly practices, civic upkeep, and responsible tourism
  • Heritage Conservation: Preservation and enhancement of monuments, heritage cities, and cultural assets
  • Inclusivity: Accessible tourism for people with disabilities and older persons

💡 Interesting Facts

📅

World Tourism Day Timing

The awards are deliberately presented on 27 September — World Tourism Day — to highlight tourism's social, cultural, political, and economic value to the international community.

👑

Presidential Honours

Awards are frequently presented by the Hon'ble President of India, making the ceremony one of the most prestigious industry events in the country.

🔄

Ever-Evolving Categories

New categories are added almost every year — from civic management (2010) and niche segments (2011) to heritage walks and film-friendly states (2012).

💰

Earnings-Based Recognition

Inbound tour operators are categorized by foreign exchange earnings — from Rs. 2.5 crore to Rs. 100 crore and above — with claims verified by statutory auditors.

🏨

Heritage Hotel Grading

Heritage hotels compete in three distinct grades — Basic, Classic, and Grand — celebrating different scales of heritage hospitality.

🎬

Bollywood's Role

Recognizing cinema's power to promote destinations, the "Most Film Promotion Friendly State/UT" award celebrates states that attract film productions.

♿

Accessibility Champion

The "Best Maintained and Disabled-Friendly Monument" award drives the ministry's agenda of barrier-free destinations for people with disabilities and older persons.

🇮🇳

Oldest Winner

Orient Express Travels & Tours, established on 26 September 1947, is India's oldest tourism company and a multiple national tourism award winner.
