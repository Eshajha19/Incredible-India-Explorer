Mountain overview

A high summit of the Garhwal Himalayas

Mana Peak is a 7,272 m mountain in Uttarakhand's Garhwal Himalayas. It is linked with the remote Mana region, high passes, glaciers, and the dramatic northern landscape near the Indo-Tibetan border area.

Geography

The peak belongs to the high Himalayan terrain of Chamoli/Uttarakhand, where steep snow slopes, glacier systems, harsh weather, and long approach valleys shape every expedition plan.

Why it matters

Mana Peak is a useful educational case for understanding Indian Himalayan elevation, borderland geography, and expedition logistics.

Climbing character

It should be treated as a serious mountaineering objective requiring trained teams, permits, guides, and high-altitude safety planning.

Planning context for Mana Peak

Mana Peak is not a casual trek. It is a high-altitude expedition objective where weather, permits, glacier safety, acclimatisation, and professional mountain leadership are crucial.

Access regionUsually planned through the Garhwal/Chamoli high-Himalayan approach corridor.
SkillsSnow craft, glacier travel, rope movement, crevasse safety, and altitude management.
SeasonExpedition windows depend on snow conditions, monsoon, and local weather advisories.
ResponsibilityFollow permits, local rules, waste discipline, and fragile alpine ecology guidelines.

Safety note

This page is educational. Do not use it as a route guide. Always consult authorised local agencies, trained guides, and official weather/permit requirements before any high-altitude plan.
