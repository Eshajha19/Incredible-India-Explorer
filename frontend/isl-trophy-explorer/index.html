League History & Evolution

A Decade of Footballing Renaissance

Launched in October 2014 with eight inaugural franchise teams featuring marquee international superstars (Alessandro Del Piero, Robert Pirès, Nicolas Anelka, Marco Materazzi), the Indian Super League transformed football culture across India.

In 2019, the Asian Football Confederation (AFC) formally designated the ISL as India's official top-tier football league. The competition introduced the prestigious ISL League Winners Shield for regular-season table-toppers alongside the historic ISL Cup Trophy for the playoff champion.

🌏
Continental Pathway: The League Shield winner earns direct qualification to the prestigious AFC Champions League 2 group stage.
