ECOLOGICAL VALUE

Why Indian Wetlands Matter

Wetlands are among the world's most productive environments. Covering over 1.3 million hectares in India, they buffer coastal storm surges, filter toxic river runoff, store vast amounts of blue carbon, and provide livelihoods for millions of fishing and farming communities.
