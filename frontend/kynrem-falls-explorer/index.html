Explore Kynrem Falls

Nestled inside Thangkharang Park near Sohra (Cherrapunji), Meghalaya. Kynrem Falls cascades in three spectacular tiers down East Khasi Hills into the plains of Bangladesh.

Tier 1: Upper Plunge

Height Drop: ~110 Meters

The water originates from rain-fed headwater streams on Sohra plateau, leaping dramatically off steep sandstone cliffs into a mist-shrouded gorge.

Flow Speed: High Velocity Plunge
Visibility: Visible from Thangkharang Viewpoint
Geology: Khasi Group Sandstone & Limestone
Ecology: Subtropical Rain-Forest Canopy

Peak Monsoon Surge

During peak monsoon in Cherrapunji (one of the wettest places on Earth), Kynrem Falls transforms into a roaring, thunderous torrent visible miles away, filling the entire gorge with dense mist and rainbows.

Flow Volume: 100% Maximum Best Viewpoint: Thangkharang Park Gate Travel Tip: Carry heavy rain gear

Nearby Attractions in Sohra Circuit

🌳 Thangkharang Park

State park maintained by Meghalaya Forest Dept, providing panoramic views of Kynrem Falls and Bangladesh plains.

🪨 Khoh Ramhah (Pillar Rock)

A giant cone-shaped rock monolith resembling an inverted Khasi basket (Khoh) with legendary folklore.

🌈 Nohkalikai Falls (340m)

Located ~16 km away, India's tallest single-drop waterfall plunging into a turquoise plunge pool.
