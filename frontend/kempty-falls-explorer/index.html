🏔️ Uttarakhand · Mussoorie

Kempty Falls Explorer

A five-tiered cascade tumbling from the Garhwal Himalayas above Mussoorie, Kempty Falls has drawn travellers to its misty pools since the 1830s. Trace its journey from mountain spring to splash pool below.

📍 Location & Water Source

Where the falls come from

Kempty Falls lies in Ram Gaon, in the Tehri Garhwal district of Uttarakhand, on the Mussoorie–Yamunotri (Chakrata) road about 15 km from Mussoorie's Mall Road and roughly 45 km from Dehradun. A perennial hill stream gathers near the village of Banglow ki Kandi, southwest of the falls, then rushes northwest off the ridge, dropping a total of about 40–50 feet through five cascading steps into a rock pool below.

  • Location: Ram Gaon, Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand
  • Altitude: ~1,364 m (4,500 ft) above sea level
  • Water source: Perennial Garhwal hill stream, tributary of the Yamuna
  • Drop height: ~40–50 ft across five cascades
  • Distance: 15 km from Mussoorie / 45 km from Dehradun
  • History: Developed as a tea-party spot in 1835 by British officer John Mekinan

🌦️ Seasons

The Falls Across the Year

Fed by a perennial stream, Kempty Falls flows year-round, but its mood shifts sharply with the seasons — from a gentle, clear trickle in winter to a churning, flood-prone torrent at the height of the monsoon.

Getting There

Kempty Falls sits right beside the Mussoorie–Yamunotri road, an easy taxi or bus ride from Mussoorie's Library bus stand.

Travel Notes

  • Best season: March–June and September–November
  • Avoid: Peak monsoon (July–August), when flow turns dangerously heavy
  • Access: By road, or via a cable-car ropeway down to the falls' base
  • Nearest railhead: Dehradun (~45 km) · Nearest airport: Jolly Grant, Dehradun (~69 km)
