📜 History of the Award

The National Teachers' Award (earlier known as the National Award to Teachers) was instituted in 1958 to felicitate remarkable teachers and headmasters in their quest to improve the quality of school education and enrich their students' learning experiences. It is one of India's highest honours for the teaching profession.

Genesis and Vision

The award was conceived to raise the prestige of the teaching profession and to give public recognition to the meritorious services of outstanding teachers working in primary, middle, and higher secondary schools across the country. It was first conferred from 1958-59 onwards.

Evolution Over the Years

For decades, the award honoured school teachers and heads of schools, with a fixed number of awards allocated as quotas to States, Union Territories, and educational organizations such as Kendriya Vidyalayas, Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas, CBSE, and CISCE. In 2023, the award was expanded under the umbrella of the National Teachers' Awards to also honour teachers from Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) and Polytechnics.

Significance and Impact

The National Teachers' Award celebrates the unique contribution of some of the finest teachers in the country, projecting them as role models. Through their commitment and industry, award-winning teachers have not only improved the quality of school education but also enriched the lives of their students and communities.

Institutional Framework

The award is administered by the Department of School Education & Literacy under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, and is executed through a transparent, fully online three-stage selection process.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

🏫 Eligible Schools

Teachers and Heads of Schools working in recognized primary, middle, high, and higher secondary schools — including State/UT-run schools, local-body schools, aided schools, private schools, KVs, JNVs, Sainik Schools, AEES, EMRS, CBSE, and CISCE-affiliated schools.

⏱️ Minimum Service

Only regular teachers and Heads of Schools with a minimum of ten years of service are eligible to apply for the award.

🖥️ Self-Nomination

The award is a self-nominating honour. Teachers and head teachers apply directly online through the official web portal of the Ministry of Education before the prescribed cut-off date.

🚫 Not Eligible

Educational administrators, inspectors of education, staff of training institutes, contractual teachers, and Shiksha Mitras are not eligible. Teachers who have indulged in tuitions are also excluded.

🎓 Retired Teachers

Normally retired teachers are not eligible, except those who have served at least four months of the award year (i.e., up to 30th April) and fulfill all other conditions.

🤝 Inclusive Recognition

Special educators and differently-abled teachers working in regular schools, as well as teachers doing outstanding work for inclusive education, are eligible for special consideration.

🎯 Selection Process

The selection of awardees is a rigorous, transparent, and fully online three-stage process — District, State, and National — based on an evaluation matrix that gives 90% weightage to performance and 10% to objective criteria.

1

Online Self-Nomination

Teachers register and submit self-nominations through the Ministry of Education's web portal, along with a portfolio containing documents, activity reports, photographs, and videos.

2

District Selection Committee

The District Selection Committee, headed by the District Education Officer, verifies facts, evaluates applications as per the evaluation matrix, and shortlists up to 3 candidates per district.

3

State / Organization Committee

The State Selection Committee (headed by the Principal Secretary of Education) and Organization Selection Committees re-verify and shortlist the best candidates within each State/UT quota.

4

Independent National Jury

A five-member Independent Jury of education experts reviews application letters and conducts video-conference interactions with shortlisted teachers for qualitative assessment.

5

Final Approval

The Jury recommends the final list — up to 50 candidates — to the Hon'ble Union Education Minister for acceptance and final approval.

6

Presentation on Teachers' Day

Selected teachers are conferred the award on 5 September, Teachers' Day, at a prestigious ceremony in New Delhi.

Evaluation Matrix (100 Marks)

  • Performance Criteria (90 marks): Innovative pedagogy and joyful learning, use of technology, learning material creation and mentorship, community engagement, school governance, and delivery of foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN)
  • Objective Criteria (10 marks): Publications, books, and research articles in journals with ISSN/ISBN in the last five years
  • An application letter (max 1500 words) narrating how the teacher fulfilled the award criteria, including outcomes for students
  • Vigilance clearance and physical verification of facts by District/Regional committees

🏷️ Award Categories

The National Teachers' Awards honour outstanding educators across different levels of the education system, with categories that have expanded over time.

🏫

School Teachers

The original category honouring teachers and Heads of Schools working in primary, middle, and higher secondary schools across State, Central, and private school systems.

  • Primary & Middle School Teachers
  • Secondary & Higher Secondary Teachers
  • Heads of Schools
  • Special Educators
🎓

Higher Education Teachers

Introduced in 2023, this category recognises outstanding faculty from Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) — universities and colleges — for their contribution to teaching, research, and mentoring.

  • University Teachers
  • College Teachers
  • Research Mentors
  • Innovative Pedagogues
🛠️

Polytechnic & Technical Teachers

Also introduced in 2023, this category honours teachers in polytechnics and technical institutions who skill India's workforce through vocational and applied learning.

  • Polytechnic Lecturers
  • Vocational Trainers
  • Skill Development Faculty
  • Industry-Integrated Instructors
🕊️

Special & Reserved Categories

Special consideration is given to differently-abled teachers and educators championing inclusive education, alongside reserved slots for Sanskrit, Persian, and Arabic teachers.

  • Differently-Abled Teachers
  • Inclusive Education Champions
  • Sanskrit / Persian / Arabic Teachers
  • Rural & Remote-Area Educators

🎖️ Award Ceremony

The National Teachers' Awards are presented on 5 September — Teachers' Day — the birth anniversary of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, philosopher, educationist, and India's second President.

🏛️

Venue: Vigyan Bhawan

The annual ceremony is held at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi, attended by the highest dignitaries of the country.

👑

Presented by the President

The awards are conferred by the President of India (or the Vice President), underscoring the national importance of teachers.

🏅

The Award

Each awardee receives a certificate of merit, a cash award of ₹50,000, and a silver medal.

🗣️

Interaction with the PM

Awardees also get the unique opportunity to interact with the Hon'ble Prime Minister, sharing their experiences and ideas for improving education.

🎊

National Celebration

The ceremony forms part of nationwide Teachers' Day celebrations that honour the role of educators in shaping the nation's future.

🌟

Recognition & Prestige

Receiving the National Teachers' Award elevates teachers as role models in their districts and states, inspiring peers across the country.

Recent Celebrations

On 5 September 2024, 82 selected teachers were conferred the National Teachers' Award by President Droupadi Murmu at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi, with awardees getting the opportunity to interact with the Prime Minister.

⏳ Timeline & Milestones

1958

Award Instituted

The National Award to Teachers is instituted to raise the prestige of the teaching profession and publicly recognise outstanding school teachers.

1959

First Awards Conferred

The first batch of meritorious teachers receives the award for distinguished service in primary, middle, and secondary schools.

1962

Teachers' Day Tradition

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan becomes President of India. His birthday, 5 September, is observed as Teachers' Day, later becoming the day of the award ceremony.

2015

Recognition Beyond Schools

Parallel recognition schemes emerge for higher education teachers, widening the scope of teacher honouring in the country.

2023

National Teachers' Awards Expanded

Two new categories are instituted under the umbrella of the National Teachers' Awards — for Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) and Polytechnics — alongside the school teachers' award.

2024

82 Teachers Honoured

President Droupadi Murmu confers the National Teachers' Award on 82 teachers at Vigyan Bhawan, with awardees interacting with the Prime Minister.

Today

A Transparent Online Process

The award now runs through a fully digital three-stage selection — District, State, and National — inviting online self-nominations from teachers across India.

✨ Interesting Facts

🎂 Born from a Birthday

When Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan became President, his students requested to celebrate his birthday. He suggested his birthday instead be observed as Teachers' Day — 5 September — to honour all teachers.

🏅 One of India's Highest Teacher Honours

The National Teachers' Award is among the most prestigious recognitions a teacher can receive in India, presented by the President of India himself.

💸 Prize and Medallion

Each awardee receives a certificate of merit, ₹50,000 in cash, and a silver medal — a fitting tribute to years of dedicated service.

🕌 Reserved for Classical Languages

Historically, a portion of the awards (about 20) has been reserved for teachers of Sanskrit, Persian, and Arabic, protecting India's classical language traditions.

📱 100% Online Since Revamp

Since the modernisation of the scheme, the award is entirely self-nomination-based, with applications submitted through the Ministry of Education's online portal.

⚖️ 90:10 Evaluation Matrix

Teachers are scored on 90 marks of performance (pedagogy, technology, community engagement, governance) and only 10 marks of objective criteria such as publications.

🗣️ A Meeting with the PM

Awardees enjoy the rare honour of interacting with the Prime Minister after the ceremony, discussing education reform and innovation.

🎓 Teaching is Nation-Building

Over the decades, the award has celebrated thousands of teachers from every corner of India — a reminder that the country's future is shaped one classroom at a time.
