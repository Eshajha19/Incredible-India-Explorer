Mountain Overview

Geography & Mountaineering Legacy

A majestic high-Himalayan copper-hued rock and ice pyramid standing on the northern borders of Uttarakhand.

🏔️ Geography & Glaciers

Located in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand near the Indo-Tibetan border, Mount Kamet is flanked by three major glaciers: East Kamet Glacier, West Kamet Glacier, and Raikana Glacier, which feed the Dhauliganga and Saraswati river systems.

📜 Historic 1931 Achievement

In 1931, British mountaineer Frank Smythe along with Eric Shipton, R.L. Holdsworth, and Lewa Sherpa reached Kamet's summit. It stood as the highest summitted peak in human history until Nanda Devi was scaled in 1936.

🧗 Subsidiary Peaks & Ridge

Kamet forms a high-altitude massif with subsidiary summits including Abi Gamin (7,355 m), Mukut Parbat (7,242 m), and Mana Peak (7,272 m), linked together by high mountain cols.

⚠️ High-Altitude Expedition Safety Notice

Mount Kamet is a serious, technical 7,000+ meter peak requiring Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) permits, trained high-altitude guides, advanced ice-craft, crevasse rescue skills, and weather monitoring. This page is strictly for educational exploration.
