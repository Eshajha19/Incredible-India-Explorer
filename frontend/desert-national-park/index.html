Park overview

A protected desert classroom in the Thar

Desert National Park showcases a unique arid ecosystem where dunes, scrub, grasses, reptiles, raptors, mammals and the Great Indian Bustard survive through specialised desert adaptations.

Thar Desert ecosystem

The landscape includes sand dunes, rocky desert patches, arid scrub and grassland mosaics. These habitats support hardy plants, reptiles, mammals, raptors and threatened desert birds.

Great Indian Bustard

The Great Indian Bustard is the flagship species for the explorer and a powerful reason to understand desert-grassland conservation.

Climate adaptations

Heat, low rainfall and scarce water make desert survival dependent on timing, shelter, movement and water-saving strategies.
