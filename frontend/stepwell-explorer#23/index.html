India's Subterranean Marvels

Stepwells (Baolis in the North, Vavs in the West) are not just water sources; they are intricate, multi-story architectural masterpieces designed for water conservation, social gathering, and spiritual retreat.

Anatomy of a Stepwell

Pavilions (Mandapas)

Ornate, pillared halls on the upper levels that provided shade and served as community gathering spaces.

Staircases (Somasar)

Steep, zigzagging, or straight flights of stairs leading down to the water table, adjusting to seasonal fluctuations.

Water Reservoir (Kund)

The deepest point, often octagonal or circular, designed to minimize evaporation and maximize water storage.

Sculptural Detail

Intricate carvings of deities, mythological scenes, and geometric patterns adorning the walls and pillars.
