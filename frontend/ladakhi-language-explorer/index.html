Writing Tradition

Tibetan Scripts in Ladakh

Explore the dual writing tradition of Ladakh—from sacred Uchen block printing in monasteries to flowing Yuged cursive calligraphy.

📜

Uchen Script (དབུ་ཅན། - Headed Block Script)

Meaning 'with a head', Uchen is the block calligraphy style standard across Buddhist scriptures (Kangyur & Tengyur), monastery frescoes, school education, and public road signs across Ladakh.

✒️

Yuged / Ume Script (དབུ་མེད། - Cursive Script)

Meaning 'headless', Yuged is the flowing cursive handwriting tradition used by historic Ladakhi scribes, local administrators, monastic ledgers, and personal correspondence.
