Nuranang Falls Explorer

Also known as Bong Bong Falls, this breathtaking ~100-metre drop forms a spectacular part of the Himalayan environment. Discover its journey from high-altitude streams to the valley below.

High-Altitude Origins

The journey begins near the towering Sela Pass at an altitude of over 13,000 feet. Here, melting snow and glacial runoff from the Eastern Himalayas gather to form pristine mountain streams. The extreme altitude and freezing temperatures keep this source pure and cold year-round.

The Nuranang River

As the streams descend from Sela Pass, they converge into the Nuranang River. Flowing through steep valleys lined with dense alpine forests and rhododendrons, the river gains momentum before merging eventually with the larger Tawang Chu river down in the valley.

The Spectacular Drop

Before meeting the Tawang Chu, the Nuranang River plunges dramatically over a steep cliff, creating the majestic Nuranang Falls. The nearly 100-metre drop scatters a fine mist across the surrounding greenery, making it one of the most picturesque locations in Arunachal Pradesh.

📍 Location & Scale

A hidden gem of the Northeast

Located in the Tawang district of Arunachal Pradesh, Nuranang Falls is approximately 2 km from Jang town. Its sheer height and raw volume make it a prominent feature of the local landscape.

  • Height: ~100 meters (approx. 328 feet)
  • Source: Northern slopes of Sela Pass
  • River System: Nuranang River → Tawang Chu
  • Best time to visit: June to November
  • Nearest Town: Jang (2 km) / Tawang (40 km)

🌦️ Seasons

The Waterfall Across the Year

Nuranang Falls changes character dramatically depending on the season, driven by Himalayan snowmelt and monsoon rains.

Monsoon (Jul-Sep)

Roaring Power

Heavy rainfall and active snowmelt turn the falls into a furious, roaring torrent. The spray can be felt from far away, and the surrounding vegetation is brilliantly lush.

Winter (Dec-Feb)

Pristine Calm

With much of the high-altitude water freezing, the flow reduces significantly. The water runs crystal clear over the rocks, sometimes fringed with ice, offering a serene, quiet beauty.

🗺️ Map

Getting There

Nuranang Falls sits conveniently close to the road connecting Bomdila and Tawang. It makes for an ideal stopover when travelling through the Sela Pass.

Travel Notes

  • Permits: An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is required to enter Arunachal Pradesh.
  • Accessibility: Located just off the main road, involving a short hike down to the base of the falls.
  • Facilities: Limited. A small local canteen usually operates near the viewing point.
