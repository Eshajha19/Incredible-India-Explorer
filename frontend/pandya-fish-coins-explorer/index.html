Historical Context

The Pandya Dynasty

The Pandyas were one of the three ancient Tamil dynasties (Muvendar). Their kingdom traditionally encompassed the southern regions of Tamil Nadu, centered around Madurai and Korkai.

The Fish Emblem (Meen)

The fish was the royal crest of the Pandyas. It symbolized prosperity, maritime power, and authority. It appeared on their flags, seals, and predominantly on their coinage.
