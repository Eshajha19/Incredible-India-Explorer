Park overview

Maharashtra's iconic tiger reserve landscape

Tadoba-Andhari National Park combines dry deciduous forest, lakes, river belts, bamboo, meadows, safari zones, birds, reptiles, herbivores, and predators into one of central India's most exciting wildlife classrooms.

Tiger reserve

The park is known for tiger sightings and tiger conservation. Its habitats support prey animals, water-dependent wildlife, forest birds, and predators across core and buffer landscapes.

Geography

Forests, lakes, the Andhari landscape, dry-season water sources, and safari gates create a diverse visitor and wildlife map.

History

The reserve's identity is tied to Maharashtra's early protected-area story and its evolution into a major tiger reserve destination.
