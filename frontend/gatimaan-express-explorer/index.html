About Gatimaan Express

Introduced as India's first semi-high-speed train, Gatimaan Express heralded a new era of rail travel in the country. Initially designed to connect Hazrat Nizamuddin in Delhi to Agra Cantonment, it offered tourists a significantly faster and more luxurious way to visit the Taj Mahal.

With a top operational speed of 160 km/h (99 mph), it stands as one of the fastest trains in the Indian Railways network. The train is completely air-conditioned and features LHB coaches, known for their safety and comfort.
