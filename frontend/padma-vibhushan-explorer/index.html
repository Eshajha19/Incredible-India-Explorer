Padma Vibhushan Explorer

Awarded for exceptional and distinguished service in any field, including service rendered by government servants.

Year Instituted: 1954 Symbol: Lotus Emblem (Padma) Announcement: Republic Day Eve

History & Origin

Established in 1954

The Padma Awards were instituted on 2 January 1954 by the President of India. Originally classified into three classes—'Pahla Varg' (First Class), 'Dusra Varg', and 'Tisra Varg'—the name was formally changed to Padma Vibhushan by presidential order on 8 January 1955.

National Stature

Positioned second only to the Bharat Ratna, the Padma Vibhushan honors exceptional luminaries whose service produces long-lasting national impact in arts, public life, science, industry, and civil leadership.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Universal Scope: Open to all individuals without distinction of race, occupation, position, or sex.
  • Exceptional Standard: Awarded for "exceptional and distinguished service" in any field of human endeavor.
  • PSU Exclusion Rule: Government servants including those working in Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) are not eligible, except doctors and scientists.
  • Posthumous Recognition: Granted posthumously in rare cases of supreme extraordinary achievement.

Medal Design & Insignia

🪷

Lotus Motif

The decoration is a circular bronze badge with a geometric square pattern. At its center is an embossed lotus flower with white gold gilding, symbolising purity and cultural heritage.

✍️

Devanagari Inscription

The word "Padma" is embossed above and "Vibhushan" below the central lotus in Devanagari script. The reverse side features the State Emblem of India and national motto.

Selection & Nomination Process

Nominations for Padma Awards open annually on May 1st via the Rashtriya Puraskar Portal. The Padma Awards Committee is constituted annually by the Prime Minister and chaired by the Cabinet Secretary.

1

Public Nominations

Self-nominations and recommendations submitted online via portal.

2

Committee Scrutiny

High-level committee evaluates nominations and verifies background.

3

Announcements & Investiture

Names announced on Republic Day eve; conferred at Rashtrapati Bhavan in spring.

Fields of Recognition

Art & Culture

Music, painting, classical dance, cinema, theatre, and traditional handicrafts.

Public Affairs & Governance

Statesmanship, parliamentary excellence, diplomacy, and law.

Science & Engineering

Space research, nuclear energy, agricultural sciences, and technology innovation.

Literature & Education

Authors, poets, educationists, journalists, and language scholars.

Social Work & Medicine

Grassroots social upliftment, healthcare pioneers, and philanthropic endeavors.

Trade & Industry

Industrial development, entrepreneurship, and economic leadership.
