Geography, Glaciers & Spirituality

The Sacred Abode of Shiva

Kedarnath Peak, standing at 6,940 metres, is a prominent mountain in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand. It is part of the same massif that houses the famous Kedarnath Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and a key destination in the Char Dham Yatra. The peak looms dramatically over the temple complex, creating one of the most awe-inspiring sacred landscapes in India.

The mountain is fed by the Chorabari Glacier, which gives rise to the Mandakini River — a tributary of the Ganges. The entire region is steeped in mythology, with legends dating back to the Mahabharata. According to tradition, the Pandavas sought the blessings of Lord Shiva at this very site after the great war, and Shiva appeared as a bull, with parts of his body emerging at different locations across the Panch Kedar.

The peak was first climbed in the early 20th century by European expeditions, and today it attracts both pilgrims and mountaineers. While the temple area is accessible via a popular trek, the high summit requires technical mountaineering skills and proper acclimatisation.

Technical Specifications

  • Elevation: 6,940 m (22,769 ft)
  • Prominence: 1,200 m (3,937 ft)
  • Coordinates: 30°47′42″N 79°04′10″E
  • Climbing Difficulty: Grade V (Hard)
  • First Ascent: 1932
  • Team: Swiss Expedition led by G. O. Dyhrenfurth
  • Glacier: Chorabari Glacier
  • River Source: Mandakini River

Trekking Information

Explore the trails and routes that lead to the base of this majestic peak.

🗺️

Kedarnath Temple Trek

The most famous pilgrimage trek in Uttarakhand, leading to the Kedarnath Temple at 3,583 m. Passes through scenic meadows, river crossings, and dramatic mountain landscapes.

📏 16 km One Way ⬆️ 3,583 m ⚡ Moderate
🌲

Vasuki Tal Trek

A stunning high-altitude lake trek starting from Kedarnath. Vasuki Tal at 4,135 m offers mesmerising views of Kedarnath Peak, Chaukhamba, and the surrounding glaciers.

📏 8 km Round Trip ⬆️ 4,135 m ⚡ Moderate
🦅

Panch Kedar Trek

A spiritual circuit covering the five sacred Shiva temples — Kedarnath, Tungnath, Rudranath, Madhyamaheshwar, and Kalpeshwar. Offers a unique blend of faith and adventure.

📏 10-12 Days ⬆️ 3,690 m ⚡ Challenging
🌸

Best Season & Permits

Best seasons: May-June (pre-monsoon) and September-October (post-monsoon). The temple opens from late April to November. Permits not required for the temple trek, but a guide is recommended for summit climbs.

🗓️ May-Jun & Sep-Oct 📋 No Permit Needed 👥 Guide Recommended

Nearby Attractions

Discover remarkable destinations in the vicinity of Kedarnath.

🏛️

Kedarnath Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas and most sacred Shiva temples in India. Located at 3,583 m, this ancient stone temple has survived centuries of harsh Himalayan weather.

🌿

Vasuki Tal

A serene high-altitude lake at 4,135 m, named after the serpent king Vasuki. Surrounded by towering peaks and glaciers, it is a popular trekking destination.

🏔️

Chaukhamba Massif

A magnificent mountain massif with four peaks, visible from the Kedarnath region. The highest peak, Chaukhamba I, rises to 7,138 m.

🌸

Gandhi Sarovar

A glacial lake at 4,020 m near Kedarnath, also known as Chorabari Tal. It is the source of the Mandakini River and offers pristine natural beauty.

🏙️

Rudraprayag

The confluence town where the Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers meet. A key stop on the journey to Kedarnath, known for its scenic setting and temples.

⛰️

Madhyamaheshwar

One of the Panch Kedar temples, located at 3,490 m. The trek passes through lush meadows and offers spectacular views of the Garhwal peaks.
