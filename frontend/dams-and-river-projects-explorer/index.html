The Idea

What a River Valley Project Is

Not a dam — a system. Storage, canals, powerhouse, command area and the institution that operates all of it.

A multipurpose river valley project is designed to do several things at once from a single storage: irrigate a command area, generate power, moderate floods, supply drinking and industrial water, and in some cases support navigation and fisheries. The dam is the visible part; the canal network, the powerhouse and the operating authority are usually the larger investment.

India took this up at scale immediately after independence. The Damodar Valley Corporation was created in 1948, modelled directly on the Tennessee Valley Authority, and Bhakra, Hirakud and Nagarjuna Sagar followed within two decades. The results are real and are not in serious dispute: the Bhakra command underwrote the Green Revolution in Punjab and Haryana, and the Damodar dams took a river that was called the sorrow of Bengal and made its floods survivable.

The costs are equally real. Hirakud displaced somewhere around 150,000 people and never finished resettling them. Sardar Sarovar produced the longest-running social movement in independent India and a World Bank withdrawal. Reservoirs silt up, deltas starve of sediment, migratory fish stop migrating, and on interstate rivers the height of a dam becomes a matter for the courts.

  • Storage, canals, powerhouse and command area — the dam is one component
  • Interstate rivers mean storage upstream is water not delivered downstream
  • Every reservoir is filling with silt from the day it closes
  • Most of India's large dams are now over forty years old

On the Numbers

Heights, storage volumes and capacities here are published project figures and are approximate. "Height" may be measured above the deepest foundation or above the river bed, which is why sources disagree. Installed capacity changes as units are added or derated. Displacement figures are contested — they vary by an order of magnitude depending on who counts and what counts as affected — and are given here as ranges with that disagreement stated.
