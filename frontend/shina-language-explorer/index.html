Shina Language Explorer

شینا • Ṣiṇā • शीना (Dardic Ṣiṇyā)

Explore Shina—an ancient Dardic language spoken in Drass (the 'Gateway to Ladakh' and second coldest inhabited place in the world) and Gurez Valley. Renowned for its distinctive pitch-accent tonal system, archaic Vedic phonological roots, mountain horse polo, and rich winter oral epics.
