1897 • Bombay (Lalbaug & Vikhroli)

Brand Origin & Swadeshi Pioneer

Born out of self-reliance and engineering genius, Ardeshir and Pirojsha Godrej transformed Indian manufacturing at the height of the Swadeshi movement.

The Founder
🔐

Ardeshir Godrej's Lock Breakthrough

In 1897, lawyer-turned-inventor Ardeshir Godrej gave up practicing law after refusing to compromise on ethics during a court case in Zanzibar. Noticing rising burglary rates in Bombay, he patented India's first unpickable lever lock in a tiny shed in Lalbaug, establishing indigenous master craftsmanship.

World First (1918)
🧼

Vatni: 100% Vegetable Soap

Before 1918, all commercial soaps globally relied on animal tallow. Ardeshir developed the world's first soap made entirely from vegetable oils. Named Vatni (short for Vande Mataram), it won the endorsement of Rabindranath Tagore and Mahatma Gandhi for pure Swadeshi living.

The Industrialist
🏭

Pirojsha Godrej & Vikhroli Model

Ardeshir's brother, Pirojsha Godrej, built the massive industrial township of Pirojshanagar in Vikhroli. He introduced fire-proof safes, steel furniture, housing for factory workers, schools, and mangrove conservation, setting global benchmarks in conscious industrialization.

1944 Bombay Explosion
💥

The Safes That Survived Fire

During the catastrophic 1944 Bombay Docks freighter explosion that flattened entire districts, financial records, gold bullion, and currency stored inside Godrej Fire-Proof Safes remained completely undamaged, cementing Godrej as the ultimate symbol of trust.

"I know of no honest reason why Godrej's soaps should not replace foreign soaps. I have used them and found them to be of excellent quality."
— Rabindranath Tagore (1922)
