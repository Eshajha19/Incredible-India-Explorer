ಕನ್ನಡ Kannada Language of the Deccan and one of India's great classical traditions

Kannada is a major Dravidian language of South India, spoken across Karnataka and neighboring regions, known for its ancient script, steady literary tradition, and strong link to temple culture, music, and public life.
