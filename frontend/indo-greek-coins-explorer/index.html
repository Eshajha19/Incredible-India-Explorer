Menander I Soter (King Milinda)

Reign: c. 165 – 130 BCE · Capital: Sagala (Sialkot)

The most famous Indo-Greek king, celebrated in the Buddhist text Milindapanha (Questions of King Milinda). His coins circulated across Punjab and Taxila.

Denomination: Silver Silver Drachm
Metal & Weight: Silver (2.45 grams)
Minting Region: Gandhara / Taxila
Cultural Connections: Greco-Buddhist Patronage

Territory of Menander I (c. 165–130 BCE)

Extended from Bactria across modern-day Afghanistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab, and reach as far east as Mathura and Pataliputra according to the Yuga Purana.

Major Mints: Taxila, Pushkalavati (Charsadda), Sagala (Sialkot), Alexandria in the Caucasus (Bagram)
