Humsafar Express

The Modern Long-Distance Sleeper Train of India

Discover More

Introduction

Humsafar Express is a premium, all-AC 3-Tier sleeper train service operated by Indian Railways. Designed for overnight and long-distance journeys, it offers enhanced passenger comfort, modern amenities, and advanced safety features. It represents a significant upgrade over traditional sleeper coaches, providing an affordable yet comfortable travel experience for the common man.

History & Development

2016

Announcement

The concept of Humsafar Express was announced in the Railway Budget 2016-17 by the then Railway Minister, Suresh Prabhu, aimed at upgrading the travel experience of long-distance passengers.

Dec 2016

Inaugural Run

The first Humsafar Express ran between Gorakhpur and Anand Vihar Terminal, marking a new era of comfortable overnight travel.

2017-Present

Expansion

The network of Humsafar trains expanded significantly, connecting major cities and remote corners of the country with over 35 active routes.

Train Design

LHB Coaches

Built on the Linke Hofmann Busch (LHB) platform, providing better safety, higher speed capacity, and anti-climbing features during derailments.

Vibrant Exterior

Distinctive vinyl coating on the exterior with a beautiful color scheme of blue and orange, giving it a modern and recognizable look.

Improved Interiors

Fire-retardant curtains, plush upholstery, and well-designed berths that improve overall passenger comfort.

Interesting Facts

  • Fully Air-Conditioned: It is typically comprised entirely of AC 3-Tier coaches, though some services now include Sleeper class.
  • CCTV Surveillance: It was one of the first train services to incorporate extensive CCTV cameras in common areas.
  • GPS Passenger Info: Coaches feature a GPS-based passenger information display system indicating upcoming stations and train speed.
  • Braille Displays: Signages include Braille to aid visually impaired passengers.
