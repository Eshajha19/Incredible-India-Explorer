Corbyn's Cove Beach

Port Blair, Andaman & Nicobar Islands

The closest and most accessible beach to Port Blair. Framed by a sweeping, crescent-shaped shoreline lined with towering coconut palm trees, it is a highly popular destination for a quick coastal escape.

🌐 Destination Profile & Travel Information

  • Location: Around 8 kilometres south-east of Port Blair's city centre, South Andaman District.
  • Landscape: A small, picturesque curve of soft, light-grey sand bounded by dynamic blue waters. The beach is flanked on both sides by low, rocky limestone cliffs and features a well-developed promenade walkway lined with seating areas and food stalls.
  • Transit Options: Highly accessible via a scenic coastal road. Visitors can easily hire an auto-rickshaw, local taxi, or rent a two-wheeler from Port Blair city centre (a smooth 15 to 20-minute drive).

🏄 Major Attractions & Coastal Activities

  • Water Sports Hub: A primary hub for high-energy water sports, including jet-ski rides, speed boat cruises, and banana boat tours.
  • Seawall Promenade: A paved pedestrian path runs alongside the palm grove, offering a popular spot for evening walks, street food tasting, and photography.
  • Safe Wading Zones: The central stretch of the cove has a very gentle underwater gradient, making it an excellent location for casual wading and beach lounging.

🗺️ Nearby Places Worth Visiting

  • Cellular Jail National Memorial: Located just 7 kilometres away, this historic colonial prison archive is a mandatory stop for understanding India's freedom struggle.
  • Japanese Bunkers: Positioned right at the entrance approach of Corbyn’s Cove Beach, these historic pillboxes are relics of the World War II occupation era.
  • Snake Island: Situated just off the coast facing Corbyn’s Cove, this small, rocky, uninhabited islet can be approached via local speedboats and is known for its marine rock formations.
