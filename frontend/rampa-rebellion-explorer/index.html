⛰️ Tribal Resistance of Eastern Ghats

Rampa Rebellion of 1922–24

The heroic Adivasi uprising against British colonial forest laws (Madras Forest Act 1882), forced labor (Vetti), and administrative exploitation in Agency areas, led by Manyam Veerudu Alluri Sitarama Raju.

Root Causes

Tribal Society, Forest Laws & Grievances

🪵

Forest Dependence & Podu Cultivation

Adivasi communities of the Agency area relied on traditional shifting agriculture (Podu) and non-timber forest produce for sustenance for centuries.

📜

Colonial Forest Regulations

The Madras Forest Act of 1882 restricted tribal access to forests, declared traditional forest land as state property, and banned Podu cultivation.

⛓️

Forced Labour (Vetti) & Exploitation

Colonial Tahsildars (notably Bastian) forced Adivasis into uncompensated road construction (Vetti) while Muttadars (estate holders) squeezed exorbitant rents.

Leader Profile

Alluri Sitarama Raju — Manyam Veerudu

Alluri Sitarama Raju (1897–1924)

Title: Manyam Veerudu (Hero of the Jungles)

Origins: Born in Mogallu, Andhra Pradesh. Deeply spiritual, studied astrology, medicine, and martial skills before moving into tribal Agency areas in 1918.

Leadership: Unified Koya and Konda Dora tribes, trained them in traditional and modern guerrilla warfare, and led daring police station raids for arms and ammunition.

"We are not fighting for personal gain, but for the inherent right of the tribal people to live freely in their ancestral hills."

Aftermath & Historical Impact

Counter-Insurgency, Capture & Long-Term Legacy

British Counter-Insurgency & Capture

The British deployed the Assam Rifles, Malabar Special Police, and spent over Rs. 15 lakh to suppress the rebellion. Alluri Sitarama Raju was trapped in Koyyuru jungle on May 7, 1924, tied to a tree, and shot without trial.

Consequences & Long-Term Legacy

The rebellion forced administrative reforms in tribal forest policy. Alluri became a immortal folk hero in Andhra Pradesh, commemorated with statues, postal stamps, and film adaptations.
