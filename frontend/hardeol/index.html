Geography, History & Significance

The Sacred Sentinel

Hardeol is a major peak of the Kumaon Himalayas, situated at the northern end of the Milam valley in the Pithoragarh district of Uttarakhand. The name 'Hardeol' translates to 'Temple of God', reflecting its sacred stature among local communities.

The mountain stands at the northeast corner of the Nanda Devi Sanctuary ring, making it a prominent landmark visible from several trekking routes in the region.

Hardeol remained unclimbed for decades due to its remote location and challenging terrain. It was finally summited in 1978 by an ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police) expedition. Only one other ascent has been recorded — by a BSF expedition in 1991 — making it one of the less frequently climbed 7,000 m peaks in India.

Technical Specifications

  • Elevation: 7,151 m (23,461 ft)
  • Prominence: ~1,691 m
  • Coordinates: 30°23′00″N 80°08′00″E
  • Climbing Difficulty: Hard
  • First Ascent: 1978
  • Expedition Team: ITBP Expedition
Trekking Guide

How to Reach & Trek Route

Hardeol is a serious mountaineering expedition, not a casual trek. Here's what you need to know.

🚗

Getting There

The journey begins at Kathgodam, the nearest major railway head. From there, travel to Munsiyari (approximately 200 km) by road. Munsiyari serves as the base town for expeditions into the Milam valley.

🏕️

Base Camp

The approach trek follows the Milam valley northward. The base camp is typically established near the Milam glacier area. The trek from Munsiyari to base camp takes approximately 6-8 days through remote Himalayan terrain.

🧊

Climbing Route

The standard route involves glacier travel, ice walls, and steep snow slopes. Technical climbing skills are essential. The summit push typically requires fixed ropes and high-altitude camp establishment above 6,000 m.

📅

Best Season

The ideal climbing window is from May to October, with September being the most favorable month. Pre-monsoon (May-June) and post-monsoon (September-October) offer the most stable weather conditions.
