Hindustani vs Carnatic Music

Understand the history, ragas, talas, gharanas, instruments, and performance traditions of India’s two classical music systems.

Quick Comparison

Feature Hindustani Carnatic
Region North India South India
Primary Focus Vocal and Instrumental improvisation Vocal-centric, based on compositions
Main Instruments Sitar, Sarod, Tabla Veena, Violin, Mridangam
Improvisation Extensive, unmetered (Alap) Structured, highly mathematical
Schools Gharanas (Gwalior, Kirana, etc.) Sampradayas (lineages)
