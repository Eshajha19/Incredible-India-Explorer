Geography, Glaciers & History

The Sacred Five Treasures

Kanchenjunga, standing at 8,586 meters, is the third-highest mountain in the world and the tallest peak in India. Located on the border between Nepal's Taplejung District and India's state of Sikkim, the mountain is part of the greater Himalayan range and the Kangchenjunga massif.

The name "Kanchenjunga" derives from the Tibetan words "Kangchen" meaning "great snow" and "junga" meaning "five treasures." This refers to the mountain's five summits, which represent the five repositories of God: gold, silver, gems, grain, and holy books. The people of Sikkim regard the mountain as sacred, and climbing to the summit is considered a sign of disrespect.

In 1955, a British expedition led by Charles Evans made the first successful ascent on May 25, with Joe Brown and George Band reaching the summit. They honored local sentiments by stopping just short of the actual summit, a tradition that continues today.

Trekking Information

Explore the trails and routes that lead to the base of this majestic peak.

🗺️

Kanchenjunga Base Camp Trek

The most popular trekking route, offering breathtaking views of the mountain's five peaks. Passes through lush rhododendron forests, alpine meadows, and high-altitude terrain.

📏 22-25 Days ⬆️ 4,800 m ⚡ Challenging
🌲

North Base Camp (Pangpema)

The Nepal side base camp at Pangpema offers the most iconic views of the mountain's north face. Less crowded with pristine forests.

📏 18-22 Days ⬆️ 5,143 m ⚡ Challenging
🦅

South Base Camp (Oktang)

The Oktang viewpoint provides stunning close-up views of the Yalung Face. Passes through the Conservation Area with rare wildlife.

📏 15-20 Days ⬆️ 4,730 m ⚡ Moderate
🌸

Best Season & Permits

Best seasons: March-May (spring rhododendrons) and October-November (clear skies). Special permits required from Indian and Nepali authorities.

🗓️ Mar-May & Oct-Nov 📋 Permit Required 👥 Guide Mandatory

Nearby Attractions

Discover remarkable destinations in the vicinity of Kanchenjunga.

🏛️

Rumtek Monastery

One of the most important monasteries in Sikkim, near Gangtok. It serves as the seat of the Karmapa and houses precious Buddhist relics.

🌿

Kanchenjunga Conservation Area

A protected area spanning 2,035 sq km, home to snow leopards, red pandas, and Himalayan black bears.

🏔️

Goecha La Pass

A high mountain pass at 4,940 m offering spectacular panoramic views of Kanchenjunga and other Himalayan peaks.

🌸

Yuksom

The historic first capital of Sikkim, gateway to the Kanchenjunga Base Camp trek. Surrounded by dense forests and monasteries.

🏙️

Gangtok

The vibrant capital of Sikkim at 1,650 m. Known for monasteries, bustling markets, and stunning Kanchenjunga views.

⛰️

Khangchendzonga National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site with diverse ecosystems from tropical forests to glaciers, named after the mountain itself.
