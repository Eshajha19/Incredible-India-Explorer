Duronto Express

India's Pioneer Non-Stop Long-Distance Train Concept

Discover More

Introduction

Duronto Express is a category of long-distance, non-stop source-to-destination trains run by Indian Railways. The word "Duronto" means "restless" in Bengali. These trains were introduced to connect major metropolitan cities and state capitals with high-speed, point-to-point services, minimizing travel time by bypassing commercial halts.

History & Development

2009

Announcement

The concept was introduced by the then Railway Minister, Mamata Banerjee, in the Indian Railway Budget of 2009-2010.

Sept 2009

First Run

The first Duronto Express ran between Sealdah (Kolkata) and New Delhi on September 18, 2009, setting a new benchmark for non-stop travel.

2016 Onwards

Commercial Halts Added

To improve occupancy and revenue, commercial halts were introduced for many Duronto trains, shifting them away from their strict "non-stop" origins.

Passenger Facilities

Coach Classes

Duronto trains typically offer 1A, 2A, 3A, and Sleeper classes, with a few fully air-conditioned configurations.

Catering

Onboard catering is provided. The cost is optionally included in the ticket, ensuring hot meals during the journey.

Sleeping Arrangements

All passengers get designated sleeping berths. LHB coaches ensure a smoother and quieter sleep experience.

Accessibility

Modern Duronto trains often feature specialized coaches for differently-abled passengers, including wider doors.

Interesting Facts

  • Unique Livery: Duronto trains feature a distinct yellowish-green livery resembling a splash of colors, setting them apart visually.
  • Speed: Originally, Duronto trains were among the fastest trains in India, with speed limits of up to 130 km/h on certain sections.
  • Technical Halts: Even in their "non-stop" era, Duronto trains had technical halts for crew changes and food/water loading.
  • First of its Kind: It was the first train concept in India explicitly designed to have no commercial stops between origin and destination.
