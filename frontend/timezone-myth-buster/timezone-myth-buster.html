⏰ Time Zone Myth-Buster

Can you guess why India — spanning 3,200 km east-to-west — uses just one time zone?

India's Longitude Span vs. Single Time Zone

India spans from ~68°E (Kutch, Gujarat) to ~97°E (Kibithu, Arunachal Pradesh) — about 29° of longitude across roughly 3,200 km.

68°E (Gujarat) 97°E (Arunachal) IST = UTC+5:30 ← ~2 hr solar difference →

📋 Factual Sources

  • Survey of India — geographic coordinates for India's eastern and western extremes
  • Government of India Resolution No. 1854 (1947) — adoption of IST
  • timeanddate.com — time zone database and daylight saving data
  • 1° longitude ≈ 85 km at 22°N latitude (cosine-scaled arc length)
