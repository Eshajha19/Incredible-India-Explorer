History & Legacy

Established in 1958 by the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), the prize is named after the founder Director of CSIR, Dr. Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar. It recognizes excellence in science and technology and is awarded annually for outstanding and conspicuous contributions in applied or fundamental research.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Citizens of India or Overseas Citizens of India (as applicable to current rules) working in India.
  • Outstanding research contributions made primarily in India during the last 5 years.
  • Age limit: up to 45 years (as per CSIR guidelines).
  • Significant published scientific work and measurable impact.
