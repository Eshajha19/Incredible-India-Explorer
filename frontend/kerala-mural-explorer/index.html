Kerala Temple Art

Kerala Murals

The sacred wall paintings of Kerala's ancient temples — drawn with natural pigments on lime-plastered walls, depicting Hindu epics, gods, and goddesses in a distinctive stylised form.

The Sacred Wall Art of Kerala

Natural Pigment Guide

Kerala murals use a strict palette of five natural pigments (Panchavarna) derived from minerals, roots, and soot — each colour carrying symbolic meaning.

Famous Temple Murals

Kerala's temples house some of the largest and oldest mural collections in India. Each temple's walls tell stories from the Ramayana, Mahabharata, and the Puranas.
