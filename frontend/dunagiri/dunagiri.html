Dunagiri Explorer

Standing at 7,066 meters in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand, Dunagiri is one of the most sacred and imposing peaks of the Kumaon region. Revered locally as the abode of Goddess Duna, this mountain guards the ancient trail to the sacred Nanda Devi sanctuary and has drawn adventurers and pilgrims for centuries.

Geography, Glaciers & History

The Guardian of the Inner Sanctuary

Dunagiri, rising to 7,066 meters, is a prominent peak in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand, located in the Chamoli district. It stands as one of the sentinels guarding the approach to the legendary Nanda Devi Inner Sanctuary, a natural amphitheater once considered unclimbable.

The name "Dunagiri" derives from "Drona" (a sage in Hindu mythology) and "giri" (mountain), linking it to the ancient epic Mahabharata. Locals revere the peak as the abode of Goddess Duna, a form of Parvati, and consider it sacred. The mountain holds deep spiritual significance for the people of Chamoli, who perform rituals before any expedition.

The first successful ascent was achieved on August 5, 1958, by an Indo-Japanese expedition team led by Hukam Singh. The route from the north face was particularly challenging, involving steep ice walls and technical rock climbing at extreme altitude. Dunagiri remains one of the less frequently climbed 7,000-meter peaks, preserving its remote and pristine character.

Technical Specifications

  • Elevation: 7,066 m (23,182 ft)

Trekking Guide

Explore the legendary trails that wind through the sacred valleys and alpine meadows at the foot of Dunagiri.

🗺️

Dunagiri Base Camp Trek

The classic trek starting from Wan village through dense forests of oak and rhododendron, crossing alpine meadows with panoramic views of Dunagiri and the Nanda Devi massif. The trail passes through Lata and Jhinjhi villages.

📏 8-10 Days ⬆️ 4,200 m ⚡ Moderate
🌲

Dunagiri Kund Trek

A serene trek to the sacred Dunagiri Kund (lake) at the base of the mountain, considered holy by local communities. The trail winds through alpine pastures with stunning views of the north face and surrounding peaks.

📏 6-7 Days ⬆️ 3,800 m ⚡ Easy-Moderate
🦅

Rataban Peak Trek

A challenging trek to Rataban Peak (6,126 m) offering close-up views of Dunagiri, Nanda Devi, and the Dronagiri range. The trail passes through the upper Rishi Ganga valley and high-altitude camps.

📏 7-9 Days ⬆️ 4,100 m ⚡ Moderate
🌸

Best Season & Permits

Best seasons: May-June (pre-monsoon wildflowers) and September-October (clear post-monsoon skies). Permits required from the Forest Department for trekking in the Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve area.
