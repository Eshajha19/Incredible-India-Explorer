About Shatabdi Express

The Shatabdi Express network represents India's premier daytime intercity rail service. Introduced to commemorate the birth centenary of India's first Prime Minister, Jawaharlal Nehru, these trains were designed to offer fast, comfortable, and reliable connectivity between major cities and business centers within a single day's journey.

Unlike overnight trains, Shatabdi services operate during the day, featuring primarily chair-car seating. They receive high priority on the railway network, ensuring punctuality and reducing travel time significantly compared to conventional express trains.
