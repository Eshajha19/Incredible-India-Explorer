Hirakud Reservoir Wetland Explorer

Discover one of Asia's largest artificial lakes, held back by the world's longest earthen dam on the Mahanadi River. Beyond irrigation and hydropower, Hirakud is a Ramsar-listed wetland supporting migratory birds, thousands of fishing families, and a scattering of submerged history.
