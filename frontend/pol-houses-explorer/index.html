Community & Layout

A Neighborhood Like No Other

A "Pol" (derived from the Sanskrit word pratoli, meaning gate) is a traditional housing cluster in the walled city of Ahmedabad. These neighborhoods were built to house communities bound by caste, profession, or religion, fostering a strong sense of security and belonging.

A typical Pol is characterized by narrow, winding streets, semi-private communal squares (chowks), and continuous rows of houses sharing common walls. The layout acts as a natural climate control mechanism, providing shade and channeling breezes through the dense urban fabric.

Key Architectural Features

  • Wooden Facades: Intricately carved wooden brackets, columns, and jharokhas.
  • Courtyards: Central open-to-sky courtyards ensuring natural ventilation.
  • Chabutra: Ornate, tower-like bird-feeding structures placed in community squares.
  • Narrow Streets: Shaded, pedestrian-scaled streets encouraging social interaction.
