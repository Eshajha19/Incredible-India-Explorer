Jharkhand Tribal Wall Art

Sohrai Painting

The ceremonial wall and floor art of Jharkhand's tribal communities — painted by women during the Sohrai harvest festival using natural earth pigments to celebrate cattle, fertility, and the forest.

The Harvest Festival Art of Jharkhand

Natural Pigments & Materials

Sohrai paintings are created entirely with natural earth pigments collected from the forest and riverbeds. The colours are mixed with water and a natural binder and applied with fingers, cloth rags, and chewed twigs.

Traditional Motifs

Sohrai motifs celebrate cattle, fertility, and forest life. Drawn freehand without preparatory sketches, each motif carries symbolic meaning tied to the agrarian cycle.
