An introduction

India, Beyond the Mainland

India's territory extends far beyond its mainland coastline. Two Union Territories — Andaman & Nicobar in the Bay of Bengal and Lakshadweep in the Arabian Sea — are entirely island-made. Scattered in between are river islands like Majuli, delta islands of the Sundarbans, and coastal rocks and backwater islets from Gujarat to Kerala. Each has its own geology, ecology, and culture.
