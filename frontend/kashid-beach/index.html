Kashid: White Sands of Raigad

Maharashtra's cleanest beach with pristine white sand, casuarina groves, and dramatic black basalt rock formations.

Maharashtra's White Sand Paradise

Kashid Beach, located in the Raigad district of Maharashtra, is one of the state's most scenic coastal destinations. Known for its clean white sand, clear blue waters, and the backdrop of lush casuarina groves, it offers a perfect weekend escape from Mumbai and Pune.

Geographic Location

Coordinates: 18.32°N, 72.93°E
District: Raigad, Maharashtra
Coastline: Konkan Coast, Arabian Sea
Nearest City: Alibaug (30 km), Mumbai (140 km), Pune (190 km)
Best Season: October to May (clear skies, pleasant weather)

Unlike many commercial beaches, Kashid retains a relatively untouched character with minimal development. The beach stretches for approximately 2 km and is flanked by casuarina trees that provide natural shade and create a distinctive silhouette against the horizon.
