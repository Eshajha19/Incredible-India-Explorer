Hemis National Park Explorer

Step into the spellbinding high-altitude wilderness of Ladakh. Nestled in the trans-Himalayan Zanskar Range, Hemis National Park is India's largest protected area and the global stronghold for the elusive Snow Leopard. Explore jagged icy peaks, ancient Buddhist Gompas, and high alpine passes.
