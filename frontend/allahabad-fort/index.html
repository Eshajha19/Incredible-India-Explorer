Overview

A Mughal stronghold beside the sacred Sangam

Allahabad Fort is presented as one of the grandest forts built by Emperor Akbar on the banks of the Triveni Sangam.

Imperial and sacred

The fort combines Mughal political power with the sacred geography of Prayagraj. Its walls, riverfront setting and internal heritage points make it a layered learning site.

Triveni Sangam

The Sangam setting gives the fort cultural, spiritual and geographic importance.

Ancient layers

The Ashoka Pillar and Patalpuri Temple connect the site with histories beyond the Mughal period.
