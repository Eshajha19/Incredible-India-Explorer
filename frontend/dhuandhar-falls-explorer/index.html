The Narmada River — one of India's five holy rivers — cuts through magnesium limestone marble rocks at Bhedaghat before narrowing down into a 30m plunge that creates mist clouds visible from afar.

Location: Bhedaghat, Jabalpur (35 km)
River: Narmada River Basin
Highlights: Marble Gorge, Mist Spray, Ropeway
Earth Science & Fluvial Erosion

Marble Rock Geology & Erosion Stages

Understand how Proterozoic limestone transformed into crystalline marble cliffs carved over millennia by the Narmada River.

01

Magnesium Metamorphism

Millions of years ago, sedimentary limestone underwent intense heat and pressure, metamorphosing into pure white, blue, and pink crystalline dolomite marble.

02

Fluvial Gorge Carving

The fast-flowing Narmada River exploited tectonic fault lines, carving out a narrow 3 km long, 100-foot deep marble canyon with vertical cliff walls.

03

Bandar Kudini Narrowing

At "Bandar Kudini" (Monkey's Leap), the canyon narrows so closely that monkeys historically leaped across the river gorge.

04

Dhuandhar Plunge Pool

The river forcefully squeezes into a narrow notch before falling 30m (98 ft), creating continuous vapor clouds (Dhuan) and a deep plunge pool.

Seasonal Experience

Monsoon Roar vs Winter Serene Boating

Compare the dramatic shift in river flow, water clarity, and tourist activities across seasons.

Monsoon Roaring Spray

During monsoon, the Narmada River swells dramatically. Water gushes over the marble notch with deafening sound, generating dense mist clouds that rise over 50 feet into the air. Boating is suspended for safety, but the ropeway offers breathtaking aerial views.

Flow: Heavy High-Volume Torrent Boating: Closed due to high currents Highlight: Aerial Ropeway Cable Ride
Visitor Information

Best Viewpoints & Travel Logistics

Plan your visit to Dhuandhar Falls with optimal viewing points and travel details.

👁️

East Bank Promenade

Paved walkway providing front-row viewing platform directly overlooking the plunge rim and mist cloud.

🚡

Aerial Ropeway Cable Car

Glide in a cable car across the 30m chasm between East and West banks for 360-degree top-down views of the waterfall.

🚣

Marble Rocks Boat Ride

Rowboats available at Panchvati Ghat during winter, allowing visitors to float through 100ft marble cliffs, specially under full moonlight (Sharad Purnima).

🏛️

Chausath Yogini Temple

10th-century circular shrine atop a nearby hill dedicated to 64 Yoginis, built by Kalachuri rulers offering panoramic river vistas.
