✨ South Indian Classical Art · Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

Tanjore Painting Showcase

Tanjore Painting (Thanjavur Art) is a classical South Indian painting style dating back to the 16th century under the patronage of Nayaka and Maratha rulers of Thanjavur. Renowned for its opulent 22-karat gold foil overlays, high-relief gesso work, and glittering semi-precious stone inlays, Tanjore art radiates divine splendor.

Royal Heritage

Classical Splendor of Thanjavur

Originating during the reign of the Vijayanagara Empire and blossoming under the Nayakas and Marathas of Thanjavur (16th–18th century CE), Tanjore painting is characterized by iconic central deities set inside gilded archways and ornamental frames (Mantara). Artists crafted these relief masterpieces on wooden boards called Palagai Padam (Jackwood or Teak plank coated with Arabic gum and unbleached cloth).

The distinctive three-dimensional relief effect and pure gold foil preserve the brilliance of Tanjore paintings for centuries without fading.

🏛️ Tanjore Art Snapshot

  • Origin: Thanjavur (Tanjore), Tamil Nadu
  • Era: 16th Century CE (Vijayanagara & Maratha Patronage)
  • Base Medium: Jackwood Board (Palagai Padam) & Cotton Canvas
  • Embellishment: 22-Karat Pure Gold Foil & Jaipur Glass/Precious Stones
  • Primary Focus: Devotional Iconography of Hindu Deities
Craftsmanship

Gold Foil & Gesso Relief Process

Step-by-step breakdown of how master artisans apply gold leaf to achieve timeless radiance.

1

Board Preparation

Unbleached cotton cloth is pasted over a seasoned Jackwood board using gum and limestone paste, creating a smooth ceramic-like gesso surface.

2

Mukku Work (Gesso Relief)

Artisans apply unrefined chalk powder mixed with natural glue to create raised 3D relief work on pillars, crowns, and jewelry embellishments.

3

Gem Inlay & Gold Leafing

Semi-precious stones and glass rubies are embedded into the gesso, followed by delicate application of 22K pure gold foil sheets over relief areas.

4

Vivid Color Shading

Rich, flat, water-based natural paints are applied to the deity's body and background, framed by glistening golden borders.

Iconography

Classical Religious Themes

Tanjore artworks focus primarily on sacred devotional icons crafted for home shrines and temple altars.

🪔

Balakrishna (Butter Krishna)

The most iconic Tanjore theme: infant Lord Krishna holding a pot of butter, encircled by prabhavali archways and gopi bards.

🐘

Lord Ganesha & Goddess Lakshmi

Golden portrayals of Lord Ganesha (Vighnaharta) and Goddess Gajalakshmi seated on lotus thrones, symbolizing prosperity.

👑

Sita Rama Pattabhishekam

Elaborate royal coronation scenes of Lord Rama and Sita, surrounded by Lakshmana, Bharata, Shatrughna, and Lord Hanuman.

Artisan Raw Materials

Color Palette & Traditional Materials

Tanjore painting uses a curated palette of vibrant, flat primary colors that contrast sharply against gold leaf.

22-Karat Gold Leaf

Ultrathin gold foil leaves that do not tarnish or corrode over centuries.

Crimson & Vermilion

Rich red hues used for backdrops and royal robes.

Ultramarine Blue

Vivid blue shades representing Lord Krishna and Vishnu.

Chalk & Arabic Gum

Used to form the durable 3D gesso relief paste.
