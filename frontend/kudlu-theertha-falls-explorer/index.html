📍 Field notes

A waterfall reached through the forest

Location & water source

Kudlu Theertha Falls lies near Hebri in Udupi district, Karnataka, within the rain-rich Western Ghats and Someshwara Wildlife Sanctuary landscape. The falls are associated with the Sita River and are also known as Koodlu Theertha or Sita Falls.

Region
Near Hebri, Udupi district
Water source
Sita River
Height
About 150 ft (Udupi Tourism)

Forest environment

The approach moves through dense Western Ghats forest, streams and rocky ground. Someshwara Wildlife Sanctuary contains evergreen and semi-evergreen forests and supports rich bird and wildlife diversity.

Evergreen forestStreamsWildlifeRainforest

Karnataka Tourism advises visitors not to explore the dense sanctuary forest without an expert guide.

TRAILHEAD

Road ends; the walk begins

From the approach near the Sita River, the route transitions from road travel to a forest trek. The final approach is not a casual roadside stop: plan for uneven terrain and allow time for the return.

  • Use sturdy footwear.
  • Carry drinking water and essentials.
  • Start with enough daylight for the return.

🧭 Access

Plan the approach, not just the destination

01

Reach Hebri

Hebri is the practical gateway for the final approach from the Udupi side. Local road access continues toward the Sita River area.

02

Cross & trek

Published route descriptions place the trail at roughly 4 km through forest after reaching the river approach. Terrain is uneven and can become slippery.

03

Return in daylight

Carry essentials, avoid heavy-rain conditions, and leave enough time for the forest return. Do not treat the trail as a late-evening walk.
