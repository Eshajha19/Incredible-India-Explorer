Mountain overview

A popular trekking peak in the Pir Panjal

Friendship Peak is a 5,289 m mountain in Himachal Pradesh's Pir Panjal range. It is known for high-altitude trekking scenery, snow views, alpine terrain, and its connection with the Manali side of the Himalayas.

Natural beauty

The Friendship Peak landscape combines forests, open meadows, moraine sections, snow patches, and broad Himalayan views. This makes it a strong addition to the Mountains of India landing page.

Why it matters

The peak is one of the more recognisable trekking objectives in Himachal, so users should be able to explore it through a dedicated page.

Responsible trekking notes

Before planning Friendship Peak

This page is educational and should not be treated as a live route guide. Trekkers must verify trail status, permissions, weather, snow conditions, guide support, emergency options, and personal fitness before travelling.

Check weatherMountain weather can change quickly, especially around snow and exposed sections.
Respect terrainStay on safe trails, avoid fragile meadows, and carry waste back.
Use guidesLocal guides and trek leaders help manage route, pace, safety, and emergency decisions.
Prepare for altitudeCold, fatigue, and altitude symptoms require honest preparation and cautious pacing.

Safety note

Friendship Peak may be popular, but it is still a high-altitude mountain environment. Use official/local guidance before real travel.
