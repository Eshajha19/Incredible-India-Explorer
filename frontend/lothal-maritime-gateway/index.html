Lothal: India's Ancient Maritime Gateway

Discover Lothal (2400 BCE – 1900 BCE) in Gujarat — world's earliest artificial tidal dockyard, hub of Mesopotamian trade, advanced bead-making industry, and Harappan urban excellence.

Engineering Marvel

Dockyard Interpretation & Warehouse

Tidal Lock-Gate & Sluice System

The 218m x 37m kiln-burnt brick basin connected to Sabarmati/Bhogavo estuary via an inlet channel. A wooden sluice gate trapped tidal waters, allowing ships to float during low tide.

The Great Granary / Warehouse

A massive raised mud-brick platform supporting 64 cubical mud-brick blocks where cargo was stored, inspected, and sealed with clay sealings (terracotta sealings found on site).

Bronze Age Network

Ancient Trade Routes & Connections

Lothal served as the gateway connecting Meluhha (Indus Valley) with Magan (Oman), Dilmun (Bahrain), and Ur/Akkad in Mesopotamia.

🏛️

Mesopotamia (Meluhha Trade)

Exported polished carnelian beads, ivory, timber, and lapis lazuli; imported silver, wool, and oil.

⛵

Persian Gulf (Dilmun & Magan)

Circular steatite button seals from Bahrain found at Lothal prove direct seafaring contact across Arabian Sea.

🌾

Intra-Harappan Links

Linked to Dholavira, Surkotada, Kalibangan, and Harappa via coastal and riverine routes.

Industries & Artisans

Bead-Making, Pottery & Metallurgical Crafts

Bead-Making Industry

State-of-the-art bead factory equipped with double-chambered circular kilns, bronze drills, agate/carnelian raw stones, and finished micro-beads.

Pottery, Shell & Metallurgy

Red ware pottery painted with animal motifs, shell bangles from Gulf of Kutch shankha shells, and copper saws and fish-hooks.

Archaeological Artifact Gallery & Credible Sources

Persian Gulf Seal

Steatite circular seal depicting gazelles, validating Persian Gulf trade.

Terracotta Boat Models

Clay boat models showing masted sailing vessels with oars.

Standardized Chert Weights

Precision cubic weights used for commercial transactions.
