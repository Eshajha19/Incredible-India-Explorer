Kokborok (Tripuri)

The living language of 1.1 million Tripuri people — from ancient Koloma script to modern digital expression, Kokborok carries centuries of cultural memory.

👋 Traditional Greeting IPA: /kʰu.lum.kʰa/

Khulumkha!

Koloma (Ancient) 𑰏𑰲𑰩𑰲𑰦𑰏𑰯
Bengali (Official) খুলুমখা
Roman (Modern) Khulumkha

"Hello / Warmest Greetings / Welcome"

Performed with folded hands, Khulumkha expresses respect, peace, and traditional hospitality — the foundation of Tripuri social etiquette.
