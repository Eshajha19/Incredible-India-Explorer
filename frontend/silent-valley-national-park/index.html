Park overview

A rare rainforest world in the Western Ghats

Silent Valley National Park is presented as one of India's last undisturbed tropical rainforests. Its story connects evergreen canopy, endemic species, rivers, conservation, and the lion-tailed macaque.

Rainforest ecosystem

Dense evergreen trees, layered canopy, humid valleys, streams and shaded forest floors create habitats for primates, birds, reptiles, insects and plants.

Lion-tailed macaque

The endangered lion-tailed macaque is a powerful symbol of canopy protection and rainforest continuity.

Conservation value

Silent Valley teaches why undisturbed rainforest, river protection and low-impact tourism matter.
