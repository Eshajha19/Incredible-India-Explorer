Shivling Explorer

Rising dramatically as a twin-peaked pyramid to 6,543 meters, Shivling is one of the most stunning and visually striking peaks in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand. Its sheer vertical granite walls and complex ice ridges make it the 'Matterhorn of India' and a legendary alpine climbing arena.

Geography, Glaciers & History

The Sacred Sanctuary

Shivling is located in the Gangotri Group of peaks in the western Garhwal Himalaya, guarding the source of the holy Ganges. Situated just opposite the Bhagirathi massif, it towers directly above Tapovan, a serene, high-altitude meadow renowned for meditation and spiritual pilgrimage.

The mountain rises as a single peak but culminates in a distinct twin summit, with the main peak being the western one. Its name represents the sacred symbol of Lord Shiva, a fitting title for a mountain of such divine majesty and geometric perfection.

First climbed in June 1974 by a team from the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), Shivling has since seen legendary alpine climbs on its west ridge and north face, winning global acclaim for pioneers of modern climbing styles.

Technical Specifications

  • Elevation: 6,543 m (21,467 ft)
  • Prominence: 850 m (2,789 ft)
  • Coordinates: 30°87'53"N 79°06'56"E
  • Climbing Difficulty: Grade V/VI (Extreme / Alpine)
  • First Ascent: June 3, 1974
  • Expedition Team: Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)
