Himalaya: Herbal Heritage

From a small Ayurvedic pharmacy in 1930 to India's trusted herbal personal care brand serving 100+ countries.

The Origin Story

1930

Foundation in Bangalore

M. Manal founded Himalaya Drug Company in Bangalore with a vision to bring Ayurvedic medicine to modern healthcare. Starting as a small pharmacy, it focused on research-backed herbal formulations.

1934

Liv.52 Launch

Himalaya launched Liv.52, its flagship liver care supplement. This Ayurvedic formulation became revolutionary in hepatology and remains one of the world's best-selling liver supplements.

1950s

Research Foundation

Established dedicated R&D facilities to scientifically validate Ayurvedic formulations, bridging traditional knowledge with modern pharmacology.

Packaging & Brand Evolution

1930s-1960s

Pharmacy Era

Simple glass bottles and cardboard packaging reflected the pharmaceutical nature of the brand. Minimal design focused on medicinal credibility.

1970s-1990s

Consumer Transition

Introduction of plastic containers and colorful labels as Himalaya expanded into personal care. The iconic green packaging became recognizable.

2000s-2010s

Modern Branding

Complete brand overhaul with modern logo, premium packaging, and eco-friendly materials. Introduction of tubes, pumps, and sustainable packaging.

2020s-Present

Digital & Sustainable

E-commerce optimized packaging, recyclable materials, and minimalist design. Focus on sustainability and premium aesthetic appeal.
