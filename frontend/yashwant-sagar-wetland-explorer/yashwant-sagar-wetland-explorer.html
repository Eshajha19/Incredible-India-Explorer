Yashwant Sagar Wetland

Ramsar Wetland & Important Bird Habitat

Near Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India

View Waterbirds View Map
Heritage

A Vital Reservoir Ecosystem

Historical Construction

Constructed over the Gambhir River to supply water to the city of Indore, Yashwant Sagar has evolved over decades from a man-made reservoir into an ecologically irreplaceable freshwater wetland habitat.

Ramsar Recognition

Recognized internationally under the Ramsar Convention for its role in biodiversity conservation, meeting strict criteria for providing sanctuary to threatened ecological communities and supporting large populations of waterbirds.

Regional Water Management

Beyond supporting wildlife, the wetland continues its historical purpose of sustaining regional groundwater levels and supplying fresh water to the expanding urban areas of Indore.

Avian Paradise

Migratory Waterbirds

An essential wintering stopover on the Central Asian Flyway.

Painted Stork

Near Threatened

Flocks of these colorful storks can be seen wading in the shallows, sweeping their half-open bills side to side to catch fish.

Northern Pintail

Winter Migrant

Arriving from the northern latitudes, these elegant dabbling ducks are among the most numerous winter visitors to the lake.

Spot-billed Duck

Resident Species

A resident duck species that utilizes the dense aquatic vegetation of Yashwant Sagar for nesting and foraging year-round.

Bar-headed Goose

High-Altitude Migrant

Known for migrating over the Himalayas, these geese find refuge and abundant food in the reservoir's winter landscape.

Other Notable Arrivals: Common Teal, Eurasian Wigeon, Gadwall, and Black-winged Stilt heavily rely on this habitat during peak winter migration.

Ecosystem

Aquatic Biodiversity

A perfectly balanced freshwater ecosystem sustaining a complex food chain.

  • Fish & Amphibians: A high diversity of native fish and amphibian species forms the primary food source for the avian population.
  • Aquatic Vegetation: Submerged and emergent plant life naturally filters water quality while providing crucial spawning grounds.
  • Insects & Reptiles: The marshy edges support a thriving population of insects and small reptiles, ensuring ecological balance.
Activity

Bird Watching

Yashwant Sagar is highly accessible, making it a favorite for nature enthusiasts.

  • Peak Season: The best time for birding is between November and February when migratory flocks arrive.
  • Photography: Early mornings offer perfect lighting over the calm waters for capturing birds in flight.
  • Responsible Guidelines: Visitors are encouraged to maintain silence, avoid bright clothing, and stay on designated paths.
