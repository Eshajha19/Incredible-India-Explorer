🇮🇳 Third-Highest Civilian Honour

Padma Bhushan Explorer

Conferred for distinguished service of a high order in any field, celebrating individuals who leave an indelible imprint on Indian society.

Year Instituted: 1954 Award Rank: 3rd Civilian Honour Conferred By: President of India

History & Genesis

Instituted in 1954

Instituted on 2 January 1954 as 'Dusra Varg' (Second Class), it was renamed Padma Bhushan by presidential notification on 8 January 1955. It ranks behind Bharat Ratna and Padma Vibhushan, preceding Padma Shri.

Purpose & Significance

Designed to recognize distinguished service of a high order, honoring trailblazers in science, medicine, corporate leadership, literature, performing arts, and public service.

Eligibility Guidelines

  • Open Access: Open to all citizens without distinction of race, occupation, position, or sex.
  • Service Standard: Requires distinguished service of a high order in any domain.
  • Government Servants Exclusion: PSUs and government officials excluded, with explicit exception for doctors and research scientists.

Medal Design

🏵️

Circular Bronze Badge

Features a circular bronze plate with a stylized geometric square outer pattern. A lotus flower is embossed in the center with three petals leaves gilded in white gold.

📜

Devanagari Inscription

"Padma" is written above the lotus and "Bhushan" below it in silver-gilt Devanagari script. The reverse bears the National Emblem of India.

Selection Process

Selection is conducted through the annual Padma Awards Committee appointed by the Prime Minister. Public recommendations are vetted with security and background checks before final presidential approval.

Fields of Recognition

Art & Literature

Classical music maestros, authors, dramatists, and painters.

Trade & Technology

Global tech CEOs, pioneering entrepreneurs, and industrial visionaries.

Medicine & Science

Surgeons, medical researchers, atomic physicists, and space scientists.

Public Life & Sports

Social leaders, parliamentarians, and legendary sports champions.

Interesting Facts

Foreign Dignitaries

Awarded to distinguished international supporters of Indian culture and industry, including diplomats and authors.

Precedence Protocols

Recipients feature high in the official Table of Precedence and receive ceremonial state honours at funerals.
