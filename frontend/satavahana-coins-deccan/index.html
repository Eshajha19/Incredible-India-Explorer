The Satavahana Empire controlled crucial trans-continental trade routes across the Western Ghats passes (Naneghat, Thalghat) linking inland capitals like Pratishthana to ports like Sopara, Kalyan, and Motupalli.

Ruler Associated: Gautamiputra Satakarni / Pulumavi
Key Coin Types: Maritime Ship Coins, Ujjain Cross
Primary Metals: Lead, Potin, Silver, Copper
Iconography & Emblems

Satavahana Symbol Hotspots

Click on sacred motifs to inspect their royal, religious, and commercial significance.

⛵

Twin-Masted Ship

Emblem of Indian Ocean trade power under Yajna Sri Satakarni, signifying maritime prowess and Roman trade connections.

🛑

Ujjain Symbol

Cross with four orb circles (sometimes containing swastikas), symbolizing universal sovereignty over the four quarters of the earth.

🏔️

Three-Arched Hill & Crescent

Represents Meru or Chaitya stupa surmounted by the lunar crescent, showing Buddhist patrons and royal mountain motifs.

🐘

Royal Elephant

Symbol of royal majesty, military strength, and divine patronage associated with Indra's Airavata on Satavahana lead coppers.

🦁

Solar Lion

Popular on Junnar and Central Deccan coinages representing sovereign power and Sakyamuni Simha (Lion of the Sakya clan).

🐂

Humped Bull (Nandi)

Associated with Shaivite worship and agrarian prosperity across Maharashtra and Telangana mints.
