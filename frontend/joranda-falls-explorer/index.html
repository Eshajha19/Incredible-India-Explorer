Joranda Falls: The Single-Drop Cascade

An astonishing 181-meter (594 ft) single-drop sheer plunge waterfall dropping perpendicularly off ancient black volcanic basalt cliffs into the deep, untouched wilderness of Similipal National Park.

💧 181m (594 ft) Unbroken Plunge 🏛️ Perpendicular Cliff Architecture 🐅 Similipal Core Sanctuary 📍 Mayurbhanj District, Odisha
Geomorphology

Single-Drop Plunge Structure & Volcanic Setting

Unlike its multi-tiered neighbor Barehipani, Joranda falls in one unbroken vertical curtain directly into a cloud-filled forest cauldron.

📐

Perpendicular Freefall

The stream launches clear of the cliff edge, maintaining an unbroken vertical trajectory for 181 meters without hitting intermediate rock ledges.

🌋

Precambrian Basalt Cliff

Cascades over sheer volcanic spilite and quartz basalt formations dating back over 2.8 billion years to the Dhanjori geological volcanic era.

🌪️

Vapor Vortex Plunge Basin

The kinetic force of the 181m fall creates a perpetual upward vortex of mist, keeping the surrounding cliff faces carpeted in rare mosses and ferns.

🌿

Pristine Forest Catchment

Fed by perennial mountain rivulets draining the high Meghasani plateau, flowing through dense sal forests before joining the Budhabalanga river network.

Winter Splendor (November – February) — Prime Visiting Period

Following the southwest monsoon, Joranda Falls roars with magnificent volume. The vertical white column stands out brilliantly against the dark mossy rock wall, surrounded by flowering wild orchids and crisp 12°C forest breezes.

Flow: Thick, Roaring Silver Column Visibility: Crystal clear with vibrant rainbows Highlights: Viewpoint photography, tiger safaris & birding
Wildlife Sanctuary

Similipal Forest Surroundings & Viewpoints

Protected within the core zone of one of India's most biodiverse tiger sanctuaries.

🔭

Forest Observation Pavilion

A raised wooden viewing tower directly opposite the falls allows visitors to safely view the entire 181-meter single drop from crest to base.

🐅

Tiger & Elephant Territory

The surrounding forest valleys provide vital foraging grounds for wild Asian elephant herds, sambar deer, and elusive Royal Bengal tigers.

🌺

94 Wild Orchid Varieties

The humid microclimate created by Joranda's continuous spray nurtures rare endemic epiphytic orchids blooming in winter and spring.

💧

Barehipani Twin Excursion

Located just 20 km away on the same safari circuit, allowing visitors to witness both the tallest single-drop and 2-tiered cascades in a single day.

Joranda Falls Pavilion

Similipal Core Reserve, Mayurbhanj

Constructed opposite the falls chasm, the official forest viewing gazebo offers an unobstructed, direct line of sight to the 181m perpendicular waterfall and its mist-shrouded base.
