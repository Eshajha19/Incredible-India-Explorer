Explore Soochipara Falls

Also known as Sentinel Rock Waterfalls ("Soochi" = Needle, "Para" = Rock in Malayalam), this breathtaking 200-meter (656 ft) three-tiered cascade drops through dense montane evergreen rainforests, feeding the Chaliyar River basin.

💧 200m (656 ft) 3-Tier Drop 🧗 Needle-Shaped Rock Climbing Cliff 🥾 2 km Montane Forest Trek 🏊 Natural Pool for Safe Swimming

Soochipara Main Cascade

200m Multi-Tiered Waterfall Drop

Surrounded by deciduous and wet evergreen forests in Meppadi panchayat, this multi-tier waterfall plunges into a refreshing rocky pool before flowing down as a tributary of the Chaliyar River.

Location: Vellarimala, Meppadi, Wayanad
Trek Distance: 2 km (Paved forest trail with steps)
Highlights: Natural pool bath, mist spray, evergreen views

Best Visiting Season & Seasonal Dynamics

Plan your visit according to river flow strength, monsoon safety restrictions, and weather clarity.

Post-Monsoon & Winter Peak (September to February)

The ideal season to explore Soochipara Falls. The waterfall flows with immense volume and crystal-clear emerald water. Lush green shola forests thrive, trekking paths are safe and dry, and visitors can enjoy bathing under the lower plunge pool.

Flow: Robust, Clear & Roaring Trek Trail: Safe, Dry & Picturesque Highlights: Natural pool swimming, rock photography & tea estate walk
Wayanad Circuit

Nearby Attractions in Wayanad

Pair your Soochipara trek with these iconic regional destinations.

⛰️

Chembra Peak (2,100 m)

The highest peak in Wayanad district, renowned for its heart-shaped lake (Hridaya Saras) and trekking through misty grasslands.

🌊

Meenmutty Falls (300 m)

A spectacular 3-tier cascade situated 12 km away, ranking as the largest waterfall in Wayanad district.

💧

Kanthanpara Waterfalls

A charming, peaceful 30-meter waterfall located just 8 km from Soochipara, ideal for relaxed family picnics.

🍃

Meppadi & Vellarimala Tea Gardens

Vast rolling emerald tea and coffee plantations offering scenic photo stops, estate tours, and fresh tea tasting.

Travel Logistics

Trek Information, Access & Safety

Essential practical details for visitors preparing for the downhill forest walk.

🥾

Trail Route & Difficulty

2 km downhill walk from the forest checkpost through well-laid concrete steps. Moderate difficulty returning uphill.

🚗

How to Reach

22 km from Kalpetta, 13 km from Meppadi town. Accessible by taxi or jeep up to the forest parking terminal.

⏰

Timings & Entry Fees

Open daily from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Entry closes by 4:00 PM). Forest department permits issued at the counter.

🛡️

Eco & Plastic-Free Protocol

Plastic bottles are tagged or restricted at the forest entry checkpoint to protect indigenous wildlife.
