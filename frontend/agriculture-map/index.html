Celebrating the Harvest

Agriculture in India is deeply intertwined with cultural celebrations. Explore major harvest festivals across the country.

🌾 Makar Sankranti / Pongal / Lohri

Mid-January

Celebrated under various names across India, this festival marks the end of the winter solstice and the harvest of Rabi crops like wheat and sugarcane. Lohri is massive in Punjab, Pongal in Tamil Nadu honors the sun god and cattle, and Makar Sankranti involves kite flying in Gujarat and Maharashtra.

🌸 Baisakhi / Rongali Bihu

Mid-April

Marks the harvest of the Rabi (winter) crops and the start of the solar new year. Baisakhi is celebrated with immense joy in Punjab involving Bhangra dances. Rongali Bihu in Assam celebrates the beginning of the Assamese New Year and the sowing season.

🌴 Onam

August-September

The biggest harvest festival of Kerala. It celebrates the rice harvest and the mythological homecoming of King Mahabali. Features elaborate flower carpets (Pookkalam), boat races (Vallam Kali), and a grand feast (Sadhya).

🍚 Nuakhai

August-September

An agricultural festival mainly observed in Odisha. The word 'Nua' means new and 'Khai' means food. It is celebrated to welcome the newly harvested rice of the season with deep cultural reverence.
