India's Renewable Energy Growth

A journey through the nation's monumental shift towards a sustainable, clean energy future.

India's Energy Mix

India is undergoing one of the world's most significant energy transitions. As the third-largest energy consumer globally, the country has prioritized increasing the share of non-fossil fuels in its electricity generation mix. Renewables—particularly solar and wind—now form the backbone of this transformation.

Solar Capacity Growth

Driven by large-scale solar parks, falling costs of photovoltaic (PV) cells, and an aggressive policy push, solar energy has seen exponential growth. Utility-scale projects in states like Rajasthan and Gujarat lead the charge in making India a global solar powerhouse.

Wind Capacity Growth

India is a pioneer in wind energy in Asia. With strong winds along its vast coastline, states like Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, and Maharashtra are major wind power producers. India is also venturing into offshore wind developments to further boost its renewable capacity.

2030 Targets

India has committed to an ambitious target of achieving 500 GW of non-fossil fuel-based energy capacity by 2030. This includes massive scaling of solar, wind, hydro, and bio-power, reducing the carbon intensity of the economy by 45% by 2030.
