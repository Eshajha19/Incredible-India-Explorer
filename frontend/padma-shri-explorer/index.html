Padma Shri Explorer

Recognizing distinguished contributions across diverse disciplines including arts, literature, sports, science, and grassroots unsung champions.

Year Instituted: 1954 Focus: Grassroots & Excellence Conferred By: President of India

History & Purpose

Instituted in 1954

Established on 2 January 1954 under the title 'Tisra Varg' (Third Class), it was renamed Padma Shri by presidential notification on 8 January 1955. It is India's fourth-highest civilian honour.

Democratic Honor

The Padma Shri celebrates citizen impact across every corner of India, honoring educators, social workers, folk artists, sports champions, and community leaders.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Broad Eligibility: Open to any citizen without distinction of race, occupation, position, or gender.
  • Discipline Scope: Covers Art, Social Work, Public Affairs, Science & Engineering, Trade & Industry, Medicine, Literature & Education, Sports, and Civil Service.
  • No Monetary Grant: Carries a commemorative certificate (Sanad) and medallion without cash grants.

Medal Design

🌸

Lotus Flower Insignia

Features a circular burnished bronze badge with a geometric square motif. The central disc displays an embossed lotus flower with burnished bronze detailing.

✍️

Devanagari Inscription

The words "Padma" and "Shri" are embossed above and below the lotus in Devanagari script. The reverse displays the Lion Capital of Ashoka and motto.

Selection Process & People's Padma

Through the "People's Padma" initiative, citizens across India submit nominations via the Rashtriya Puraskar Portal. The Padma Awards Committee scrutinizes entries to identify grassroots heroes and unsung champions.

Fields of Recognition

Grassroots & Unsung Heroes

Tree planters, indigenous healers, organic farmers, and rural educators.

Arts & Folk Heritage

Craft revivers, tribal dancers, puppet masters, and folk singers.

Sports & Athletics

Olympic medallists, Paralympic champions, and indigenous martial arts masters.
