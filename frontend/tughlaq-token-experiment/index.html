Before the experiment

Why introduce a new currency?

Muhammad bin Tughlaq's reign was marked by ambitious administrative and economic policies. He needed large resources to maintain his massive army and fund his campaigns. The token currency experiment attempted to change how monetary value was represented and circulated by introducing copper and brass tokens that would have the same value as gold and silver coins (tanka).

Historical Interpretations

The token currency experiment is often discussed as an example of the difficulties involved in maintaining trust in a fiat-like monetary system. While the Sultan's concept was ahead of its time, its failure has been interpreted in the wider context of his ambitious administrative policies, lack of minting monopoly, and the economic realities of 14th-century India.
