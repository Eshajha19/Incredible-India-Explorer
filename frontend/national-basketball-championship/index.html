National Basketball Championship

The National Basketball Championship is India's premier domestic basketball competition organized under the Basketball Federation of India (BFI).

The championship brings together leading state and institutional teams and has played a significant role in developing basketball talent across the country. It serves as an important pathway for national team selection and elite-level competition.
