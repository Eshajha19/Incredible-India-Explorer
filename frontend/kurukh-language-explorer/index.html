Kurukh Scripts: Tolong Siki & Devanagari

Explore the dual writing systems of Kurukh—from the indigenous Tolong Siki alphabet to standardized Devanagari orthography.

🔤

Tolong Siki Script (𑒏𑒳𑒛𑒳𑒐)

Created in 1999 by Dr. Narayan Oraon, Tolong Siki was designed to capture specific Kurukh phonetics, tone contours, and glottal stops. Officially recognized in Jharkhand and West Bengal for school education and literary publications.

𑒏𑒳𑒛𑒳𑒐 𑒏𑒰𑒟𑒰 𑒢𑒰𑒧𑒹 𑒡𑒩𑒧𑒹
📜

Devanagari Script (कुड़ुख़)

Standardized with modified diacritics (such as ़ for retroflex stops and ऽ for glottal sounds), Devanagari is widely used in university curriculums, dictionaries, community periodicals, and digital media.
