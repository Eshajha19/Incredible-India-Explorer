Geology

Oxbow Lake Formation

A natural marvel formed by the meandering of the Gandak River.

Kanwar Lake, locally known as Kabartal Jheel, is a classic example of an oxbow lake. It was formed thousands of years ago when the meandering Gandak River changed its course, leaving behind a crescent-shaped water body.

During the monsoon season, the lake expands significantly as it receives floodwaters from the Gandak, creating a vast shallow wetland that is ideal for aquatic vegetation and wading birds.

🌊

The Oxbow Process

River meanders are cut off from the main channel, creating isolated, nutrient-rich water bodies that become biodiversity hotspots.

Global Recognition

Ramsar Site

Designated in 2020, recognizing its international importance for waterbird conservation.

Kanwar Lake was designated as Bihar's first Ramsar Site in 2020. This recognition highlights its critical role in supporting vulnerable, endangered, and critically endangered bird species.

The wetland provides essential feeding and roosting grounds for tens of thousands of migratory waterfowl that travel along the Central Asian Flyway, escaping the harsh winters of Central Asia.

Protection

Wetland Conservation

Efforts to protect the lake from encroachment and pollution.

Conservation challenges include agricultural encroachment, water hyacinth proliferation, and pollution from nearby settlements. The Bihar State Wetland Authority is actively working on demarcation and management plans.

Community-based conservation initiatives are being promoted to involve local villagers in protecting the lake while providing alternative livelihoods that do not harm the ecosystem.

People

Local Communities

The symbiotic relationship between the wetland and the people of Begusarai.

The lake is integral to the livelihoods of local fishing communities. Sustainable fishing practices are encouraged to ensure that fish populations remain viable for future generations.

During the winter months, the influx of birdwatchers and researchers also provides eco-tourism opportunities for the local economy, fostering a sense of pride and ownership in conservation.
