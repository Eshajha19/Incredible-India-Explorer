🛡️ Children's Bravery

Sanjay Chopra Award

Presented under the National Bravery Awards to recognize exceptional acts of bravery and selflessness by boys.

ICCW Awarded By
Republic Day Honored On

History & Legacy

The Sanjay Chopra Award is an integral part of the National Bravery Awards. It was instituted in memory of Sanjay Chopra, who tragically lost his life while bravely resisting kidnappers. The award was established to honor extraordinary courage displayed by children, recognizing selfless acts performed despite great personal risk.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Awarded specifically to boys under the National Bravery Awards framework.
  • Recognizes exceptional courage and presence of mind in dangerous situations.
  • Acts must be performed in the service of saving lives or protecting others against crime or natural disasters.
  • Selection is based strictly on verified acts of bravery reported by authorities.

Award Significance

Inspiration: Encourages courage and selflessness among children nationwide.

Recognition: Provides national recognition for extraordinary bravery at a very young age.

Heroism: Celebrates real-life heroism, showing that courage has no age limit.

Responsibility: Inspires young citizens to act responsibly and bravely during emergencies.

Interesting Facts

  • 🎖️ Named in honor of the brave teenager Sanjay Chopra.
  • 🤝 Presented as part of the overarching National Bravery Awards by the ICCW.
  • 👧 There is a counterpart award for girls known as the Geeta Chopra Award.
  • 🐘 Award recipients traditionally participated in the Republic Day parade on decorated elephants (and now on open jeeps).

Frequently Asked Questions

Sanjay Chopra was a brave boy who, along with his sister Geeta, lost his life while courageously resisting kidnappers in New Delhi in 1978. The award was named in his honor.

The Indian Council for Child Welfare (ICCW) historically organizes and selects the recipients for the National Bravery Awards.
