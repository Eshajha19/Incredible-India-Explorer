📜 History of the Award

The Vigyan Ratna is the highest honour in India's new family of national science awards — the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (RVP). The RVP scheme was approved by the Union Cabinet in December 2023 and announced by the Ministry of Science & Technology in January 2024, modernizing India's science honours after more than six decades.

Genesis and Vision

The Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar was created to recognize exceptional contributions in science, technology, and innovation — and to motivate young Indians to pursue scientific careers. The Vigyan Ratna sits at the very top of this framework, reserved for scientists with lifetime achievements in their fields.

Evolution Over the Years

The RVP framework absorbed India's long-standing Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize as the Vigyan Yuva – Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar category, dedicated to young scientists. The first awards, including the inaugural Vigyan Ratna, were conferred on 23 August 2024 — India's first National Space Day, marking the anniversary of the Chandrayaan-3 lunar landing.

Significance and Impact

As India's highest scientific honour, the Vigyan Ratna shines a spotlight on the scientists whose lifelong pursuit of knowledge has strengthened the nation — from fundamental research to space missions and transformative technology.

Institutional Framework

The awards are administered by the Ministry of Science & Technology, with selection committees chaired by the Principal Scientific Adviser to the Government of India, and are presented by the President of India at Rashtrapati Bhavan.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

🧑‍🔬 Who Can Apply

Scientists, researchers, technologists, and innovators from academia, research & development organizations, government institutions, and industry are eligible for the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar categories.

🏆 The Vigyan Ratna Standard

Nominations for the Vigyan Ratna are considered for candidates with lifetime achievements and sustained contributions in science, technology, or innovation — the very highest standard of scientific excellence.

👥 Teams Welcome

In addition to individual categories, the Vigyan Team category recognizes collaborative teams and groups whose joint efforts have delivered exceptional scientific outcomes.

📝 How to Nominate

Nominations are invited through the official Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar online portal, where individuals and organizations can submit detailed proposals with supporting documentation for the award committees.

Core Evaluation Themes

Nominations are assessed on the basis of scientific excellence, innovation, societal impact, and contribution to national development — with the Vigyan Ratna additionally requiring a demonstrated record of sustained lifetime achievement.

⚙️ Selection Process

The selection process is rigorous and transparent, ensuring that India's highest science honours are conferred only on the most deserving.

1

Nomination

Nominations are submitted through the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar online portal by individuals, institutions, and scientific bodies, with full documentation of the candidate's contributions.

2

Field-wise Screening

Nominations are examined by expert committees across scientific disciplines, which shortlist candidates based on originality, impact, and excellence of their work.

3

High-Level Selection Committee

A high-level committee chaired by the Principal Scientific Adviser to the Government of India reviews the shortlist and recommends the final awardees.

4

Government Approval & Conferment

The recommended names are approved by the Government of India and conferred by the President of India at Rashtrapati Bhavan on National Space Day.

Evaluation Matrix

  • Scientific Excellence: Rigour, originality, and quality of research and innovation
  • Societal Impact: Contribution to solving India's real-world challenges
  • Innovation: Novelty and creativity of scientific and technological approaches
  • National Development: Alignment with India's scientific and strategic goals
  • Lifetime Achievement: Sustained record of contributions over the course of a career (Vigyan Ratna)

🏷️ Award Categories

The Vigyan Ratna is the apex of the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar framework, which comprises four categories — the first three honour individuals, while the fourth recognizes teams.

💎

Vigyan Ratna

Highest Honour

  • For lifetime achievements in science, technology, and innovation
  • India's supreme national science award
  • Conferred with a medal, scroll, and ₹25 lakh cash prize
🌟

Vigyan Shri

Distinguished Contributions

  • For distinguished individual contributions to science, technology, or innovation
  • Conferred with a medal, scroll, and ₹10 lakh cash prize
🚀

Vigyan Yuva – Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar

Young Scientists

  • For outstanding contributions by young scientists (typically below 40 years of age)
  • Carries forward the legacy of the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize
  • Conferred with a medal, scroll, and ₹5 lakh cash prize
👥

Vigyan Team

Group Achievement

  • For outstanding collaborative contributions by teams or groups
  • Conferred with a medal, scroll, and ₹50 lakh cash prize to be shared
