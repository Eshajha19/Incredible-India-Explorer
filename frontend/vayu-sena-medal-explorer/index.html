🛩️ Indian Air Force Gallantry Honour

Vayu Sena Medal Explorer

Conferred upon all ranks of the Indian Air Force for such individual acts of exceptional devotion to duty or courage as have special significance for the Air Force.

Instituted: 1960 Awarded By: President of India Equivalent To: Sena Medal · Nao Sena Medal

History & Genesis

🗓️

Instituted in 1960

The Vayu Sena Medal ("Air Force Medal") was instituted on 26 January 1960 by the President's Secretariat Notification No. 18-Pres/60. It was established alongside five other service decorations — the Vishisht Seva Medal series, Sainya Seva Medal, Videsh Seva Medal, Sena Medal (Army) and Nao Sena Medal (Navy) — to create a structured system of recognition for distinguished and gallant service across the three armed forces.

🛫

Purpose & Special Significance

The medal is awarded for "such individual acts of exceptional devotion to duty or courage as have special significance for the Air Force." It is the Indian Air Force's principal commendation, honouring hazardous flying, mid-air emergencies, daring rescues and outstanding devotion to duty that set a benchmark for air warriors.

🤝

A Tri-Service Family of Medals

The Vayu Sena Medal is the air force counterpart of the Sena Medal (Indian Army) and the Nao Sena Medal (Indian Navy). All three share the same institutional framework, hierarchy and award philosophy, ensuring parity in how gallantry and devotion to duty are recognised across the Indian Armed Forces.

Eligibility Criteria

  • All Ranks of the Air Force: Every rank of the Indian Air Force is eligible, from airmen and technicians to flying officers and air marshals.
  • Individual Acts: Awarded specifically for individual acts of exceptional devotion to duty or courage with special significance for the Air Force.
  • Gallantry & Devotion to Duty: Conferred in two streams — Vayu Sena Medal (Gallantry) for acts of bravery and Vayu Sena Medal (Devotion to Duty) for outstanding peacetime service.
  • Posthumous Awards: The medal may be awarded posthumously to personnel who laid down their lives in the line of duty.
  • Repeated Recognition: A bar is authorised for every subsequent award of the medal to the same individual, worn on the ribbon.
  • Monetary Allowance: Gallantry awardees receive a monthly monetary allowance, enhanced with each bar to the medal.
  • Presidential Prerogative: The President of India may cancel and annul an award, and may also restore it subsequently.

Medal Design & Insignia

⭐

Distinctive Star Shape

The medal is a star-shaped decoration of standard silver. Its striking silhouette immediately differentiates the air force honour from the circular Army medal and the pentagonal naval medal of the same family.

🎖️

Obverse: The National Emblem

The obverse bears the State Emblem of India — the Lion Capital of Ashoka — in the centre, encircled by a garland of leaves, symbolising national pride and sovereignty. The mounting is a ring attached to a metal strip ornamented with Ashoka leaves.

🦅

Reverse: Himalayan Eagle

The reverse features the Himalayan Eagle with wings spread, representing the vigilance, reach and soaring spirit of the Indian Air Force, with the inscription "Vayu Sena Medal" in Hindi along the rim.

🎗️

Ribbon & Bars

The riband is 32 mm wide with alternating diagonal stripes of saffron and silver grey, giving the ribbon a distinctive "woven" appearance that echoes the colours of the Indian Air Force. Additional awards are denoted by bars attached to the ribbon.

Air Operations & Acts of Valour

The Vayu Sena Medal has been earned across decades of air power history — from daring wartime missions to hazardous peacetime rescues and humanitarian airlifts. Here are some of the defining missions associated with the award.

⚔️

1965 & 1971 Wars

During the Indo-Pakistani wars of 1965 and 1971, IAF personnel flew relentless strike, reconnaissance and air-supply sorties. Officers and airmen earned the Vayu Sena Medal for exceptional flying skill, damage recovery and devotion to duty under fire.

🗻

Siachen & Operation Meghdoot

From 1984 onwards, IAF helicopters and transport aircraft have sustained the world's highest battlefield at Siachen. Airlifts of troops and supplies at extreme altitudes and in punishing weather have produced many Vayu Sena Medal citations for extraordinary airmanship.

🛡️

IPKF Operations in Sri Lanka

During Operation Pawan (1987–90), IAF helicopter crews flew assault, casualty evacuation and supply missions over the jungles of northern Sri Lanka in support of the Indian Peace Keeping Force — including daring night landings at contested helipads.

🎯

Kargil: Operation Safed Sagar

In the 1999 Kargil War, IAF precision strikes at 18,000 feet — Mirage 2000s delivering laser-guided bombs on enemy positions — demanded outstanding courage and skill. Many air warriors received the Vayu Sena Medal for these high-risk sorties.

🚁

Rescue & Humanitarian Airlifts

IAF crews have repeatedly earned the honour through relief and evacuation operations — flood and earthquake rescues, tsunami relief (2004), Operation Rahat in war-torn Yemen (2015) and the Nepal earthquake relief (2015).

🛰️

Modern Air Defence Era

In recent years the award has recognised personnel for covert operations and air defence missions — including precise strikes, interception of aerial intrusions and operations employing advanced systems such as the S-400 air defence missile system.

Selection Process

Recommendations flow through the air force chain of command, are scrutinised at Air Headquarters, and are finally approved and announced by the President of India. Names of recipients are published in official Air Force Orders and the Gazette of India.

1

Recommendation

Station and squadron commanders put forward personnel for individual acts of exceptional courage or devotion to duty.

2

Vetting & Scrutiny

Citations are examined and validated at Air Headquarters within the Ministry of Defence.

3

Presidential Approval

Gallantry awards are approved by the President and announced on Republic Day (26 Jan) and Independence Day (15 Aug).

4

Publication

Awardee names are published in Air Force Orders and official gazette notifications.

5

Investiture

Medals are presented at investiture ceremonies at Rashtrapati Bhavan or by senior air force authorities in the field.

Timeline of the Vayu Sena Medal

1953

A Landmark Act of Airmanship

Squadron Leader Harchand Singh Gill commands difficult airlanding operations in Assam after an attack on a composite column — an act later recognised with the Vayu Sena Medal and a Bar.

1960

Medal Instituted

President's Secretariat Notification No. 18-Pres/60 (26 January 1960) institutes the Vayu Sena Medal; designs are approved by the President later that year.

1961

First Awards Announced

The first presentations of the Vayu Sena Medal are made from 1960 onwards, with early recipients such as Flight Lieutenant Ian Steele Loughran honoured for gallantry.

1965

Indo-Pak War

During the 1965 war, IAF air warriors earn the medal for outstanding flying, strike missions and devotion to duty under combat conditions.

1971

Liberation War Operations

The 1971 Indo-Pak War sees sustained IAF operations in the east and west; numerous Vayu Sena Medal (Gallantry) citations follow for exceptional airmanship.

1984

Siachen & High-Altitude Era

From Operation Meghdoot onwards, IAF airlift operations at the world's highest battlefield become a recurring source of Vayu Sena Medal awards.

1994

Two Categories Formalised

The award is formally split into Vayu Sena Medal (Gallantry) and Vayu Sena Medal (Devotion to Duty), clarifying the two recognition streams.

1999

Monetary Allowance Introduced

From 1 February 1999, the Government sets a monthly stipend for gallantry awardees of the medal, later enhanced further.

2025

Modern Air Defence Recognition

On Independence Day 2025, 26 Vayu Sena Medals (Gallantry) are awarded for covert operations and air defence missions, including the use of advanced systems like the S-400.

Interesting Facts

🔢

More Than a Thousand Honoured

Since its institution, more than 1,000 distinct recipients — and over 1,400 individual awards — have been conferred with the Vayu Sena Medal, reflecting the IAF's long tradition of recognition.

📐

A Unique Woven Ribbon

The saffron-and-silver-grey diagonal stripes give the ribbon an unmistakable "woven" look — no other Indian gallantry ribbon uses this diagonal pattern.

🦅

The Himalayan Eagle Motif

The Himalayan Eagle on the reverse of the medal is a nod to the IAF's own badge, representing the soaring vigilance and reach of India's air power.

🎖️

A Family of Three

The Vayu Sena Medal, Sena Medal and Nao Sena Medal were all instituted on the same day in 1960 — 26 January — as the tri-service gallantry family.

📜

Published in Gazette

Every award is gazetted by the Government of India, and recipient names are announced twice a year on Republic Day and Independence Day.

💺

From Antarctic Ice to Himalayan Snow

Recipients have been honoured for feats ranging from a one-engine forced landing on the Antarctic ice shelf to precision landings at the world's highest airfields.
