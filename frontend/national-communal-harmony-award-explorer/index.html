📜 History of the National Communal Harmony Award

The National Communal Harmony Award was instituted in 1996 by the National Foundation for Communal Harmony (NFCH), an autonomous organization set up under the Union Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. It represents the nation's tribute to individuals and organizations working selflessly to strengthen the fabric of unity in diversity.

Genesis and Inception

Following historical challenges to social harmony in the early 1990s, the Government of India established the National Foundation for Communal Harmony in 1992. In 1996, the Foundation launched this national award scheme to publicly acknowledge and encourage outstanding voluntary efforts aimed at preventing communal conflicts, spreading the message of peace, and promoting national integration.

Evolution of the Honour

Over nearly three decades, the award has become India's benchmark recognition for peacebuilding. It honours courageous field workers, social reformers, educators, and civil society institutions that stand against violence, foster interfaith dialogue, and assist child victims of communal and caste strife.

🕊️ NFCH Core Mission

"To promote communal harmony, strengthen national integration, and assist in the physical and psychological rehabilitation of child victims of communal, caste, ethnic, or terrorist violence."

🎯 Purpose and Objectives

The primary purpose of the National Communal Harmony Award is to recognize and encourage outstanding efforts that foster peace, mutual trust, and goodwill among diverse religious, linguistic, and cultural communities across India.

🤝

Promoting Brotherhood

Celebrating grassroots initiatives that build lasting inter-community trust, tolerance, and peaceful coexistence.

🕊️

Conflict Resolution

Honouring individuals and groups who actively intervene during communal tensions to de-escalate violence and restore normalcy.

🌱

Child Rehabilitation

Supporting and highlighting efforts that provide care, education, and holistic recovery to children affected by communal or terrorist violence.

🇮🇳

National Integration

Strengthening the secular and democratic ideals enshrined in the Constitution of India through active civic engagement.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

The National Communal Harmony Award is open to all Indian citizens and organizations dedicated to peacebuilding and communal unity. The criteria are structured as follows:

👤 Individual Category

  • Open to all Indian citizens without distinction of religion, caste, gender, or region.
  • Requires a minimum of 10 years of sustained, outstanding service in promoting communal harmony and national integration.
  • Demonstrated courage in preventing riots or protecting vulnerable communities during conflicts.

🏛️ Organization Category

  • Open to non-governmental organizations (NGOs), trusts, and voluntary institutions registered in India.
  • Requires at least 5 years of active, verified work in peacebuilding, interfaith dialogue, or victim rehabilitation.
  • Must maintain a non-sectarian, non-political, and non-discriminatory character.

🚫 Disqualifications

  • Government officials and active political office-bearers are not eligible in their official capacity.
  • Organizations advocating communal, casteist, or extremist ideologies are strictly barred.
  • Self-nominations without verified third-party endorsements are not considered.

⚖️ Selection Process and National Jury

The selection process for the National Communal Harmony Award is renowned for its high integrity, transparency, and multi-tier evaluation system overseen by a statutory National Jury.

1

Nationwide Nominations

Nominations are invited from State Governments, Union Territory Administrations, Central Ministries, Vice Chancellors, eminent awardees, and public figures.

2

Scrutiny & Field Verification

An executive screening committee verifies the work, credentials, and impact of nominated individuals and institutions with local reports and independent audits.

3

Jury Deliberation

The high-level National Jury, chaired by the Vice President of India, evaluates shortlisted candidates and decides the winners based on merit and impact.

4

Conferment by the President

The awards are formally presented by the President of India at a ceremony held at Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi.

👑 High-Level National Jury Composition

  • Chairman: Vice President of India
  • Member: Prime Minister's Nominee
  • Member: Union Home Minister
  • Member: Leader of the Opposition (Lok Sabha)
  • Members: Two eminent public personalities nominated by the Chairman

💡 Interesting Facts & Key Highlights

🕊️

NFCH Campaign Week

Every year from November 19 to 25, NFCH observes Communal Harmony Campaign Week and Flag Day across schools and colleges in India.

👑

Chaired by Vice President

The Selection Jury is statically chaired by the Vice President of India, making it one of the most prestigious jury panels in the country.

👧

Rehabilitation Focus

Proceeds collected by NFCH during Communal Harmony Week directly support the education and healthcare of children orphaned in communal riots.

📜

No Bar on Posthumous Awards

The award can be conferred posthumously in extraordinary cases where individuals sacrificed their lives to prevent violence.

🌟

Grassroots Heroes

Unlike standard political honours, it actively seeks out unsung field workers operating in sensitive conflict zones.

🇮🇳

Unified Creed

Promotes the fundamental duty under Article 51A(e) of the Indian Constitution to promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood.
