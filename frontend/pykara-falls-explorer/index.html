🌲 Tamil Nadu · Nilgiri Hills

Pykara Falls Explorer

Sacred to the Toda people, the Pykara river rushes off the Nilgiri plateau's edge near Ooty in two dramatic cascades — 55 and 61 metres tall — after a journey through pine forest, grassy downs and a hydroelectric reservoir.

📍 Location & River

The Pykara River's Journey

Pykara Falls lies about 19–21 km from Ooty on the Ooty–Mysore road, in the Nilgiris district of Tamil Nadu. The Pykara river — the largest river in the Nilgiris and considered sacred by the indigenous Toda people — rises at Mukurthi Peak, flows north through Mukurthi National Park, then bends west at the plateau's edge, passing through the Pykara Dam and reservoir before dropping over the falls in a series of cascades.

  • Location: Pykara, Nilgiris district, Tamil Nadu (~19-21 km from Ooty)
  • River: Pykara River, rising at Mukurthi Peak
  • River system: Pykara → Moyar → Bhavani → Kaveri
  • Height: Two main falls of ~55 m (180 ft) and ~61 m (200 ft)
  • Structure: A series of cascades over the plateau edge; the last two are named Pykara Falls
  • Best season: August–January, for full flow and pleasant weather

🌦️ Seasons

The Falls Across the Year

Flow is regulated in part by the Pykara Dam upstream, but the river still responds strongly to the monsoon, making the falls noticeably fuller from August through January.

🏞️ Gallery

Nilgiri Landscape & River

The Pykara valley is ringed by shola forest, eucalyptus groves and the rolling grassy downs of Wenlock Downs, with Toda settlements scattered across the plateau.

Getting There

Pykara is reached by road from Ooty along the Ooty–Mysore highway, with the falls viewpoint a short, easy walk from the parking area.

Travel Notes

  • Best season: August–January for full flow and clear weather
  • Distance: ~19-21 km from Ooty; falls are ~2 km from Pykara Lake
  • Access: Short 5–10 minute walk on a paved path from the parking area to the viewpoint
  • Nearest railhead: Ooty (Udhagamandalam) Railway Station · Nearest airport: Coimbatore
