Kurmali (कुड़माली / কুড়মালী)

Discover Kurmali, a vibrant Indo-Aryan language spoken primarily in the Chota Nagpur plateau region across Jharkhand, West Bengal, and Odisha. Known for its rich oral traditions and Jhumar folk songs.

📍 Region: Eastern India
🗣️ Family: Indo-European
✍️ Scripts: Devanagari, Bengali, Odia

Cultural Heritage

Kurmali is deeply intertwined with the agrarian lifestyle of the Kudmi Mahato community and other regional groups. The language boasts a massive repertoire of oral literature, particularly the Jhumar (झूमर) folk songs sung during agricultural festivals like Karam and Tusu. Despite historical marginalization, Kurmali is experiencing a cultural renaissance today through literature and digital media in eastern India.
