Kudle: Gokarna's Crescent

A serene crescent-shaped beach where yoga, spirituality, and pristine nature meet along Karnataka's sacred coastline.

Gokarna's Hidden Crescent

Kudle Beach is one of the five pristine beaches of Gokarna in Karnataka's Uttara Kannada district. Shaped like a perfect crescent, it offers a quieter, more meditative alternative to the popular Om Beach, attracting yoga practitioners, spiritual seekers, and travelers seeking tranquility.

Geographic & Spiritual Location

Coordinates: 14.55°N, 74.32°E
District: Uttara Kannada, Karnataka
Coastline: Karavali Coast, Arabian Sea
Nearest Town: Gokarna (2 km), Kumta (25 km)
Nearest Airport: Dabolim, Goa (140 km) / Mangaluru (180 km)
Best Season: October to March (pleasant weather, calm seas)

The beach is part of the sacred Gokarna coastline, a region revered in Hindu tradition as one of the seven Mukti Sthalas (places of liberation). Lord Shiva is believed to have manifested here as the Atmalinga, making the entire region spiritually significant.

Natural Setting

Wellness & Spiritual Culture

Kudle has emerged as one of India's leading yoga and wellness destinations, with numerous shalas (schools), retreat centers, and meditation spaces dotting the cliff-side overlooking the beach.
