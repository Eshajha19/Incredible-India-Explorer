मगही Magahi Language of the Magadha Region

Descended from Magadhi Prakrit, the vernacular once spoken across the ancient kingdom that gave rise to the Maurya and Gupta Empires, Magahi is still living, spoken language today across south-central Bihar and Jharkhand.
