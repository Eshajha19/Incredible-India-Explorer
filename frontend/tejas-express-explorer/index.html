Tejas Express

India's Premium Semi-High Speed Fully Air-Conditioned Train

Discover More

Introduction

Tejas Express is India's first semi-high speed fully air-conditioned train introduced by Indian Railways. It features modern onboard facilities like aircraft-style personalized LCD entertainment screens, Wi-Fi, modular bio-toilets, and comfortable seating arrangements, marking a leap in India's passenger train experience.

History & Development

2016

Announcement

The Tejas Express was announced in the 2016 Railway Budget as a new premier train service operating at speeds up to 130 km/h (capable of 200 km/h).

May 2017

Inaugural Run

The first Tejas Express was flagged off on May 24, 2017, operating between Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (Mumbai) and Karmali (Goa).

Oct 2019

IRCTC Operations

The Lucknow-New Delhi Tejas Express became the first train in India to be fully operated by private operators (IRCTC), setting a new precedent for Indian Railways.

Passenger Experience

Entertainment

Personalized LCD entertainment screens in Executive Class, offering movies, music, and passenger information systems.

Seating

Ergonomic seating with comfortable armrests, leg rests (in Executive Class), and reading lights for every passenger.

Connectivity

Onboard Wi-Fi and multiple charging/USB ports per seat ensure passengers stay connected throughout the journey.

Catering

High-quality catering services with vending machines for tea and coffee, and modular pantry equipment.

Automatic Doors

Centralized automatic sliding doors for passenger safety, controlled by the guard panel.

Modern Toilets

Modular bio-toilets with sensor-based taps, hand dryers, and a water level indicator.

Interesting Facts

  • Speed: Tejas Express is designed to operate at a maximum speed of 200 km/h, though commercial operations are restricted to 130 km/h depending on track conditions.
  • Safety First: Features include smoke/fire detection and extinguishing systems, and CCTV surveillance.
  • Call Buttons: Similar to aircraft, passengers have attendant call buttons at every seat.
  • Blind Mechanisms: Automatic Venetian blinds on windows can be rolled up or down easily.
