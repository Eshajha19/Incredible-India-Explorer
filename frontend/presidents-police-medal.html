🎖️ Sovereign Police Honour

President's Police Medal (PPM)

India's premier decoration awarded for distinguished service and gallantry to police personnel across State Forces, Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), and Central Intelligence Organizations.

1951 Instituted
GoI / MHA Conferred By
Bi-Annual Republic & Independence Day

⏳ History & Genesis

Instituted on March 1, 1951 by the President of India, the President's Police Medal replaced the pre-independence King's Police and Fire Service Medal. It was formally established to recognize supreme sacrifice, conspicuous bravery, and unblemished distinguished service by police personnel across the nation.

Originally designated as the President's Police and Fire Service Medal, it was reorganized in 1973/1975 into separate dedicated honours for police, fire services, correctional services, and home guards. In January 2024, the Ministry of Home Affairs rationalized the medal structure into the President's Medal for Distinguished Service (PMDS) and President's Medal for Gallantry (PMG).

🏅 Award Categories

The President's Police Medal is conferred under two distinct categories recognizing distinct facets of law enforcement excellence:

Gallantry Category

President's Police Medal for Gallantry (PPMG / PMG)

Awarded for conspicuous gallantry and valor in saving lives, protecting property, countering terrorism, or subduing armed criminals under extreme personal risk.

  • Open to all ranks across state police and CAPFs.
  • Awarded posthumously in cases of supreme sacrifice.
  • Carries a permanent monthly monetary allowance from MHA.
Distinguished Service Category

President's Police Medal for Distinguished Service (PPMDS / PMDS)

Awarded for distinguished service of a high order, marked by sustained excellence, exceptional leadership, integrity, and long-standing devotion to duty.

  • Requires at least 20 to 25 years of clean service record.
  • Requires prior award of the Police Medal for Meritorious Service.
  • Conferred annually to senior & frontline officers of proven merit.

📜 Eligibility Criteria

Personnel serving across all statutory law enforcement agencies and armed police forces in India are eligible for consideration:

Eligible Police Forces & Organizations

  • State Police Forces: All State Police Cadres across all 28 States.
  • Union Territory Police: Delhi Police, Jammu & Kashmir Police, Puducherry Police, etc.
  • Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs): BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB.
  • Central Intelligence & Crime Organizations: Intelligence Bureau (IB), Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), R&AW, National Investigation Agency (NIA), Railway Protection Force (RPF).

Key Selection Criteria

  • Gallantry: Immediate act of valor in anti-terror ops, riot control, high-seas rescue, or hostage liberation. No length of service limitation.
  • Distinguished Service: Minimum 20+ years of outstanding police service, impeccable integrity, vigilance clearance, and exemplary confidential reports.

🎨 Medal Design & Insignia

The President's Police Medal features intricate bullion craftsmanship and official insignia:

🛡️ Medal Obverse

Circular silver medallion (38mm diameter). Features the State Emblem of India (Ashoka Lion Capital with Satyameva Jayate) surrounded by the embossed inscription "PRESIDENT'S POLICE MEDAL".

🌿 Medal Reverse

Bears two stylized lotus wreaths surrounding a central rectangular plaque inscribed with "FOR DISTINGUISHED SERVICE" or "FOR GALLANTRY", topped by a five-pointed star.

🎗️ Ribbon & Clasp Bar

Suspended by a 35mm silk ribbon featuring equal vertical stripes of dark navy blue, silver-white, and deep saffron red. Subsequent awards are denoted by silver bars attached to the ribbon bar.

📋 Selection Process Workflow

Selection for the President's Police Medal follows a rigorous multi-tier evaluation system:

1
Nomination
Initiated by State DGPs, UT Chiefs, or CAPF Directors General.
2
Screening
Departmental Screening Committee reviews service dossiers & citations.
3
MHA Review
Ministry of Home Affairs verifies integrity & vigilance clearance.
4
Honours Committee
Central Honours & Awards Committee conducts final selection.
5
President Approval
President of India approves the final list of awardees.
6
Investiture
Announced on Republic/Independence Day & conferred at Rashtrapati Bhavan.

📅 Historical Timeline

Key milestones in the evolution of the President's Police Medal:

1951

Medal Institution

President's Police and Fire Service Medal formally instituted on March 1, 1951, replacing the British-era King's Police Medal.

1973 - 1975

Separation & Restructuring

Medal separated into specialized honours: President's Police Medal (PPM), President's Fire Services Medal, and President's Home Guards Medal.

1985

Expansion to CAPFs

Formally standardized quota allocations across newly expanding Central Armed Police Forces (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB).

2008

26/11 Heroes Recognized

Special investiture ceremonies conferring posthumous President's Police Medals for Gallantry to Mumbai 26/11 martyrs.

2024

Rationalization & Modern Nomenclature

MHA updated classification under the President's Medal for Distinguished Service (PMDS) and President's Medal for Gallantry (PMG).

💡 Interesting Facts & Trivia

🗓️

Bi-Annual Announcement: Announced twice every year—on Republic Day (January 26) and Independence Day (August 15).

💵

Monetary Stipend: Gallantry awardees (PPMG) receive a lifelong monthly cash allowance from the Central Government.

🏅

Order of Precedence: Takes precedence over the standard Police Medal (PM) in official uniform wear regulations.

👔

Uniform Ribbons: Awardees wear ribbon bars on daily duty uniforms and miniature medals on formal ceremonial dress.
