Mountain Overview

The Four-Pillared Summit of Garhwal

A grand multi-peaked mountain massif spanning from Gangotri to Badrinath.

🏔️ Geography & Glacial Source

Chaukhamba I is situated at the head of the main Gangotri Glacier in Chamoli/Rudraprayag districts. Its western snow basins feed the Bhagirathi River (Ganga), while its eastern faces drop steeply into the Satopanth Tal glacial basin.

🏛️ Meaning of "Chaukhamba"

The name literally means "Four Pillars" in Sanskrit/Hindi. The massif forms a continuous ridge featuring four summits, with Chaukhamba I (7,138 m) as the principal and highest peak.

📜 Mountaineering Heritage

First summitted on June 13, 1952 by French climbers Lucien George and Victor Russenberger via the northeast ridge. The peak presents technical ice walls, avalanche slopes, and crevassed glacier travel.
