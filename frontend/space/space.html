INCOSPAR Formed by Dr. Vikram Sarabhai

The Indian National Committee for Space Research (INCOSPAR) was established under the Department of Atomic Energy, headed by pioneer scientist Dr. Vikram Sarabhai, setting the foundation for India's civilian space vision.
