🌿 Meghalaya · East Khasi Hills

Nohsngithiang Falls

Witness the awe-inspiring Seven Sisters Falls. Plunging 315 metres over the limestone cliffs of the Khasi Hills, it transforms into a breathtaking spectacle during the monsoon.

📍 Scale & Setting

A 315-Metre Plunge

Located near Mawsmai village in Cherrapunji (Sohra), the falls owe their existence entirely to the heavy regional rainfall.

  • Height: 315 meters (1,033 feet) - India's 4th tallest
  • Source: Monsoon Rainfall
  • Setting: Limestone Cliffs of the Khasi Hills
  • Best time to visit: June to September
  • Nearest Town: Cherrapunji / Shillong

🌦️ Seasons

A Tale of Two Seasons

Because Nohsngithiang Falls is fed entirely by rainwater, its appearance undergoes an extreme transformation between the monsoon and winter months.

Monsoon (Jun-Sep)

The Seven Sisters Awaken

During the torrential monsoon rains that Cherrapunji is famous for, the cliffs come alive. Seven distinct, powerful streams plunge over the edge, creating a thundering spectacle against the vibrant green gorges.

Winter (Dec-Feb)

The Silent Cliffs

Without the monsoon rains to feed them, the falls dry up completely. The towering limestone cliffs stand stark and bare, offering a striking, rugged view of the East Khasi Hills' dramatic geology.

🗺️ Map

Getting There

The falls are located just 1 kilometre south of Mawsmai village, making them easily accessible for tourists visiting Cherrapunji.

Travel Notes

  • Accessibility: The viewpoint is right by the road, making it accessible for everyone without the need for trekking.
  • Best Views: Sunset is particularly stunning as the falls are illuminated by the setting sun, creating a magical atmosphere.
  • Nearby Attractions: The famous Mawsmai Cave is located very close by, along with the Eco Park.
