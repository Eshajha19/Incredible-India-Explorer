Fort overview

A Maratha hill fort remembered for courage

Sinhagad Fort is celebrated for the legendary Battle of Sinhagad and the bravery of Tanaji Malusare. This explorer connects its older Kondhana identity, Maratha history, architecture and trekking route.

Battle memory

The fort's most powerful story is the daring Maratha recapture and Tanaji Malusare's sacrifice, which transformed Sinhagad into a symbol of valour.

Hill-fort setting

The elevated Sahyadri location made the fort useful for defence, surveillance and regional control.

Heritage trek

Visitors experience history through gates, ramparts, memorials, stone paths and viewpoints.
