Cosco: Sporting Excellence

India's trusted sporting goods brand empowering athletes and sports enthusiasts since 1935.

The Origin Story

🏏

1935: Foundation

Cosco was founded in Delhi with a vision to provide quality, affordable sporting goods to Indian athletes. Starting as a small sports equipment supplier, it quickly gained trust among schools and clubs.

🏭

Manufacturing Excellence

By the 1950s, Cosco established its own manufacturing facilities, producing cricket bats, hockey sticks, and sports accessories that met international quality standards.

🏆

Sports Legacy

Cosco equipment has been used by countless Indian athletes in national and international competitions, contributing to India's sporting achievements.

Major Products & Innovations

1960s

Cricket Bat Revolution

Introduced English Willow cricket bats that matched international quality, making professional-grade equipment accessible to Indian cricketers.

1980s

Hockey Stick Innovation

Developed lightweight composite hockey sticks that improved player performance and became standard in Indian hockey academies.

2000s

Table Tennis Equipment

Launched professional-grade table tennis rackets and accessories, supporting India's growing table tennis community.

2020s

Modern Sports Gear

Expanded into fitness equipment, sports apparel, and technology-integrated sports accessories for the modern athlete.

Indian Sports Connection

🏫

School & College Sports

Cosco is the preferred brand for educational institutions across India, supplying equipment for school sports programs and inter-college tournaments.

🏟️

Professional Sponsorships

Sponsors various state-level and national tournaments, providing equipment and supporting grassroots sports development.

🎯

Athlete Partnerships

Collaborates with professional athletes for product testing and feedback, ensuring equipment meets competitive standards.

Brand Development Journey

Cosco's journey from a local sports supplier to a national brand reflects India's growing sports culture. The company has consistently invested in R&D, quality control, and athlete feedback to maintain its position as a trusted sporting goods manufacturer.
