Manas National Park Explorer

Step into a pristine Eastern Himalayan sanctuary — holding five prestigious conservation statuses and serving as the exclusive natural habitat for the endemic Golden Langur and the world's smallest wild pig, the Pygmy Hog.

Five Protection Designations

5-Fold Conservation Protection

Manas is uniquely endowed with five official conservation designations protecting its rich riverine and grassland biodiversity.

Transboundary Hydrology

The Manas River & Alluvial Plains

Flowing down from the high peaks of Bhutan, the Manas River shapes the sanctuary's landscape before joining the Brahmaputra.

Transboundary Waterway

Known as Drangme Chhu in Bhutan, the Manas River flows south across the international boundary into Assam, creating a seamless ecological corridor shared between India and Bhutan.

🚣 Eco-Rafting: Visitors can enjoy scenic boat rafting along the crystal-clear river waters from Mathanguri Lodge to Bansbari.

River Split into Manas & Beki

As the river hits the flat plains at Mathanguri, it splits into two primary channels — the main Manas River and the Beki River — depositing fertile silt that sustains extensive savannah grass beds.

🌾 Grassland Ecosystem: Supports wild water buffaloes, rhinos, swamp deer, and the endangered Bengal Florican.
