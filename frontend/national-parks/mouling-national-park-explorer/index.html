Mouling National Park Explorer

Journey into the Eastern Himalayas of Arunachal Pradesh — a rugged, largely unexplored biodiversity hotspot along the Siang River, home to the Red Panda, Clouded Leopard, and over 230 species of Himalayan birdlife.

Birdlife & Rivers

Mouling's dramatic elevation gradient and river systems make it one of the richest and least-surveyed corners of the Eastern Himalayas.

🦜 Birdlife

🔭 230+ Species: One of the least-surveyed birding frontiers in the Eastern Himalayas.

🌊 Rivers

💧 Siang River: The Indian stretch of the Yarlung Tsangpo/Brahmaputra system.

Wildlife of Mouling
