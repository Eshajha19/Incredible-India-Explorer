Valley of Flowers National Park Explorer

Step into the legendary alpine valley of the Garhwal Himalaya where over 600 species of flowers carpet the meadows each monsoon — a UNESCO World Heritage Site of breathtaking Himalayan biodiversity, rare plants, and wildlife.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

About the Valley of Flowers

A valley carpeted in alpine blooms, hidden in the Bhyundar Gorge of the Garhwal Himalayas.

The Seasonal Bloom

Buried under snow for most of the year, the valley awakens after the monsoon breaks, when the Pushpavati river swells and the meadows burst into a vivid carpet of Himalayan Blue Poppies, Brahma Kamal, cobra lilies, primulas, and anemones. The brief bloom — from mid-June to September — draws trekkers from around the world to witness one of nature's most spectacular displays.

🥾 Visiting Window: The park opens only between June and September; a permit is required and overnight camping inside the valley is prohibited.
