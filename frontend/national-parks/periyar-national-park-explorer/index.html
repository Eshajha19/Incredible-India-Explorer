📍 Thekkady, Kerala 🌊 26 km² Periyar Lake 🐘 Project Elephant Reserve

Periyar National Park Explorer

Step into Kerala's premier wildlife sanctuary high in the Cardamom Hills — home to wild Asian Elephant herds, scenic boat safaris across Periyar Lake, dense evergreen spice forests, and thriving biodiversity.

Aquatic Lifeline

Periyar Lake & Boat Safaris

Formed by the Mullaperiyar Dam in 1895, the 26 km² artificial lake offers India's unique boat safari experience for wildlife observation.

The Aquatic Wildlife Haven

Periyar Lake winds through dense forest hills, creating scenic submerged tree snags where cormorants, darters, and fish eagles roost. During dry seasons, herds of wild elephants, gaurs, sambar deer, and wild boars assemble along the lake banks to drink and bathe.

🚢 KTDC Boat Safaris: Scheduled cruises depart daily from Kumily boat jetty with mandatory safety gear.
Rich Biodiversity

Fauna & Wildlife Protection

Periyar safeguards over 1,000 Asian Elephants, 40+ Bengal Tigers, rare endemic primates, and 320+ species of birds.

Aromatic Canopy

Cardamom Hills & Spice Forests

Thekkady is India's spice capital — producing cardamom, black pepper, cinnamon, nutmeg, and organic tea estates.

🌱 Green Cardamom

Thrives under shady evergreen forest canopy, yielding aromatic green pods celebrated globally.

🖤 Malabar Black Pepper

Known as "Black Gold", harvested from climbing vines entwined around native forest trees.

☕ Tea & Coffee Estates

Rolling tea gardens around Kumily and Vandiperiyar offering scenic plantation walks and tasting tours.
