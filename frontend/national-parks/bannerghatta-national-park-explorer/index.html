Bannerghatta National Park Explorer

Located on the outskirts of Bengaluru, Bannerghatta is a unique biological park offering grand safaris, India's first Butterfly Park, and a dedicated wildlife rescue centre, all within a vital elephant corridor.

Wildlife of Bannerghatta
