Bhitarkanika National Park Explorer

Step into the intangible mangrove maze of eastern India — the world's largest refuge of saltwater crocodiles, a Ramsar-listed wetland of rivers, creeks, and lush mangroves teeming with 240+ bird species and wildlife.

Ramsar Wetland & National Park

About Bhitarkanika

A Ramsar-listed mangrove wilderness where the Brahmani, Baitarani, and Dhamra rivers meet the Bay of Bengal.

A Kingdom of Mangroves & Tidal Creeks

Declared a national park in 1998 and designated a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance in 2002, Bhitarkanika shelters one of India's largest mangrove tracts and the world's most populous estuarine crocodile population. Its labyrinth of tidal creeks, mudflats, and dense mangrove forests nurtures a staggering web of aquatic and terrestrial life all the way to the legendary Gahirmatha marine sanctuary on the coast.

🕊️ Visiting: The park opens (subject to monsoon) from late September to June; boat permits are required from Forest Department at Khola and Guptiis.
