Park Overview

History

Declared a national park in 1978, Mollem is part of the larger Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary. It has protected the region's ecological balance for decades.

Western Ghats

Located in the Western Ghats, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the world's eight hottest biodiversity hotspots, featuring ancient geological formations.

Dudhsagar Falls

The majestic four-tiered Dudhsagar Falls, meaning "Sea of Milk", is one of India's tallest waterfalls, located within the park's boundaries.

Conservation Efforts

Mollem National Park is actively protected against deforestation, illegal mining, and human encroachment. Community-led eco-tourism and strict forest department patrols ensure the preservation of this vital Western Ghats ecosystem.
