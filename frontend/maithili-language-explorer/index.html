Language Origins & Script History

Script Heritage: Tirhuta & Devanagari

Maithili belongs to the Eastern zone of the Indo-Aryan language family (Bihari group), descending directly from Magadhi Prakrit. In 2003, via the 92nd Constitutional Amendment Act, Maithili was included in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution as an official language of India.

Historical Script Information:

  • Tirhuta (Mithilakshar / 𑒧𑒱𑒟𑒱𑒪𑒰𑒏𑓂𑒭𑒩): An ancient Brahmic script dating back to the 10th century CE, historically used for writing Sanskrit treatises, royal decrees, genealogy records (Panji Prabandha), and Maithili poetry.
  • Devanagari Script (वर्तमान लिपि): In contemporary times, Devanagari is universally adopted for administration, primary and higher education, mass media, and literature.
  • Kaithi Script: Historically also written in Kaithi for informal accounting and administrative record-keeping across Bihar.

Literary & Cultural Grandeur

Mithila's Rich Literary Heritage

From 14th-century Vaishnava lyricism to folk visual arts recognized globally.

📜

Mahakavi Vidyapati (मैथिल कोकिल)

Revered as the Nightingale of Mithila (Maithil Kokil), Vidyapati (c. 1352–1448) composed sublime devotional lyrics depicting the divine love of Radha and Krishna, reshaping Eastern Indian literature including Bengali, Odia, and Assamese.

🎨

Madhubani (Mithila) Art

An internationally celebrated GI-tagged folk art form originating in the Mithila region, traditionally created by women using natural pigments, twig pens, and intricate geometric patterns on walls and handmade paper.

📖

Varna Ratnakara & Panji Prabandha

Composed by Jyotirishwar Thakur in 1324 CE, the Varna Ratnakara is the oldest known prose text in any Indo-Aryan vernacular. Mithila also maintains the unmatched 700-year-old Panji Prabandha genealogical register.
