Ecosystem

Mangrove Forests

A unique intertidal ecosystem where land meets sea.

Pichavaram is characterized by its dense canopy of mangrove trees, primarily Avicennia and Rhizophora species. These trees have adapted to survive in saline, waterlogged conditions with specialized root systems.

The forest is interspersed with 51 intricate water channels, creating a labyrinthine network of backwaters that is both ecologically rich and visually stunning.

🌳

Nature's Coastal Shield

Mangroves act as a natural barrier against cyclones, tsunamis, and coastal erosion, protecting inland communities.

Experience

Boat Rides

Explore the mangroves from the water.

The best way to experience Pichavaram is by coracle or rowboat. Guided boat rides take visitors through the narrow, winding channels, offering close-up views of the mangrove roots, birdlife, and occasional sightings of otters and monitor lizards.

Eco-friendly boating practices are enforced to minimize disturbance to the delicate ecosystem and ensure the sustainability of tourism.

Protection

Conservation

Safeguarding the green shield of the Coromandel Coast.

Conservation efforts focus on preventing illegal logging, controlling pollution from the Vellar and Coleroon rivers, and promoting community-based ecotourism. The Tamil Nadu Forest Department actively monitors the health of the mangrove cover.
