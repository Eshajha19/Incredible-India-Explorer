Kaziranga National Park Explorer

Immerse yourself in India's most spectacular wildlife sanctuary — a UNESCO World Heritage Site on the floodplains of the Brahmaputra. Home to two-thirds of the world's Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceroses, dense tiger populations, wild water buffalo herds, and over 500 species of migratory and resident birds.

Flagship Species

The Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros

Kaziranga is the last great stronghold of Rhinoceros unicornis. From fewer than 20 individuals in 1908 to over 2,600 today — this is one of the most remarkable conservation success stories in the world.

🦏 Rhinoceros unicornis

Kaziranga's rhino population is the single largest wild population of the One-Horned Rhinoceros anywhere on Earth. These gentle giants spend their days grazing in the tall grasslands and wallowing in muddy beels. Their thick grey hide is folded into protective plating, and their single horn — made of keratin, like human hair and nails — can grow up to 57 cm long.

🧬 Conservation Success: From the brink of extinction at 12 individuals in the early 1900s, Kaziranga's rhino population has recovered to over 2,600 — protected by a dedicated force of 1,800 forest guards and 165 anti-poaching camps.
Floodplain Ecosystem

Wetlands & Brahmaputra Floodplain Ecology

The annual monsoon floods of the Brahmaputra River shape Kaziranga's dynamic landscape, creating a mosaic of tall grasslands, wetlands, and forests that support extraordinary biodiversity.

🌾 Tall Wet Grasslands

Dominant grass species: Phragmites karka, Saccharum spontaneum — growing 4–6 meters tall. Primary rhino habitat and tiger ambush cover.

🌱 Short Grasslands

Cynodon dactylon, Imperata cylindrica — 0.5–1 meter. Preferred grazing areas for swamp deer and water buffalo.

💧 Wetland Beels (Lakes)

Over 100 seasonal and perennial beels dot the park. Critical dry-season refuges for fish spawning, waterfowl congregations, and megafauna drinking water.

🌊 Annual Flood Cycle: The Brahmaputra overflows every monsoon, inundating up to 70% of the park. These floods deposit nutrient-rich silt, recharge aquifers, and reset the grassland succession — essential for rhino habitat.
Rich Biodiversity

Wildlife of Kaziranga

From the iconic One-Horned Rhino to the Bengal Tiger, Wild Water Buffalo, Swamp Deer, and the Ganges River Dolphin — Kaziranga protects an extraordinary concentration of endangered species.

Avian Diversity

Bird Species of Kaziranga

With over 500 recorded species, Kaziranga is a birdwatcher's paradise — hosting endangered Greater Adjutant Storks, Bengal Floricans, Spot-billed Pelicans, and thousands of migratory waterfowl from Central Asia.

Conservation Milestones

Kaziranga Conservation Timeline

From a hunting reserve in the 1900s to one of the world's most successful rhino conservation stories — tracing the history of protection that saved the One-Horned Rhinoceros from extinction.
