🏔️ Language & Heritage

कश्मीरी Kashmiri

Discover the ancient language, rich literature, and cultural heritage of Kashmir — the paradise on earth.

📜

Language Classification

Family: Indo-Aryan (Dardic subgroup)

Region: Jammu & Kashmir

Scripts: Devanagari, Perso-Arabic (Nastaliq), Sharada (historical)

Speakers: ~7 million+

Status: Official language of Jammu & Kashmir

📖

Literary Heritage

Poetry: Lal Ded, Nund Rishi, Habba Khatoon

Literature: Kashmiri poetry, folk tales, Sufi poetry

Folk Songs: Wanwun, Lol, Chhakri

Classical: Sufiana Kalam (Kashmiri Sufi music)

Influences: Sanskrit, Persian, Urdu, Sanskrit

✍️ Writing Systems

Major Writing Systems

Kashmiri is written in multiple scripts, each reflecting its diverse cultural influences.

🔤

Devanagari Script

Official script used in government and education. Includes all Kashmiri sounds with added diacritics for unique phonemes.

🕌

Perso-Arabic Script (Nastaliq)

Commonly used in cultural and religious contexts. Features the beautiful Nastaliq calligraphic style.

Common Kashmiri Greeting

"आदाब" / "اسلام علیکم" Ādāb / Assalamu Alaikum "Hello / Peace be upon you"

The greeting "Ādāb" is a respectful salutation widely used in Kashmiri culture, while "Assalamu Alaikum" is common in Kashmiri Muslim communities.

📚 Literary Heritage

Literature & Culture

Explore the rich literary traditions and cultural contributions of Kashmir.

🎵

Sufi Poetry

Kashmiri Sufi poetry, particularly the works of Lal Ded and Nund Rishi, blends spirituality with folk traditions. Their verses are still recited and revered today.

📝

Folk Literature

Rich oral traditions include folk tales, ballads, and songs that preserve Kashmir's cultural memory and values through generations.

🎭

Performing Arts

Kashmiri performing arts include the soulful Sufiana Kalam music, the energetic Rouff dance, and the traditional Bhand Pather folk theater.

🏛️

Modern Literature

Contemporary Kashmiri literature has produced acclaimed poets, novelists, and short story writers exploring themes of identity, resistance, and longing.

📍 Regional Information

Region Jammu & Kashmir
Major Cities Srinagar, Jammu, Anantnag, Baramulla
Cultural Centers Srinagar (Kashmir Valley), Jammu (Dogri culture)
Literary Hubs Srinagar (Kashmiri literature), Jammu (Dogra literature)

Kashmiri is the official language of Jammu & Kashmir, spoken across the Kashmir Valley and in Kashmiri communities worldwide.
