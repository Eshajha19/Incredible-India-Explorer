The Dawn of Human Art & Architecture

Prehistoric Rock Art 🎨

India is home to some of the oldest rock art in the world, dating back to the Paleolithic era. Sites like Bhimbetka offer a glimpse into the daily lives, hunts, and dances of early humans.

Rock-Cut Architecture 🏛️

Instead of building up, ancient Indian artisans carved massive temples and monasteries directly into mountainsides. This tradition, spanning Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain faiths, peaked at Ajanta and Ellora.

Conservation 🛡️

Preserving these monuments from natural erosion and human impact is a monumental task. The ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) employs structural supports and chemical treatments to save them for future generations.
