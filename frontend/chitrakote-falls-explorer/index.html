🌾 Chhattisgarh · Bastar

Chitrakote Falls Explorer

Often called the "Niagara of India," Chitrakote Falls spreads the Indravati River into a nearly 300-metre-wide horseshoe curtain — India's widest waterfall. See how it transforms between monsoon flood and dry-season calm.

📍 Location & River

On the Indravati, deep in Bastar

Chitrakote Falls sits about 38 km west of Jagdalpur in the Bastar district of Chhattisgarh, roughly 273 km from the state capital Raipur. The Indravati River — which rises in Odisha's Kalahandi district and eventually joins the Godavari — plunges over a rock escarpment here, curving into the wide horseshoe shape that gives the falls their fame.

  • Location: Bastar district, Chhattisgarh (38 km from Jagdalpur)
  • River: Indravati River (tributary of the Godavari)
  • Height: ~29 m (95 ft)
  • Width: Up to ~300 m (980 ft) at monsoon peak — India's widest waterfall
  • Shape: Horseshoe cataract, 3 to 7 streams depending on flow
  • Best season: July–October (monsoon), for peak volume and drama

Scale: Height vs Width

Chitrakote is short compared to India's tallest falls, but no other Indian waterfall spreads this wide.

🐴 Horseshoe Formation

Why It Curves Like a Horseshoe

The Indravati approaches the escarpment across a broad, gently curving rock lip. Instead of one narrow channel, the river spreads sideways along this curved edge before it drops, so the entire crest — not just the centre — carries water. Seen from above or from the opposite bank, that curved crest reads as a horseshoe, widest and most continuous in monsoon when the river has enough volume to cover its full breadth; in the dry months the flow contracts into a few separate streams over the same curve.

Getting There

Chitrakote Falls is reached by road from Jagdalpur, following the Indravati downstream through Bastar's forests.

Travel Notes

  • Best season: July–October for full monsoon flow; winter and summer for calmer, clearer views
  • Nearest town: Jagdalpur, ~38 km away
  • Nearest railhead: Jagdalpur Railway Station · Nearest airport: Swami Vivekananda Airport, Raipur (~273 km)
  • Activities: Boating below the falls in the dry season, riverside viewpoints, evening illumination
