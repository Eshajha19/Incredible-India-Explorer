Brand Origin & Purpose

Established under Operation Flood by the National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) to transform India from a milk-deficient nation into the world's largest milk producer.

NDDB Initiative
🏛️

National Dairy Development Board

In 1974, Mother Dairy was set up as a wholly owned subsidiary of NDDB under the vision of Dr. Verghese Kurien ("Father of the White Revolution") to ensure a stable supply of pure milk in Delhi-NCR.

Farmer Empowerment
🐄

Farmer Cooperative Linkage

Unlike commercial corporate dairies, Mother Dairy was created to source milk directly from rural farmer cooperatives across villages, providing fair prices to dairy farmers and eliminating exploitative middlemen.

Urban Purity Promise
🥛

Bulk Vended Token Milk

Mother Dairy pioneered token-operated bulk milk vending machines in Indian cities, offering chilled, standardized, hygienic milk at affordable prices without plastic packaging waste.

Operation Flood • The White Revolution

Connection to India's Dairy Revolution

How Mother Dairy served as the urban pillar of Operation Flood—the world's largest dairy development program.

Building the Anand Model in Capital Cities

Operation Flood (1970–1996) replicated Gujarat's successful Amul cooperative model across India. Mother Dairy was established specifically as the mega-processing plant and distribution arm for India's capital region, creating a reliable urban demand sink for rural milk yields.

  • Direct Rural Sourcing: Milk collected from thousands of village dairy cooperative societies across Punjab, Haryana, UP, and Rajasthan.
  • Quality Standards: Introduced multi-stage testing for fat percentage, SNF (Solids-Not-Fat), and zero-adulteration guarantees.
  • Consumer Price Stability: Kept milk prices stable for urban households while providing remunerative returns to smallholder farmers.
