Park overview

India's iconic lion landscape

Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary protects a dry forest ecosystem where Asiatic lions, leopards, deer, reptiles, birds, and local communities form one of India's best-known conservation stories.

Gir Forest

Gir's landscape combines dry deciduous forest, thorn scrub, grassland openings, riverine belts, rocky hills, and water bodies. This mixed habitat supports predators, prey, scavengers, reptiles, and rich birdlife.

Asiatic lions

The Asiatic lion is Gir's flagship species and the centre of the park's global conservation importance.

Responsible safari

A good safari is quiet, respectful, distance-aware, and focused on learning rather than chasing animals.
