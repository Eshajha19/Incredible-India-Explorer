Matangini Hazra Explorer

"Gandhi Buri" of Midnapore — a 71-year-old widow who walked into gunfire holding the tricolour, becoming the first martyr of the Quit India Movement.

Biography

Early Life

Born Matangini Maity in 1870 in Hogla village near Tamluk, Bengal, to a poor peasant family. She never received formal schooling and was widowed by age eighteen.

Gandhian Influence

Deeply inspired by Gandhian ideals of non-violence and civil disobedience, she devoted herself to social service and earned the affectionate name "Gandhi Buri" — old lady Gandhi.

Path to Sacrifice

She joined the Salt Satyagraha, protested the Chowkidari tax, and was jailed more than once — building toward her defining moment in 1942.

Quit India Movement

On 29 September 1942, Matangini Hazra led thousands toward the Tamluk Police Station in defiance of British rule.
