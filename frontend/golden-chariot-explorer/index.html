Introduction

The Golden Chariot is a luxury tourist train that connects the most prominent historical and natural destinations across South India. Named after the famous Stone Chariot in Hampi, the train offers an unparalleled travel experience, combining the romance of train journeys with the opulence of five-star hospitality.

Operated by the Karnataka State Tourism Development Corporation (KSTDC) in partnership with Indian Railways, it showcases the diverse culture, grand architecture, and stunning landscapes of Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Goa.
