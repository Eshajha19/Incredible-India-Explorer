⛰️ Tamil Nadu · Kolli Hills

Agaya Gangai Explorer

"Ganges of the Sky" — a sheer 300-foot drop of the Aiyaru river deep in the Eastern Ghats, reached only by a steep, thundering descent of over a thousand forest steps from the Arapaleeswarar Temple.

📍 Location & Water Source

Where the falls come from

Agaya Gangai lies in the Kolli Hills of the Eastern Ghats, in Namakkal district, Tamil Nadu — reached via a road famous for its 70 hairpin bends across 30 km. The falls are formed by the Aiyaru river (also called the Panchanathi stream locally), which plunges over a sheer rocky cliff near the Arapaleeswarar Temple, in a valley ringed by mountains on every side.

  • Location: Kolli Hills, Namakkal district, Tamil Nadu
  • Coordinates: 11°16'01"N, 78°23'38"E
  • Water source: Aiyaru river (Panchanathi stream)
  • Height: ~300 ft (91 m), sheer single drop
  • Also known as: Peraruvi, Akasa Ganga
  • Best season: Post-monsoon, November–January/March

🌦️ Seasons

The Falls Across the Year

The Aiyaru is fed largely by monsoon rain, so Agaya Gangai's flow varies sharply through the year — from a roaring flood to a much gentler curtain.

🪨 Geology & Vegetation

Eastern Ghats Rock & Forest

The falls drop over an exposed granite-gneiss cliff face typical of the Eastern Ghats' ancient crystalline bedrock, part of the Kolli Hills massif that rises to over 1,300 m. The surrounding slopes carry dense tropical dry-to-moist deciduous forest, with the Kolli Hills also known for coffee, pepper, jackfruit and the traditional millet cultivation of the local Malayali tribal community. The nearby Korakka Siddhar and Kalanginatha Siddhar caves sit within this same forest, long associated with herbal and medicinal plant traditions.

Getting There

Reach the Arapaleeswarar Temple by road via the Kolli Hills' famous 70-hairpin-bend ghat road, then descend on foot — there is no vehicle access to the base of the falls.

Travel Notes

  • Steps: ~1,300 steps one-way, roughly 45–60 minutes down and 60–90 minutes back up
  • Fitness: Moderate-to-difficult; railings and rest sheds line most of the staircase
  • Best season: November–January for full, clear flow and cooler trekking weather
  • Nearest town: Semmedu (~12 km) · Namakkal (~54 km) · Nearest railhead: Namakkal Railway Station
