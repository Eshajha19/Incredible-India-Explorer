At a Glance

Kalighat is the first modern school of Indian popular art — cheap souvenir paintings for pilgrims that grew into a sharp-eyed chronicle of colonial Calcutta.

History

From a riverside pilgrim trade to a school of satire — the journey of Kalighat across a century of change.

Brushwork Visualization

Deconstructing the technique that made Kalighat instantly recognisable — the discipline of a line never lifted.

Themes

Two souls in one art — the divine icons of the temple and the satirical mirror held up to society.

Gallery

Deities, epics and everyday satire — the faces of Kalighat in colour and line.
