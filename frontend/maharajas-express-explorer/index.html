Introduction

The Maharajas' Express is a luxury tourist train owned and operated by Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC). It is widely regarded as one of the most luxurious trains in the world, having won the "World's Leading Luxury Train" award multiple times at the World Travel Awards.

Designed to recreate the elegance and pageantry of the personal carriages of erstwhile Maharajas, the train offers intricately curated itineraries that cover India's most fascinating heritage sites, palaces, and wildlife reserves, primarily across Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh.
