Kolleru Lake

India's magnificent inter-deltaic wetland — cradled between the Krishna and Godavari rivers, where 200,000 migratory birds find winter refuge and pelicans have bred for centuries.

Explore the lake ↓ Meet the birds 🦆 Which bird are you?
Inter-Deltaic Basin

Between Two Great Rivers

Kolleru sits in the unique low-lying depression where the Krishna and Godavari deltas meet — a natural flood-balancing basin that has shaped Andhra's hydrology for millennia.

🗺️

Location & Basin

Straddling Eluru, Krishna and West Godavari districts. The lake swells from 901 km² in dry season to over 3,000 km² during monsoon floods.

🌊

Hydrology

Fed by Budameru, Tammileru, Ramileru, and 27 other streams. Outflows to the Bay of Bengal via the Upputeru channel.

🌾

Ecotone

Shallow eutrophic wetland with extensive Phragmites reed beds, lily ponds, and submerged macrophytes — ideal for waterfowl.

Aquatic Fauna

Fish Diversity & Fisheries

Kolleru supports 67+ fish species and sustains thousands of fishing families. Major commercial catches include catla, rohu, and the iconic Kolleru prawn.

Challenges & Hope

Conservation Battles

Kolleru faces severe pressures — but community-led restoration and Supreme Court interventions are bringing the lake back to life.
