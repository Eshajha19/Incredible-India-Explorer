Geographical & Hydrological Profile

The Roar of the Kumaradhara

Mallalli Falls is situated in the northern region of Kodagu (Coorg) district, Karnataka, at the foothills of the Pushpagiri mountain range.

The waterfall is formed directly by the perennial Kumaradhara River, which plummets over tiered rock formations into deep gorges before continuing its journey toward Subrahmanya.

Surrounded by dense semi-evergreen and shola forests, the landscape features rich biodiversity, dramatic elevation drops, and rugged trekking paths.

🌿 Fast Facts & Data

  • Location: Somwarpet Taluk, Coorg, Karnataka
  • Water Source: Kumaradhara River
  • Elevation / Height: ~61–68 meters (200+ feet cascade)
  • Trek Difficulty: Moderate (approx. 400–500 concrete stairs)
  • Coordinates: 12.7058° N, 75.8239° E
  • Best Time to Visit: July to December (Monsoon to Post-Monsoon)

Monsoon: Peak Flow & Roaring Force

The Kumaradhara River swells exponentially. Dense mist blankets the valley, heavy rapids rush across the falls, and accessibility down to the lower basin is restricted due to strong water currents and slippery steps.

🥾 Trail & Access Guide

  • By Road: Reach Somwarpet (26 km) or Sakleshpur (45 km). Vehicles can go up to the designated parking lot near the ticket point.
  • Walking / Trek: Concrete steps lead from the viewpoint down to the base viewing platform.
  • Step Count: Approx. 450 to 500 steps downward. Going down takes 15–20 minutes; climbing back up takes 30–40 minutes.
  • Precautions: Leeches are common during monsoons; wear sturdy footwear with good grip.
