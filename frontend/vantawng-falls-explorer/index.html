🌿 Mizoram · Serchhip District

Vantawng Falls Explorer

Plunging an impressive 229 metres (751 feet) through dense bamboo forests, Vantawng Khawhthla is the highest, most spectacular waterfall in Mizoram.

📍 Location & Scale

A breathtaking two-tiered cascade

Hidden in the deep valleys near Thenzawl, the Vanva River creates a spectacular two-tiered waterfall, surrounded by the verdant Mizo Hills.

  • Height: 229 meters (751 feet)
  • Source: Vanva River
  • Mountain System: Mizo Hills / Lushai Hills
  • Best time to visit: September to January
  • Nearest Town: Thenzawl (5 km) / Aizawl (137 km)

🏞️ Ecosystem

Explore the Environment

The Bamboo Grooves

The hills surrounding Vantawng are deeply forested with vast stretches of bamboo. Due to the dense and rugged terrain, the base of the waterfall is inaccessible, ensuring the surrounding ecosystem remains largely untouched.

The Vanva River

Flowing through the Serchhip district, the Vanva River gathers strength as it weaves through the hills before plunging 229 metres down a spectacular cliff face, creating the iconic two-tiered drop.

Mizo Hills (Lushai Hills)

The steep, folding ridges of the Mizo Hills create the perfect geography for dramatic waterfalls. The viewpoint for Vantawng is situated across a deep gorge, offering a panoramic view of the cascading water against the green mountain backdrop.

🌦️ Seasons

The Waterfall Across the Year

Vantawng's character shifts dramatically with Mizoram's intense monsoon.

Monsoon (Jun-Aug)

Thunderous Volume

During the heavy monsoon rains, the Vanva River swells massively. The falls become a single, roaring curtain of white water that obscures the two tiers, generating a thick mist that engulfs the gorge.

Post-Monsoon (Sep-Jan)

Clear and Distinct

As the rains subside, the water clears and the distinct two-tiered nature of the falls becomes beautifully visible against the dark rocks. The surrounding forest is at its greenest.

🗺️ Map

Getting There

The viewpoint for Vantawng Falls is located about 5 kilometres south of Thenzawl, which is easily accessible from the state capital, Aizawl.

Travel Notes

  • Accessibility: The actual base is inaccessible due to rugged terrain. A viewpoint constructed by the tourism department provides the best panoramic view.
  • Permits: An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is required for tourists to enter Mizoram.
  • Nearby: You can also visit Chhingpuii Thlan (a memorial of a tragic love story) and the handloom centres in Thenzawl.
