Police Medal for Gallantry (PMG)

Awarded to police personnel for exceptional courage, bravery, and devotion to duty in the face of grave danger.

1951 Instituted
GoI Awarded By

History & Legacy

Instituted in 1951, the Police Medal for Gallantry is one of India's highest police gallantry awards. Awarded by the Government of India, it recognizes acts of exceptional courage and devotion to duty. Over the decades, it has stood as a symbol of the ultimate sacrifice and bravery displayed by law enforcement officers across the nation.

Purpose & Recognition

The medal is specifically designed to recognize:

  • Outstanding gallantry and bravery.
  • Actions during anti-terror and counter-insurgency operations.
  • High-stakes rescue missions.
  • Exceptional courage while protecting citizens.
  • Service that goes far beyond the normal call of duty.

Eligibility Criteria

Personnel from various law enforcement agencies are eligible for the PMG, including:

  • State Police Forces
  • Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs)
  • Union Territory Police
  • Other eligible law enforcement organizations as per Government of India rules

Medal Design

Appearance: Circular in shape, typically struck in bronze, bearing distinct national and police motifs.

National Emblem: The obverse features the State Emblem of India (Ashoka Chakra) along with the motto "Satyameva Jayate".

Ribbon Colors: Features specific stripes denoting its status as a gallantry medal, generally a combination of light and dark hues representing the police forces.

Symbolism: Represents honor, duty, and the shield of protection offered by the police to the public.

Selection Process

6 Announcement

Interesting Facts

  • 🛡️ One of India's highest police gallantry honors.
  • 📅 Traditionally announced twice a year: on Republic Day and Independence Day.
  • ⚡ Recognizes extraordinary courage under highly dangerous and life-threatening conditions.
  • 🕊️ Awarded to both living officers and posthumously to those who made the supreme sacrifice.
