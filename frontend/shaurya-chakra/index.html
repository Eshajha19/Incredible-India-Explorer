Award overview

Honouring courage beyond the battlefield

Shaurya Chakra is India's third-highest peacetime gallantry award, presented for bravery, courage and self-sacrifice away from battlefield conditions.

What it represents

The award recognises exceptional courage in dangerous situations. It keeps stories of duty, self-sacrifice and national service visible for future generations.
