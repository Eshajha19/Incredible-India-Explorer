Nohkalikai Falls: India's Tallest Plunge

Plunging 340 meters (1,115 feet) in a single sheer drop off a red sandstone plateau near Cherrapunji, Nohkalikai is India's tallest plunge waterfall — fed entirely by rainfall in one of the wettest places on Earth, and named for a haunting Khasi legend.

Geological Setting

The Sohra Plateau & Khasi Hills

Nohkalikai sits at the edge of the Cherrapunji (Sohra) plateau, one of the wettest inhabited places on Earth, where the Khasi Hills drop abruptly toward the Bangladesh plains.

Bedrock: Red Sandstone Cliffs

The falls plunge over an escarpment of red and buff sandstone laid down over millions of years, exposed where the plateau breaks sharply toward the plains below.

Terrain: Khasi Hills Escarpment

Rolling grassy uplands give way abruptly to a steep escarpment, creating dramatic long-range views across the valley toward the Bangladesh plains on clear days.

Plunge Pool: Emerald Basin

At the base, minerals and depth give the plunge pool its striking blue-green color, a signature feature visible from the main viewpoint far above.

Climate: World-Record Rainfall Zone

Cherrapunji and neighboring Mawsynram rank among the wettest places on Earth, so the stream feeding Nohkalikai is almost entirely rain-fed rather than glacier or spring-fed.

Travel Seasons

Seasonal Flow & Visibility Comparison

Nohkalikai flows year-round, but volume, mist, and visibility shift dramatically with Meghalaya's extreme monsoon.

Post-Monsoon (October to November) — Best Season

The heaviest rains have passed, skies clear more often, and the falls still carry strong, clean volume. This is the sweet spot for unobstructed views of both the plunge and the plateau's long-range valley vistas.

Flow: Strong, Clear Water Visibility: High, Fewer Clouds Highlights: Best photography window of the year

Nohkalikai Falls Main Viewpoint

East Khasi Hills, Meghalaya

A paved lookout park on the plateau rim gives an uninterrupted head-on view of the full 340m plunge and its emerald plunge pool far below.

Access: 7 km from Cherrapunji (Sohra) town, ~55 km from Shillong
Approach: Paved road directly to the viewpoint parking area
Nearest Hub: Shillong (nearest major town with rail/air links onward)
