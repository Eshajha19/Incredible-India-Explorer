Impact on Agriculture & Hydrology

🌾

Kharif Crop Season

The Southwest Monsoon dictates the sowing of major crops like rice, cotton, sugarcane, maize, and pulses across 60%+ of Indian arable land.

🌧️

Tamil Nadu Winter Rain

The Northeast Monsoon accounts for nearly half of Tamil Nadu's total rainfall, filling reservoirs like Chembarambakkam and supporting Rabi cropping.

💧

Groundwater & Rivers

Perennial rivers (Ganga, Brahmaputra) and rain-fed rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery) rely on seasonal monsoonal precipitation for annual flow recharge.
