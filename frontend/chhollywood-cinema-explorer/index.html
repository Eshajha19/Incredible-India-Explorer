🎬 Chhattisgarh & Central India · 1965 – Present

Chhollywood (Chhattisgarhi Cinema)

Celebrate the vibrant heartbeat of Chhattisgarhi cinema. Steeped in tribal folklore, enchanting folk music (Lok Sangeet), and rural authenticity—from early milestones to modern commercial blockbusters.

Industry Overview

A Cultural Renaissance in Central India

Chhollywood—the vibrant film industry of Chhattisgarh—has evolved into a powerful cultural medium showcasing local dialects, traditions, festivals like Pola and Teeja, and the resilient spirit of the tribal heartland.

Driven by enthusiastic regional talent and deeply engaging social and romantic storytelling, Chhattisgarhi cinema has captured the imagination of millions, bridging grassroots folk arts with modern cinematic expression.

🌿 At a Glance

  • Pioneer Landmark: Punchhi Ke Pajaha (1965), marking the dawn of the regional film journey.
  • Modern Milestone: Mor Chhaiya Bhuiya (2000), which revolutionized commercial success.
  • Core Themes: Rural harmony, family values, folklore, social justice, and lively regional music.
  • Production Hub: Raipur and surrounding cultural centers.
