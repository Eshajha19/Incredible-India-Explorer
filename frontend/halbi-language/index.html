Cultural Heritage

Halbi is widely spoken among tribal and rural communities in Central India, particularly in Bastar and neighboring regions. The language deeply reflects the cultural traditions, folk songs, storytelling, festivals, agriculture, and forest-based lifestyles of the people. Due to its geographical spread, many speakers use Halbi alongside Hindi, Odia, and Marathi.
