Bharat Award

The premier honour under the National Bravery Awards, recognizing the most exceptional act of bravery by a child.

ICCW Awarded By
Republic Day Honored On

History & Legacy

Introduced under the prestigious National Bravery Awards framework, the Bharat Award is the highest honour bestowed upon a child in India. It recognizes the most extraordinary act of courage performed by a child, honoring exceptional selflessness, bravery, and presence of mind. Awarded at the national level, it serves to inspire courage and a sense of duty among young citizens.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Child recipients who meet the overarching National Bravery Awards eligibility criteria.
  • Acts involving exceptional courage or selfless service performed in dangerous circumstances.
  • Verified nominations submitted through the prescribed official selection process.
  • Recognition is given specifically for bravery performed despite significant personal risk.

Award Criteria

Exceptional Bravery: Acts of courage that go well beyond ordinary expectations for a child.

Saving Lives: Demonstrated courage in saving lives or protecting others from imminent danger.

Presence of Mind: Quick thinking and decisive action during severe emergencies.

National Significance: Selfless action under dangerous circumstances that warrants national-level recognition.

Interesting Facts

  • 👑 It is considered the highest and most prestigious honour among all categories of the National Bravery Awards.
  • 🌟 Recognizes the absolute most exceptional act of bravery by a child in a given year.
  • 🐘 Award recipients are traditionally honored during the grand Republic Day parade celebrations in New Delhi.
  • 🇮🇳 Serves as a powerful symbol to inspire courage, leadership, and selfless service among the youth.

Frequently Asked Questions

While all are part of the National Bravery Awards, the Bharat Award is designated as the absolute highest honour, given for the most outstanding and exceptional act of bravery of the year, regardless of the child's gender.

The Prime Minister of India typically presents the award to the child hero ahead of Republic Day, and the child participates in the Republic Day parade.
