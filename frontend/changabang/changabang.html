Changabang Explorer

Standing tall at 6,864 meters, Changabang is a majestic and complex mountain in the Garhwal Himalayas in Uttarakhand. Known for its formidable climbing routes and technical steep rock faces, this peak is a classic mountaineering expedition destination.

Geography, Glaciers & History

The Sacred Route

Changabang is located in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand. The name 'Changabang' translates to 'Shining Mountain', reflecting its steep, rocky profile that catches the sunlight beautifully.

The mountain is part of the Nanda Devi Sanctuary wall, standing out with its sheer granite faces.

The peak was first climbed in 1974 by a team led by Chris Bonington. The route features challenging technical elements, particularly on its steep, icy rock faces.

Technical Specifications

  • Elevation: 6,864 m (22,520 ft)
  • Prominence: ~500 m
  • Coordinates: 30°30′00″N 79°55′30″E
  • Climbing Difficulty: Grade VI (Extreme)
  • First Ascent: 1974
  • Expedition Team: Indo-British Expedition
