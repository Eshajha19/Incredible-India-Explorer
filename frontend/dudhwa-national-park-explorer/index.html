🌾 Terai Arc Savanna Landscape 🦌 50% of World Barasingha Population 📍 Lakhimpur Kheri, Uttar Pradesh

Dudhwa National Park Explorer

Step into Uttar Pradesh's premier wildlife sanctuary — a damp, fertile Terai belt of towering Sal timber forests, tall elephant grass savannahs, oxbow wetlands, and reintroduced Great One-Horned Rhinoceroses.

Ecological Treasure

The Terai Ecosystem & Wetlands

Dudhwa protects a vibrant tapestry of tall alluvial grasslands, dense virgin Sal forests, and perennial riverine oxbow lakes.

Ancient Sal Forests

Dominated by magnificent 150-year-old Shorea robusta (Sal) trees intermingled with Asna, Shisham, and Jamun groves that form a lush emerald canopy over the forest floor.

Tall Grassland Meadows (Phantas)

Alluvial savannah grasslands blanketed with tall Narkul and Elephant Grass standing up to 4 meters high — providing prime grazing and cover for Swamp Deer, Tigers, and Rhinos.

Oxbow Wetlands & Lagoons

Meandering tributaries (Suheli and Mohana rivers) form serene oxbow lakes like Jhaadi Taal and Bankey Taal, supporting Marsh Crocodiles, Fishing Cats, and migratory waterfowl.

Conservation Flagships

Swamp Deer & Rhino Reintroduction

Dudhwa is world-renowned for saving the nominate Barasingha subspecies and executing India's most successful rhino translocation project.

Barasingha (Swamp Deer) Refuge

Dudhwa safeguards over 50% of the world's remaining population of the nominate wetland Barasingha (*Rucervus duvaucelii duvaucelii*). Adapted with splayed hooves for marshland wading, stags grow magnificent 12-tined antlers.

🦌 Sathiana Herds: Large herds gather during winter mornings in the open Sathiana and Sonaripur phantas.

1984 Rhinoceros Translocation Project

Extinct in UP since the late 19th century, the Great One-Horned Rhinoceros was triumphantly reintroduced in 1984 when 5 rhinos from Assam and Nepal were translocated to a 27 km² solar-fenced enclosure at Sonaripur.

🦏 Thriving Population: The population has flourished to 35+ wild rhinos with a second sanctuary area opened at Bhadi Tal.
Apex Predators

Tigers & Terai Fauna

Part of the Dudhwa Tiger Reserve network forming a vital transboundary wildlife corridor connecting India with Nepal.
