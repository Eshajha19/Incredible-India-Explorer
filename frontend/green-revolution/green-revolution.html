1. Pre-Green-Revolution Food Security Context

In the mid-1960s, consecutive severe monsoons led to acute foodgrain scarcity across India.

🌧️

1965–1966 Drought Crisis

Back-to-back droughts led to severe crop failures, forcing reliance on food aid ship imports under the US PL-480 program.

🔬

Scientific Breakthrough

Dr. M.S. Swaminathan collaborated with Dr. Norman Borlaug to introduce semi-dwarf, disease-resistant wheat varieties adapted to Indian soils.

⚡

Policy Shift

Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri and C. Subramaniam prioritized agricultural self-sufficiency as a matter of national security.
