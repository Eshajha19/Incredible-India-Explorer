Historical References & Sources

Numismatic Literature

  • Stanley Lane-Poole: The Coins of the Moghul Emperors in the British Museum (London, 1892) — A fundamental catalog detailing inscription variations.
  • R. B. Whitehead: Catalogue of Coins in the Punjab Museum, Lahore: Coins of the Mughal Emperors (Oxford, 1914) — Key records of regional mint marks.
  • S. H. Hodivala: Historical Studies in Mughal Numismatics (Calcutta, 1923) — Detailed analysis of Persian couplets and metrology.
  • C. J. Brown: The Coins of India (Heritage of India Series) — Accessible guide to coinage transitions from Delhi Sultanate to Mughal standardisation.
