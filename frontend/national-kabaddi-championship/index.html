National Kabaddi Championship

The National Kabaddi Championship is one of India's premier domestic kabaddi competitions organized under the Amateur Kabaddi Federation of India (AKFI).

Bringing together state teams from across India, it serves as a platform for identifying national-level talent and has played a significant role in the development and popularization of kabaddi throughout India.

Men's Championship

State teams competing in the men's division.

  • Multiple state champions
  • National team talent pipeline
  • Annual competition structure

Women's Championship

Women's state kabaddi teams competing nationally.

  • Growing participation
  • National representation
  • Development of women athletes

Basic Rules

  • Two teams compete on a rectangular court.
  • A raider enters the opponent's half.
  • Raider must score and return safely.
  • Tackles earn defensive points.
  • Team with the highest score wins.
  • Standard match duration applies.
