Founder Story
👩‍💼

Meena Bindra's Enterprise

At age 39, homemaker Meena Bindra borrowed ₹8,000 to buy fabric, hire a tailor, and design 40 block-printed cotton salwar suits from her home in Mumbai, which sold out instantly.

Industry Shift
👗

Ready-to-Wear Convenience

Before Biba, Indian women bought unstitched fabric dress materials and waited weeks for tailors. Biba introduced standardized sizing, off-the-rack availability, and flawless finishing.

Name & Meaning
💖

"Biba": Pretty & Endearing

The brand name "Biba" is a Punjabi term of endearment for a pretty, charming, and respectable young woman—embodying grace, confidence, and warmth.

Bollywood Wardrobe Partnerships

2002

Na Tum Jaano Na Hum

Biba partnered with Bollywood to merchandise Esha Deol's ethnic wardrobe, popularizing flowy printed kurtis among young moviegoers.

2003

Baghban & Devdas

Styled traditional sarees and festive suits for major cinematic family dramas, cementing Biba as the festive brand for Indian women.

2004

Hulchul & Beyond

Pioneered official movie costume retail replicas in stores across India, allowing fans to buy outfits seen on silver screen stars.
