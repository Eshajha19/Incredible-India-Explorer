Introduction

A Gujarati craft painted, not woven

Rogan art is a traditional textile painting practice associated especially with the Kutch region of Gujarat. The word rogan refers to oil-based colour, and the craft uses a paste prepared from castor oil and pigments.

The artisan places coloured paste onto fabric with a stylus-like tool and draws fine lines and shapes. Designs may be created freehand or developed by transferring a half-pattern to create balanced compositions.

The technique requires control, patience and a strong visual vocabulary, making it both decorative textile art and an important part of Gujarat's living cultural heritage.

Artist spotlight

The Khatri artisan tradition

Rogan painting is closely associated with artisan families in Kutch, including Khatri practitioners who have helped preserve the technique across generations.

The craft is a body of knowledge involving material preparation, colour handling, drawing technique, composition and patient repetition.

Contemporary practitioners have helped bring Rogan to new audiences through exhibitions, demonstrations and workshops while continuing to adapt the tradition.
