At a Glance

Dokra (or Dhokra) is one of India's oldest living metal crafts — a seamless, one-piece bronze cast whose every example is unique.

Historical Background

From the bronzes of Mohenjo-daro to the village furnaces of the Dokra Damar — the 4,500-year journey of the lost-wax craft.

The Lost-Wax Process

Six steps of cire perdue casting — the ancient recipe of wax, clay, fire and bronze that makes every Dokra piece a one-off.

Artisan Communities

The tribal smiths and regional schools that keep the lost-wax flame burning across India's heartland.

Craft Gallery
