🌊 Blue Flag Certified Beach No. 7

Radhanagar Beach

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman & Nicobar Islands

Widely regarded as one of the most beautiful beaches in Asia, Radhanagar Beach is globally celebrated for its pristine white sands, turquoise waters, and ancient tropical mahua forests that line its shores.

🌐 Geographical Profile

  • Location: Western Coast, Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), India.
  • Coordinates: 11.9841° N, 92.9515° E.
  • Terrain: A crescent-shaped coastline stretching over 2 kilometers. It features an exceptionally wide sandy foreshore with a gentle slope into the sea, completely free of sharp coral reefs near the shore, making it highly ideal for swimming.

🏄 Major Attractions & Eco-Activities

  • The Saffron Sunset: Due to its due-west exposure, Radhanagar is legendary for raw, unobstructed sunsets that paint the horizon in vivid shades of amber and magenta.
  • Forest Canopy Walking: The beach boundary is ringed by towering, old-growth Mahua trees, providing lush natural shade right up to the high-tide line.
  • Open-Water Swimming: The waves are gentle with manageable swells, making it a highly accessible spot for wading and safe open-water swimming.
  • Eco-Footprint Tracking: Motorized water sports are intentionally banned here to preserve the marine eco-balance and prevent acoustic pollution.

🗺️ Nearby Places Worth Visiting

  • Elephant Beach: Located just a short boat ride or forest trek away, this is the primary hub on Havelock for active water sports like snorkeling, sea walking, and glass-bottom boat rides.
  • Vijaynagar Beach (Beach No. 5): Positioned on the opposite side of the island, it is a serene coast lined with coconut groves, perfect for catching early morning sunrises.
  • Kalapathar Beach: A dramatic coastline characterized by black rocks set against aqua-green waters, ideal for beachcombing and morning photography.
