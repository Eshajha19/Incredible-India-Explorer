Bamboo Craft of India (Living Green Artistry)

Discover how bamboo becomes furniture, baskets, lamps, musical instruments, and ritual objects across India's forests, villages, and artisan clusters.

Historical Overview & Craft Legacy

Bamboo craft traditions in India are deeply rooted in rural life, especially in the North-East, where bamboo is both material and livelihood. For centuries, communities have used it to create baskets, mats, houses, musical instruments, and ceremonial objects with remarkable efficiency and beauty.

What makes bamboo craft significant is its sustainability: it grows quickly, requires minimal processing, and supports circular local economies. The craft reflects a careful understanding of forests, seasons, and cultural ritual.
