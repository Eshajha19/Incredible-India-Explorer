The Warrior Traditions of India

From the jungle gyms of Kerala to the plains of Punjab, India's martial arts (Shastra Vidya) are a profound synthesis of combat, spirituality, and physical mastery, dating back thousands of years.

Traditional Weapons (Astras & Shastras)

Urumi

The flexible sword of Kalaripayattu, acting like a whip made of highly flexible steel blades.

Gatka Stick

Wooden sticks used in the Sikh martial art of Gatka, simulating swords for rapid, flowing strikes.

Silambam Staff

A long bamboo or wooden staff used in the Tamil art of Silambam, focusing on distance and agility.

Thang-Ta Sword

The ceremonial sword and spear used in Manipur's Huyen Langlon, combining grace with lethal precision.
