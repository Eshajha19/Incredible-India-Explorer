Tiger Falls: Forest Cascade of Chakrata

Known locally as Keraao Pachad, Tiger Falls plunges 95 meters (312 feet) in an unbroken direct drop from high deodar and oak canopies into a crystal-clear mountain pool, making it one of the highest direct waterfalls in Uttarakhand.

💧 95m (312 ft) Direct Drop 🌲 Coniferous Deodar & Oak Woods 🥾 5 km Scenic Downhill Trek ⛰️ Elevation: 1,400 m (4,593 ft)

Tiger Falls Gorge (1,400 m)

Garhwal Coniferous Valley

Tucked in an amphitheater of tall deodars and rhododendrons, Tiger Falls is accessible either via a steep 5 km direct downhill nature trek from Chakrata town or by driving 20 km along a scenic mountain road followed by a 1 km walk.

Trail Distance: 5 km (Downhill trek) / 1 km (Road head)
Trek Duration: 1.5 – 2 hours downhill, 2.5 hours return
Surroundings: Cedrus deodara, Quercus oaks, ferns

Trail Logistics

Trek Information & Accessibility Guide

Everything you need to know for reaching Tiger Falls safely.

🥾

Route 1: Downhill Forest Trek (5 km)

Starts right from Chakrata market. Follows an unpaved scenic mountain trail through ancient deodar woodlands, local terraced farms, and Jaunsari hamlets.

🚗

Route 2: Motor Road + Short Walk (1 km)

Drive 20 km from Chakrata to the Tiger Falls road-head parking. From the parking lot, an easy 1 km paved descent with guardrails leads directly to the pool.

🚌

How to Reach Chakrata

87 km from Dehradun city (3.5 hours drive via Vikas Nagar). Nearest railway station and airport are in Dehradun.

ℹ️

Visitor Accessibility & Guidelines

Carry drinking water and sturdy trekking shoes. Small local tea stalls serve hot Maggi and chai near the pool. Respect local eco-preservation rules.

Nearby Attractions in the Chakrata Circuit

Explore more wonders across the tranquil heights of Chakrata.

🌅

Chilmiri Neck

A plateau viewpoint 4 km from town offering jaw-dropping 360-degree sunset views and panoramic glimpses of Rohini and Bandarpoonch Himalayan peaks.

🌲

Deoban (2,865 m)

Meaning 'God's Own Forest', this ridge offers views of over 55 major Himalayan mountain peaks and pristine mixed coniferous forests.

🌳

Kanasar Deodar Forest

Home to some of Asia's oldest and widest trunk deodar trees, surrounding a peaceful green meadow and forest rest house.

🪨

Budher Caves (Miola Caves)

Ancient limestone cave system situated 30 km from Chakrata, discovered by German explorer Miola amidst alpine pastures.
