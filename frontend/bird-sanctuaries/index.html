Bird Sanctuaries Guide

Discover India's rich avian biodiversity. Explore critical wetland habitats, learn about the Central Asian Flyway, and trace the seasonal migration of millions of birds.

🌍 The Central Asian Flyway

India lies at the heart of the Central Asian Flyway (CAF), a major migratory route spanning from the Arctic to the Indian Ocean. Millions of waterbirds use this route to escape harsh winters.

🦆 Wetland Conservation

Wetlands are the primary habitats for both resident and migratory birds. Designations like Ramsar Sites highlight wetlands of international importance critical for avian survival.

🦅 Endemic & Threatened

Sanctuaries play a vital role in protecting India's endemic species and globally threatened birds (like the Siberian Crane and Great Indian Bustard) from habitat loss and poaching.
