1. What Defines an Indian Classical Raga?

A raga is not merely a static scale or key, but a dynamic melodic framework with precise tonal gravity.

🎼

Swaras (Notes)

7 pitches (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni) with natural, flat (Komal), and sharp (Tivra) variations.

↗️

Arohana & Avarohana

Ascending and descending scale paths defining which notes can be emphasized or omitted.

👑

Vadi & Samvadi Notes

The King (primary) and Queen (secondary) dominant notes establishing the tonal centerpiece.

⏰

Samaya Chakra

The 24-hour cycle linking swara moods to natural solar angles and human circadian rhythms.
