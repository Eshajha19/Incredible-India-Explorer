🌟 Highest Civilian Honour for Children

Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar (PMRBP)

Recognizing exceptional achievements of Indian child prodigies, innovators, environmentalists, sports stars, artists, and brave young heroes.

1996 Instituted
5 - 18 Yrs Age Eligibility
6 Fields Categories
₹1 Lakh Cash Prize

⏳ History & Genesis

Instituted in 1996 by the Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD), Government of India, the award was originally titled the "National Child Award for Exceptional Achievement". It was created to celebrate outstanding talents and accomplishments of children across the nation.

In 2018, the award scheme was restructured and re-titled as the Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar (PMRBP). The unified award incorporates the Bal Shakti Puraskar (for individual child achievers) and recognizes children who display exceptional abilities and courage that inspire the nation.

🏅 Award Categories

The Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar is presented across 6 major domains of excellence:

💡 1. Innovation

Awarded to children who invent ground-breaking tech prototypes, scientific devices, environmental sensors, or digital apps that solve real-world problems.

📚 2. Scholastic Achievements

Recognizes students with exceptional academic brilliance, national/international olympiad ranks, mathematical research, or publishing scholarly works.

🏹 3. Sports

Honours child athletes who excel in international or national championships in archery, gymnastics, chess, swimming, shooting, or indigenous sports.

🎨 4. Arts & Culture

Conferred on young prodigies in classical dance, vocal music, instrumental performance, painting, sculpture, literature, or theater.

🌱 5. Social Service

Honours young leaders who spearhead impactful social campaigns, tree plantation drives, literacy missions for underprivileged peers, or anti-plastic drives.

🛡️ 6. Bravery

Recognizes selfless acts of extraordinary courage by children who save human lives, rescue drowning peers, or subdue dangerous wild animals under imminent peril.

📜 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the following official eligibility criteria established by the Ministry of Women & Child Development:

Age & Citizenship Limits

  • Age Limit: Must be an Indian citizen residing in India aged between 5 and 18 years as of August 31 of the nomination year.
  • Achievement Record: Achievement must be documented with certificates, prototypes, published papers, or police/district magistrate verification for bravery.

Exclusions & Criteria

  • Single Recipient: An achievement for which the child has already received PMRBP in previous years is not eligible again.
  • Verification: All claims undergo strict background verification by District Magistrates / Collectors.

📋 Selection Process Workflow

Selection for the PMRBP is conducted through a multi-level national screening process:

1
Online Nomination
Submitted via the unified Rashtriya Puraskar Portal (awards.gov.in).
2
District Verification
District Magistrates / Collectors verify background & claims.
3
MWCD Screening
Screening Committee chaired by Joint Secretary (MWCD) evaluates applications.
4
National Committee
National Selection Committee led by Union Minister (MWCD) finalizes list.
5
PM & President Approval
Approved by the Prime Minister & President of India.
6
Investiture Week
Conferred during National Girl Child Day / Republic Day week in January.

🏛️ Award Ceremony & National Honours

The PMRBP ceremony is a prestigious multi-day national event celebrating young Indian achievers:

Preserved Traditions & Ceremonial Highlights

👑 Presidential Investiture

Conferred directly by the President of India at Durbar Hall / Ashoka Hall at Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi.

☕ PM Interaction

Winners enjoy an exclusive interactive session and high tea with the Prime Minister of India at 7 Lok Kalyan Marg.

🚜 Republic Day Parade

Awardees ride in open ceremonial gypsies down Kartavya Path during the televised Republic Day Parade.

🏅 Medal & Cash Prize

Recipients receive a silver medallion, a citation scroll, a cash prize of ₹1,00,000 (₹1 Lakh), and a blockchain digital certificate.

📅 Historical Timeline

Key historical milestones of the Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar:

1996

Medal Institution

Instituted as the "National Child Award for Exceptional Achievement" by MWCD to honor child prodigies.

2018

Restructuring as PMRBP

Renamed Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar (PMRBP), incorporating Bal Shakti Puraskar across 6 fields.

2022

Digital Blockchain Certificates

Became India's first national award to issue tamper-proof digital blockchain certificates to all winners.

2024

Unified Rashtriya Puraskar Portal

Nominations integrated seamlessly into the central government Rashtriya Puraskar Portal (awards.gov.in).
