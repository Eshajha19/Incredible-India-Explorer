Historical Background

Phulkari, which literally translates to "flower work," is a traditional embroidery style originating from Punjab. The craft has deep roots in the region's domestic life, where women traditionally embroidered textiles for everyday use, special occasions, and weddings. Passed down from mother to daughter, Phulkari is a vibrant expression of Punjabi culture, narrating stories of village life, joy, and sorrow through colourful threads on hand-spun cotton.
