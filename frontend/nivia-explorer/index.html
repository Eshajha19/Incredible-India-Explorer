Nivia: India's Football Legacy

Since 1934, crafting world-class footballs and sports equipment in Jalandhar, the sports capital of India.

The Origin Story

⚽

1934: Foundation in Jalandhar

Nivia was founded as "Freewill Sports" in Jalandhar, Punjab - a city that would become India's sporting goods manufacturing hub. Started by the Saini family with a focus on football production.

🏭

The Jalandhar Advantage

Jalandhar became known as the "Sports Capital of India" with over 2,000 manufacturers. Nivia emerged as the premium brand, exporting to 70+ countries and setting quality benchmarks.

🌍

Global Recognition

Nivia footballs received FIFA Quality Pro certification - the highest international standard, making them official match balls for multiple international tournaments.

Football Excellence

Nivia Ashtang - The Flagship

The Nivia Ashtang is a revolutionary football with 8 panels (instead of traditional 32), reducing seams by 75%. This FIFA Quality Pro certified ball has been used in multiple international tournaments including the Indian Super League, I-League, and FIFA U-17 World Cup.

Evolution of Nivia Footballs

1950s

Hand-Stitched Leather Era

Traditional 32-panel leather footballs hand-stitched by skilled craftsmen in Jalandhar.

1980s

Synthetic Revolution

Introduction of synthetic PU leather balls that offered better durability and water resistance.

2000s

Thermal Bonding Technology

Adoption of thermal bonding for seamless panels, improving ball flight consistency.

2014

Ashtang Launch

Revolutionary 8-panel football launched, becoming India's first FIFA Quality Pro certified ball.

2020s

Smart Technology

Integration of smart chips for tracking ball movement and performance analytics.

Brand Milestones

1962

Nivia Brand Launch

Freewill Sports launched the premium "Nivia" brand name, positioning it as India's top sports equipment brand.

1998

Official Sponsor

Became official match ball supplier for the National Football League (NFL), India's premier football competition.

2014

FIFA Certification

Nivia Ashtang becomes India's first football to receive FIFA Quality Pro certification.

2017

FIFA U-17 World Cup

Official match ball supplier for the FIFA U-17 World Cup hosted by India.

2020

ISL Partnership

Extended partnership with Indian Super League as official match ball supplier.

2023

Smart Ball Innovation

Launch of smart footballs with embedded tracking technology for performance analytics.

Indian Sports Connection

🏟️

League Partnerships

Official match ball supplier for ISL, I-League, Santosh Trophy, and multiple state football associations across India.
