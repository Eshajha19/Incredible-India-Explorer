🎬 Coastal Karnataka & Tulu Nadu · 1971 – Present

Tulu Cinema (Coastalwood)

Explore the dynamic and fast-growing world of Tulu cinema. Rooted in the rich cultural traditions of Tulu Nadu, vibrant folk arts like Yakshagana and Bhoota Kola, and modern urban narratives.

Industry Overview

A Flourishing Coastal Voice

Tulu cinema, popularly known as Coastalwood, operates primarily from Mangaluru and Udupi in Coastal Karnataka. Beginning with its first milestone film *ENnatir Kural* in 1971, the industry has steadily grown into a passionate creative hub.

Highlighting coastal societal values, regional humor, family sentiments, and distinctive folklore, Tulu films have garnered immense local adoration and widespread critical acclaim for their originality.

🌊 At a Glance

  • Pioneer Landmark: *ENnatir Kural* (1971), directed by A. Srinivasa Rao.
  • Production Centers: Mangaluru and Udupi (Coastal Karnataka).
  • Core Elements: Coastal folklore, local humor, social realism, and vibrant native traditions.
  • Milestone Era: Rapid expansion and modernization through hits like *Oriyard Ori Asal* and *Chaldi*.
