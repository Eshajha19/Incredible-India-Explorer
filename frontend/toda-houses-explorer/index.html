Cultural Context

The Toda Community and Their Environment

The Toda people are a pastoral community living in the high-altitude Nilgiri Hills of Tamil Nadu. Their traditional barrel-vaulted houses, known as *dogles*, reflect a deep symbiotic relationship with the surrounding Shola forests and grasslands.

Constructed entirely from locally sourced natural materials—such as bamboo, rattan, and thatch—the structures are uniquely adapted to the heavy monsoon rains and cool climate of the region. The houses sit within a semi-circular enclosure of loose stones, forming small hamlets called *munds*.

Key Architectural Features

  • Shape: Distinctive barrel-vaulted/half-barrel roof structure.
  • Materials: Bamboo, dried grass, rattan (cane), and wood.
  • Climate Control: Thick thatch insulation and a tiny entrance preserve warmth.
  • Art: Elaborate mural decorations on the front wooden facade.
