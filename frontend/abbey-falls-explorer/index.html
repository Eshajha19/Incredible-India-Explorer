The place

A waterfall inside Coorg's working landscape

Abbey Falls sits among coffee and spice estates near Madikeri. The approach crosses a plantation landscape before the sound of falling water opens into the gorge.

📍

Location

Kodagu district, Karnataka, roughly 9 km from Madikeri according to the Kodagu district administration.

🌊

Water source

The cascade is part of the Kaveri river system and joins the river downstream after dropping over a broad rocky face.

🌿

Vegetation

Coffee, cardamom, pepper vines and dense Western Ghats greenery frame the approach and gorge.

🥾

Access

From the entrance, visitors follow a short paved trail through plantation greenery to viewing points and the hanging bridge.
