Biodiversity Hotspots

🏔️

Western Ghats

One of the world's eight hottest hotspots of biological diversity. The endemic species here are found nowhere else on Earth.

⛰️

Himalayas

Home to unique alpine flora and fauna adapted to extreme altitudes and cold, including the elusive Snow Leopard.

🌳

Indo-Burma (Northeast)

A region of high endemism with complex ecosystems ranging from tropical rainforests to temperate broadleaf forests.

Conservation Efforts

India has established numerous protected areas, wildlife sanctuaries, and biosphere reserves to safeguard its endemic heritage. Community-led initiatives and strict anti-poaching laws play a crucial role.
