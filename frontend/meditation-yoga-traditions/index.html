🕉️ Yoga Lineages of India

India is the birthplace of yoga — a 5,000-year-old tradition that has evolved into diverse schools, each offering a unique path to physical health, mental clarity, and spiritual awakening.

🧘 Meditation Techniques

Meditation (Dhyana) is the 7th limb of Patanjali's Ashtanga Yoga. These ancient techniques offer paths to inner peace, heightened awareness, and liberation.

🔮 The Seven Chakras

Chakras are energy centers along the spine. Each governs specific physical, emotional, and spiritual aspects. Meditation and yoga practices aim to balance and activate these energy centers.

🌬️ Pranayama — Breath Control

Pranayama is the 4th limb of yoga — the conscious regulation of prana (life force) through breathing techniques. These practices directly influence the nervous system and mental state.
