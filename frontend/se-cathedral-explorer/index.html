Se Cathedral

Discover Old Goa's Portuguese Architectural Heritage

📍 Old Goa (Velha Goa), Goa, India

Historical Background

During the Portuguese colonial period, Old Goa served as the capital of Portuguese India. The monumental Se Cathedral is the most imposing structure from this era.

Commissioned by the Portuguese Viceroy Redondo to commemorate the victory of Afonso de Albuquerque over a Muslim army, construction began in 1562 and spanned several decades. The cathedral is intrinsically linked to the Archdiocese of Goa and the rapid spread of Christianity during Portuguese rule.

Portuguese Architecture

The Se Cathedral is an outstanding example of Portuguese-Manueline architecture with profound Iberian influences. Its design reflects the grandeur and religious fervor of the 16th century.

Characterized by a striking whitewashed exterior, classical architectural elements, and intricate interior ornamentation, the cathedral remains one of the largest and most impressive churches in Asia.

Cathedral Structure

  • Main Nave: The sprawling, vaulted main nave is renowned for its excellent acoustics and majestic scale.
  • Altars & Chapels: Houses several exquisitely carved altars. The main altar is dedicated to St. Catherine of Alexandria, flanked by gilded chapels on either side.
  • Main Façade: The grand, classical façade features Tuscan proportions, measuring impressively in height and breadth.
  • Interior Details: Features detailed reredos, woodwork, and colonial-era paintings depicting scenes from the life of St. Catherine.

The Famous Bell Tower

The cathedral initially had two flanking towers, but one collapsed in 1776 and was never rebuilt. The surviving tower houses the famous "Golden Bell".

Renowned for its rich tone, it is the largest bell in Goa and one of the best in the world. Historically, its solemn tolling marked important religious and civic events throughout the city.

Old Goa Heritage

As the former capital of Portuguese India, Old Goa was once a thriving metropolis known as the "Rome of the East".

Today, it boasts an incredible concentration of colonial monuments, churches, and convents. The Se Cathedral stands as the anchor of this historic landscape, preserving the architectural memory of a bygone era.

UNESCO Recognition

The Se Cathedral is a prominent constituent of the Churches and Convents of Goa UNESCO World Heritage property.

Inscribed in 1986, this UNESCO recognition protects the cathedral's irreplaceable legacy and highlights its global significance as a masterclass of colonial religious architecture.
