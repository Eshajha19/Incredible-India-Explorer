👑 Award Hierarchy & Order of Precedence

Understand the ranking, order of wear, and precedence of civilian, gallantry, and specialized national honours in India.

Civilian Honours

Civilian Order of Precedence

  1. 1st: Bharat Ratna — Highest Civilian Award
  2. 2nd: Padma Vibhushan — Exceptional & Distinguished Service
  3. 3rd: Padma Bhushan — High Order Distinguished Service
  4. 4th: Padma Shri — Distinguished Service in Any Field
Wartime Gallantry

Wartime Military Valour

  1. 1st: Param Vir Chakra (PVC) — Most Conspicuous Bravery
  2. 2nd: Maha Vir Chakra (MVC) — High Gallantry in Battle
  3. 3rd: Vir Chakra (VC) — Gallantry in Enemy Presence
Peacetime Gallantry

Peacetime Valour

  1. 1st: Ashoka Chakra — Highest Peacetime Valour
  2. 2nd: Kirti Chakra — Conspicuous Gallantry
  3. 3rd: Shaurya Chakra — Peacetime Bravery & Counter-Insurgency
Sports Honours

National Sporting Excellence

  1. 1st: Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna — Highest Sporting Honour
  2. 2nd: Arjuna Award — Outstanding Performance (4 Years)
  3. 3rd: Dronacharya Award — Excellence in Sports Coaching
Specialized Honours

Literary, Science & Public Honours

  1. Jnanpith Award — Highest Literary Honour
  2. Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar — Highest Science Honour
  3. Gandhi Peace Prize — International Non-Violence Honour
  4. National Communal Harmony Award — Integration Honour

💡 Fun Facts & Historical Trivia

🍃

Peepal Leaf Medallion

The Bharat Ratna medal is uniquely shaped like a peepal leaf made of toned bronze, with a sun embossed in platinum on the front.

🎨

Foreign Designer of PVC

The Param Vir Chakra medal was designed by Savitri Khanolkar (born Eve Yvonne Maday de Maros), a Swiss-born woman married to an Indian Army officer.

👩

Youngest Ashoka Chakra Winner

Neerja Bhanot was the first female and youngest recipient of the Ashoka Chakra (posthumously at age 22) for saving 359 passengers on Pan Am Flight 73.

🏏

Youngest Bharat Ratna

Sachin Tendulkar became the youngest person (age 40) and first sportsperson to receive the Bharat Ratna in 2014.

🎬

Satyajit Ray's Design

The plaque presented to Sahitya Akademi awardees was personally designed by Oscar-winning film director Satyajit Ray.

🏝️

Andaman Islands Naming

In 2023, 21 uninhabited islands in Andaman & Nicobar were officially named after the 21 Param Vir Chakra awardees.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Most national awards, including Civilian, Gallantry, and Sports awards, are formally conferred by the President of India at ceremonial investiture functions held at Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi.

Any Indian citizen can submit self-nominations or recommend grassroots heroes through the official government portal Rashtriya Puraskar Portal (awards.gov.in) during the annual open window.

No, the Bharat Ratna carries no monetary grant. Recipients receive a Sanad (certificate) signed by the President, a peepal-leaf medal, and 7th rank in the Indian Order of Precedence.

Wartime awards (PVC, MVC, VC) are given for bravery in the direct presence of the enemy in war. Peacetime awards (Ashoka, Kirti, Shaurya) are given for courage away from the battlefield, including counter-insurgency and anti-terror operations.
