A high summit of the Garhwal Himalayas

Kalanka is a 6,931 m mountain in Uttarakhand's Garhwal Himalayas. This page adds the missing peak to the Mountains of India collection with a clean, responsive, educational layout.

Kalanka belongs to the dramatic Garhwal Himalayan region, where steep ridges, snow slopes, glacier-fed valleys, and high-altitude weather create a demanding mountain environment.
