Deccan Odyssey

A Majestic Train Journey Through the Maratha Heartland

Discover the Journey

Introduction

The Deccan Odyssey is a special luxury train based on the model of the Palace on Wheels, specifically aimed at boosting tourism on the Konkan route of Indian Railways. It is a joint venture between the Maharashtra Government and the Ministry of Railways, offering travelers a 5-star hotel experience on wheels as they explore heritage sites, beaches, and wildlife across Maharashtra and neighboring states.

History & Development

2001

Conception

The Maharashtra Tourism Development Corporation (MTDC) and Indian Railways conceptualized a luxury train to promote tourism in Maharashtra, similar to Rajasthan's Palace on Wheels.

Jan 2004

Inaugural Journey

The Deccan Odyssey made its maiden journey on January 16, 2004, flagged off from the Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) in Mumbai.

2014 & Beyond

Expansion

The train expanded its offerings from a single route to multiple distinctive journeys covering Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Southern India.

Luxury Facilities

Royal Accommodation

21 luxuriously appointed coaches, including 11 for accommodation (Presidential Suites and Deluxe Cabins) with attached bathrooms.

Fine Dining

Two multi-cuisine restaurants, Peshwa I and Peshwa II, serving exquisite Indian and continental delicacies.

Mumbai Hi Lounge

A well-stocked bar and lounge car offering a relaxed atmosphere with select wines and spirits.

Health & Spa

The train features a dedicated spa car offering Ayurvedic massages, a steam room, and a mini-gymnasium.

Interesting Facts

  • Coach Naming: The coaches are named after different regions and dynasties of Maharashtra, such as Kadamba, Hoysala, and Chalukya.
  • Conference Car: It includes a dedicated conference car capable of hosting business meetings for up to 30 people, equipped with modern audiovisual facilities.
  • Awards: The Deccan Odyssey has won the prestigious World Travel Award for Asia's Leading Luxury Train several times.
  • Capacity: It can accommodate up to 88 passengers, ensuring a highly personalized and uncrowded luxury experience.
