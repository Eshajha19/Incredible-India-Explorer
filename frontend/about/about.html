  Our Journey

Our Story

From a simple idea to a thriving open-source ecosystem — here's how Incredible India Explorer came to life.

Why It Started

India's vast cultural, geographical, and historical richness deserves a living digital home. The project was born from a desire to create an interactive, accessible, and ever-growing resource that showcases every facet of Incredible India.

Inspiration

Inspired by India's timeless heritage — from the snow-capped Himalayas to the sun-kissed coasts, from ancient monuments to bustling modern cities — the project aims to capture the spirit of a nation in pixels and code.

Open-Source Growth

What started as a solo project quickly grew into a vibrant community. Developers, designers, writers, and history enthusiasts from across the globe started contributing — adding modules, fixing bugs, and shaping the roadmap.

Vision for the Future

We envision Incredible India Explorer as the definitive open-source reference for India's diversity — with 200+ modules, multilingual support, educational curricula integration, and immersive experiences powered by community innovation.

  Our Purpose

Our Mission

Four pillars that guide everything we build and every decision we make.

Education

Making India's geography, history, and cultural heritage accessible to students, educators, and lifelong learners through interactive modules and engaging visual content.

Tourism & Heritage

Showcasing India's monuments, natural wonders, festivals, and cuisines to inspire travel, preserve heritage, and foster appreciation for the country's rich diversity.

Interactive Learning

Transforming static information into immersive experiences — quizzes, games, timelines, maps, and scrollytelling narratives that make learning active and enjoyable.

Open-Source Collaboration

Building a welcoming community where anyone can contribute — whether you're a seasoned developer or a first-time open-source participant. Every contribution matters.
