Brand Origin & Founder Vision

How an American consultant's love for Indian handlooms grew into India's largest private platform for craft-based products.

Founder Vision
🏛️

John Bissell & Handloom Exports

In 1960, John Bissell, an American consultant with the Ford Foundation, established Fabindia as an export company for Indian handloom home furnishings, working directly with master weavers in North India.

1976 Landmark
🏪

First Retail Store in Delhi

In 1976, Fabindia opened its legendary first retail outlet in Greater Kailash, New Delhi, introducing urban Indian consumers to authentic hand-woven upholstery, durries, and hand-printed fabrics.

Philosophy
🌿

Celebrate Rural Craftsmanship

Fabindia's founding ethos was to ensure traditional techniques were preserved by giving artisans a sustainable, dignified livelihood and a steady market rather than charity.

Social Enterprise Model

Artisan Connection & Empowerment

How Fabindia pioneered Community Owned Companies (COCs) to make artisans equity shareholders in the supply chain.

Community Owned Companies (COCs)

In 2007, Fabindia launched a revolutionary supply chain model by setting up regional Artisan Micro-Market Companies. Over 26,000 rural artisans became shareholders in these companies, receiving dividend payouts and fair wages.

  • 55,000+ Craftspeople: Sustaining weaver clusters across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal, UP, and Madhya Pradesh.
  • Preserving Endangered Crafts: Preventing young weavers from migrating to urban labor by offering profitable rural livelihoods.
  • Pre-Financing & Fair Trade: Providing raw materials and advance payments so artisans never fall into debt traps.
