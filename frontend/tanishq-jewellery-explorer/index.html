Brand Origin & Tata Vision

How Titan Company brought transparency, hallmarking, and organized retail to India's fragmented gold market.

Etymology
✨

'Tan' + 'Nishq'

The name Tanishq was coined by Xerxes Desai. It combines 'Tan' (meaning body in Sanskrit) and 'Nishq' (a gold coin or ornament), signifying adornment of the body.

Market Reform
🏛️

Organizing Unorganized Gold

In 1994, Indian gold retail was dominated by local jewelers with opaque pricing and unverified purity. Tanishq introduced standardized pricing, itemized billing, and state-of-the-art manufacturing.

Tata Ethos
🤝

Ethical Sourcing & Trust

Back by the Tata Group's legacy, Tanishq established ethical gold sourcing, conflict-free diamond certification, and transparent exchange policies across all showrooms.

The Karatmeter & 100% Purity Promise

How Tanishq introduced non-destructive X-ray gold testing to expose impurity practices and restore consumer trust across India.

Revolutionizing Consumer Confidence

In 1998, Tanishq imported non-destructive X-ray fluorescence Karatmeters and placed them free of cost in front of every showroom. Customers could test gold bought from any jeweler, discovering widespread under-caratage in traditional markets.

  • X-Ray Precision: Measures gold purity down to 0.01% in under 3 minutes without melting or scratching jewellery.
  • Full Melt Value Guarantee: Established fair exchange value for old gold ornaments.

Design Journey

Design Evolution & Cinematic Royalty

Tracing Tanishq's evolution from European-inspired export lines to regal Indian heritage collections.

1994–1996

European Export Phase

Initial collections featured 18K Western-style geometric designs aimed at international markets before pivoting to Indian preferences.

1997–2005

22K Regional Gold Pivot

Shifted focus to 22K yellow gold, recreating regional motifs—Kanthi, Kasu Mala, Jhumkas, and temple architecture designs.

2008–2018

Bollywood Cinematic Royalty

Hand-crafted 400+ kg of authentic gold, polki, and gemstone jewellery for epic period films Jodhaa Akbar and Padmaavat.

2017–Present

Rivaah Wedding & Modern Mia

Launch of Rivaah catering to 15+ distinct regional Indian brides alongside Mia for modern office wear.
