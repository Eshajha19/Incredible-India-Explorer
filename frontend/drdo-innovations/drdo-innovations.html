About DRDO

India's Shield of Innovation

DRDO operates under the Ministry of Defence, providing cutting-edge technologies to the Indian Armed Forces. With a vast network of laboratories spread across India, DRDO works on a broad spectrum of disciplines — aeronautics, armaments, electronics, combat vehicles, instrumentation, missiles, materials, life sciences, and more.

From the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme to the development of BrahMos — the world's fastest supersonic cruise missile — DRDO has consistently pushed the boundaries of indigenous defence technology.
