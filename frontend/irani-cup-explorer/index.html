The Silver Challenge Cup

Commissioned in 1959 by the BCCI. Credited to the legendary BCCI treasurer and president Zal R. Irani.

Origins & History

A 65-Year Crucible of Test Match Prospects

The tournament was conceived during the 1959–60 season to commemorate the Silver Jubilee of the Ranji Trophy championship. Following the demise of Zal R. Irani, a key BCCI official, manager, and administrator who served Indian cricket from 1928 until his death in 1970, the competition was officially named the Irani Cup in his honor.

The Unique Concept: Unlike knockout tournaments, the Irani Cup is a single, showpiece 5-day first-class match played at the start or conclusion of the domestic season. It pits the reigning Ranji Trophy Champion team against the Rest of India — a powerhouse composite team selected by national selectors featuring top performers across all other Indian states.

🏏
National Team Stepping Stone: An Irani Cup century or 5-wicket haul is traditionally viewed as the direct audition criteria for the Indian National Test team.

Tournament Fast Facts

  • First Edition: 1959–60 (Ranji Champions Bombay vs ROI at Karnail Singh Stadium, Delhi)
  • Most Titles (Team): Rest of India (30+ Titles) · Mumbai (15 Titles)
  • Format: 5-Day First-Class (Red-ball / White-clothing)
  • Deciding Factor: Outright win, or first-innings lead if drawn
  • Reigning Champion (2024–25): Mumbai (Won on First Innings Lead vs ROI at Ekana, Lucknow)
Historic Battlegrounds

Iconic Venues of the Irani Cup

Prestigious stadiums across India that have witnessed legendary first-class duels.

🏟️

Wankhede Stadium & Brabourne

Mumbai, Maharashtra

The spiritual home of Mumbai cricket, hosting historic Irani matches where Gavaskar, Tendulkar, and Vengsarkar scored monumental tons.

🏟️

M. Chinnaswamy Stadium

Bengaluru, Karnataka

A premier venue witnessing classic battles with dramatic turnarounds and fierce seam/spin bowling displays.

🏟️

Feroz Shah Kotla (Arun Jaitley)

New Delhi

Hosted the inaugural 1959-60 clash at Karnail Singh/Kotla where Polly Umrigar led Bombay to victory over ROI.

🏟️

BRSABV Ekana Stadium

Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh

Host of the thrilling 2024 edition where Sarfaraz Khan's unbeaten double-century ended Mumbai's 27-year Irani Cup drought.

Notable Performances & Milestones

Record-breaking individual feats etched into Indian first-class cricket lore.

Highest Individual Score

Gundappa Viswanath · 247

Rest of India vs Bombay (1977–78 at Bengaluru)

A masterclass of wristy elegance against a lethal Bombay attack.

Double Century Milestone

Sarfaraz Khan · 222*

Mumbai vs Rest of India (2024–25 at Lucknow)

Became the first Mumbai batsman to score an Irani Cup double century, leading Mumbai to their 15th title.

Best Bowling in an Innings

Anil Kumble · 8/49

Rest of India vs Delhi (1992–93 at Wankhede)

A devastating spell of flippers and googlies that dismantled the Delhi batting lineup.

Legendary Milestone

Sachin Tendulkar · 103* at age 16

Secured his maiden Indian Test team tour to Pakistan right after this historic Irani Cup hundred.
