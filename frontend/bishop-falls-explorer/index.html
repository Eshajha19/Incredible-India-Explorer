🌿 Shillong · Meghalaya

Bishop Falls

A spectacular three-tiered cascade plunging 135 metres down the steep gorges of Shillong, famed for its twin relationship with Beadon Falls.

📍 Location & Scale

A Breathtaking Three-Tiered Drop

Located in the dense, rocky valleys of Shillong, Bishop Falls and its twin, Beadon Falls, crash into the same deep gorge from opposite sides.

  • Height: 135 meters (443 feet)
  • Source: Umiam River (Shillong Peak catchment)
  • Setting: Rocky, forest-clad gorge in Suna Valley
  • Best time to visit: Post-Monsoon (September to November)
  • Nearest City: Shillong (Approx. 2 km from Mawlai)
🔄 Twin Cascades

Bishop vs. Beadon Falls

These two majestic waterfalls tumble down the same steep gorge side-by-side. Use the tabs to compare their unique characteristics.

Bishop Falls

  • Height: 135 meters (443 feet)
  • Structure: Three-tiered, relatively uninterrupted steep drop.
  • Flow: Powerful and thick, creating a massive roar and mist in the gorge.
  • Surroundings: Framed by lush, dense forest.

Beadon Falls

  • Height: ~100 meters (approximate, often cited as slightly shorter than Bishop)
  • Structure: Rocky and interrupted. The water breaks over angular stones.
  • Flow: Turbulent and scattered due to the jagged rocks interrupting its fall.
  • Surroundings: Characterized by sharp, angular rock formations.
🌦️ Seasons

Seasonal Appearance

Like most waterfalls in Meghalaya, Bishop and Beadon Falls change dramatically with the seasons.

Monsoon

Monsoon Roar

During the intense monsoon (June–September), both falls swell tremendously. The gorge fills with dense mist, and the deafening roar of the water makes the viewpoints an exhilarating experience.

Post-Monsoon

Clear Cascades

In autumn and winter, the water level recedes. The distinctive three-tiered structure of Bishop Falls and the rocky interruptions of Beadon Falls become crisp and clear against the green valley.

🗺️ Map

Getting There

Bishop and Beadon Falls are located just off the NH40 highway, easily accessible from the center of Shillong.

Travel Notes

  • Accessibility: The falls are viewed from a designated viewpoint across the gorge near Mawlai. There is no direct path to the base.
  • Best Views: A single viewpoint offers a sweeping panoramic look at both falls plunging into the Suna valley.
  • Nearby Attractions: Ward's Lake, Umiam Lake, and Elephant Falls are also popular nearby destinations in Shillong.
