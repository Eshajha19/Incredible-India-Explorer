Vijayanagara Gold Coinage (The Varaha Standard)

The Vijayanagara Empire's gold Pagodas — struck to the enduring Varaha weight standard — carried royal and religious imagery from the boar emblem to the infant Balakrishna, and remained a trusted currency across South India and the Indian Ocean trade for centuries.
