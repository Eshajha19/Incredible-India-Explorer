Vande Bharat Express

Experience India's first indigenously designed, semi-high-speed, engine-less train, redefining modern rail travel.

Explore Details

Introduction

The Vande Bharat Express, also known as Train 18, represents a leap forward in India's railway technology. As a semi-high-speed, daytime intercity train, it is recognized for its aerodynamic design, modern amenities, and engine-less electric multiple unit (EMU) configuration.

Designed and manufactured by the Integral Coach Factory (ICF) in Chennai, this "Make in India" initiative showcases the country's capability to build world-class rolling stock, significantly reducing travel time between major cities while maximizing passenger comfort and safety.
