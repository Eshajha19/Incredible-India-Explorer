Dabur: Ayurveda to Modernity

From a small pharmacy in 1884 to India's trusted healthcare brand serving millions worldwide.

The Origin Story

🌿

1884: The Beginning

Dr. S.K. Burman founded Dabur in Kolkata as a small Ayurvedic pharmacy, producing herbal medicines for common ailments like cholera and malaria. The name "Dabur" derives from "Dr. Burman."

🏭

Ayurvedic Foundation

Unlike allopathic medicine companies of the era, Dabur focused exclusively on traditional Ayurvedic formulations, making ancient healthcare accessible to the masses at affordable prices.

📈

Early Growth

By the early 1900s, Dabur had established itself as a trusted name in herbal medicine, with products like Dabur Chyawanprash becoming household essentials across India.

The Ayurveda Connection

Dabur's commitment to Ayurveda goes beyond product development. The company maintains traditional manufacturing processes, sources herbs from sustainable farms, and invests in Ayurvedic research through the Dabur Research Foundation.
