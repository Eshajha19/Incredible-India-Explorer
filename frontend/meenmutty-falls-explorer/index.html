🌳 Kerala · Wayanad

Meenmutty Falls Explorer

The largest waterfall in Wayanad — a three-tiered cascade dropping roughly 300 metres through dense Western Ghats forest, reached only by a 2 km trek near the Banasura Sagar Dam.

📍 Location & Water Source

Where the falls come from

Meenmutty Falls lies near Padinjarathara in the Wayanad district of Kerala, close to the Banasura Sagar Dam and roughly 25–29 km from Kalpetta. The water comes from forest streams draining the Banasura hills of the Western Ghats, which gather and plunge over a series of rocky escarpments in three distinct tiers before continuing downstream toward the Kabini river system.

Note: this is the Meenmutty Falls near Banasura Sagar Dam — not to be confused with a separate, smaller Meenmutty Falls near Meppadi, which has been closed to visitors for several years.

  • Location: Padinjarathara, near Banasura Sagar Dam, Wayanad, Kerala
  • Distance: ~25-29 km from Kalpetta
  • Water source: Forest streams off the Banasura hills, Western Ghats
  • Height: ~300 m (984 ft) across three tiers — the largest waterfall in Wayanad
  • Structure: Three-tiered cascade, each tier reached by a separate stretch of trek
  • Best season: October–May (closed/dangerous during peak monsoon)

🌦️ Seasons

The Falls Across the Year

Meenmutty's flow swings sharply between the calm, swimmable falls of the dry months and a genuinely dangerous monsoon torrent that has claimed lives since 1991.

🌲 Forest Ecosystem

Western Ghats Forest Backdrop

The trek to Meenmutty runs through dense, moist deciduous and evergreen forest typical of the Wayanad plateau in the Western Ghats — part of a UNESCO World Heritage biodiversity hotspot. The dense canopy shelters diverse birdlife, and the trail passes near the Banasura Sagar Dam, whose reservoir and forested hills form the immediate backdrop to the falls.

🥾 Trek & Access

Getting to Each Tier

There's no vehicle access to the falls — only a roughly 2 km forest trek from the parking area, split across three legs of increasing difficulty.

Trek Notes

  • First tier: ~500 m walk from the entrance; smaller, shallow, safe for swimming
  • Second tier: A further ~500 m on steps built over boulders, along the stream
  • Third tier: The hardest ~500 m stretch, crossing slippery rocks with rope assistance; rewards with views of Banasura Sagar Dam and the valley
  • Permits: Forest Department entry permits and a nominal fee are usually required; a local guide is recommended

Getting There

Reach the parking area / entrance near Padinjarathara by road from Kalpetta, close to the Banasura Sagar Dam, then trek in on foot.

Travel Notes

  • Best season: October–May; avoid the peak monsoon (June–September), when the trail is closed or dangerous
  • Nearest town: Kalpetta (~25-29 km)
  • Nearest railhead: Kozhikode Railway Station · Nearest airport: Kozhikode (Calicut) International Airport
  • Note: The falls are sometimes closed entirely — check with local Forest Department / DTPC Wayanad before visiting
