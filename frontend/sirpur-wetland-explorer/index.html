Heritage

Wetland History

From a royal hunting ground to a protected urban sanctuary.

Sirpur Lake, located near the city of Mahasamund in Chhattisgarh, has a rich history. It was once a favored hunting ground for the local royalty and has evolved into a critical habitat for wildlife.

Over the decades, the lake has faced pressures from urbanization and agricultural expansion, making its recent conservation efforts all the more significant.

🏰

Historical Significance

The lake's name is derived from the nearby historical site of Sirpur, an ancient capital of the Dakshina Kosala kingdom, adding cultural depth to its ecological value.

Global Recognition

Ramsar Site

Designated in 2022, recognizing its importance for waterbird conservation.

Sirpur Wetland was designated as a Ramsar Site in 2022, becoming one of the newest additions to India's network of internationally important wetlands. This recognition highlights its role in supporting vulnerable bird species and maintaining regional biodiversity.

Protection

Conservation

Safeguarding the urban wetland for future generations.

Conservation efforts focus on preventing encroachment, managing solid waste, and controlling water pollution from nearby settlements. The Chhattisgarh State Wetland Authority is actively involved in preparing and implementing the management plan.

Community awareness programs and the promotion of eco-tourism are key strategies to ensure the long-term protection of the lake.
