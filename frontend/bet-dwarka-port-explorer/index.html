Bet Dwarka Ancient Port

An island story of anchors, shell-working and seaborne exchange at the mouth of the Gulf of Kutch.

Historical overview

Bet Dwarka, also known as Shankhodhar, is an island off Gujarat's Saurashtra coast. Its sheltered waters placed it on coastal routes moving between the Gulf of Kutch, the Makran coast and peninsular India. Literary traditions connect the island with Dwarka, while archaeology provides a separate, material record of maritime activity.

Archaeological significance

Marine and terrestrial investigations have recovered stone anchors, pottery, beads, shell objects and structural remains. These finds indicate changing phases of coastal settlement and exchange; interpretations continue as research develops.

Maritime trade

Anchors and harbour-related finds point to vessels using the island's natural landing places. Shell, fish products, beads and local craft goods joined wider Gujarat and Arabian Sea networks.
