Warang Citi Alphabet (𑢹𑣗𑣜𑣊 𑣏𑣂𑣕𑣂)

Warang Citi is a pure alphabetic writing system featuring independent vowel and consonant characters with specialized tone modifiers. Standardized under Unicode block U+118A0–U+118FF and taught at universities across Jharkhand and Odisha.

𑢹𑣉𑣉 𑣎𑣋𑣜 𑢶𑣕𑣦 𑢵𑣂𑣞𑣰𑣖 𑢷𑣂𑣊𑣂 𑢠𑣉𑣊𑣋
📖

Devanagari & Odia Transcriptions

In regional schools, newspapers, and community publications, Ho is also written in adapted Devanagari (in Jharkhand) and Odia script (in Mayurbhanj) using diacritics for checked glottal stops.
