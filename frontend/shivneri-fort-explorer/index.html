Shivneri Fort Explorer

Perched on a hill above Junnar in the Sahyadris, Shivneri Fort is revered as the birthplace of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, founder of the Maratha Empire — a natural stronghold shaped like a Shiv-Pind, guarded by seven zigzagging gates and centuries of history.

At a Glance

Overview

A compact but formidable hill fort guarding the old trade route from Desh to the port of Kalyan.

Shivneri Fort stands at an elevation of roughly 2,260 ft near Junnar in Maharashtra's Pune district. Encircled on all sides by steep, unassailable cliffs, its silhouette is often described as resembling a Shiv-Pind. Long before its Maratha legacy, the surrounding hills sheltered Buddhist rock-cut caves dating to the 1st century, and the fort itself guarded a vital trading route linking the Desh plateau to the coastal port of Kalyan.

Through the Centuries

History

From Yadava stronghold to Maratha cradle — Shivneri's long chain of rulers and sieges.

The hill was fortified under the Yadavas of Devagiri around the 12th–14th centuries, before passing to the Bahmani Sultanate and later the Nizamshahi rulers of Ahmednagar. In 1595, the Sultan of Ahmednagar granted the fort to Maloji Bhosale, grandfather of Shivaji Maharaj, bringing it into the Bhosale family's hands for generations to come.

    Sacred Ground

    Birthplace of Shivaji Maharaj

    Where Jijabai gave the Maratha Empire its founder, on 19 February 1630.

    Shiv Kunj & the Birth Chamber

    With her husband Shahaji Bhosale away on campaign, Jijabai stayed at the secure hilltop fort, where she gave birth to Shivaji on 19 February 1630. The ground floor of a modest two-storey building marks the exact birth spot, now watched over by a statue and cradle. Nearby, the Shivkunj memorial — its foundation laid and later inaugurated by Maharashtra's first Chief Minister, Yashwantrao Chavan — depicts young Shivaji describing his dreams of Swarajya to his mother.
