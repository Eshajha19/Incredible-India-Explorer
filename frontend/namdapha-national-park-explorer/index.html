Namdapha National Park Explorer

Discover India's 4th largest national park — a wild rainforest wilderness spanning an extraordinary altitudinal gradient from 200m tropical dipterocarp jungles to 4,571m snow-capped Himalayan peaks.

Altitudinal Span

Geography & Altitudinal Zones

Namdapha is famous for its extreme elevation gradient from 200 meters along the Noa-Dihing River to the 4,571-meter summit of Daphabum Peak, encompassing four distinct climate zones.

Unique Sanctuary

The Four Feline Big Cats of Namdapha

Namdapha is uniquely renowned as the only national park in the world harboring four feline big cat species within a single protected ecosystem.

Faunal Diversity

Red Panda & Endangered Mammals

Beyond big cats, Namdapha is home to 96+ mammal species, including India's only ape and endemic rare mammals.

Avian Paradise

Birdlife Explorer (425+ Species)

Discover the rich birdlife of Namdapha's primary rainforests — from massive Great Hornbills to secretive forest ducks and tragopans.
