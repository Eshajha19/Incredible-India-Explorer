⚔️ Senior Peacetime Military Decoration

Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM)

Awarded to members of the Indian Armed Forces for distinguished service of an exceptional order in peace and operational command.

1960 Instituted
GoI / MoD Conferred By
3 Services Army, Navy, Air Force

⏳ History & Genesis

Instituted on January 26, 1960 by the Government of India, the medal was originally designated as the "Vishisht Seva Medal, Class II". On January 27, 1967, it was formally re-titled as the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM).

The AVSM was established to acknowledge high-level leadership, technical innovation, and exceptional command duty during non-conflict periods. It ranks above the Vishisht Seva Medal (VSM) and below the Param Vishisht Seva Medal (PVSM). It serves as the peacetime equivalent to the wartime Uttam Yudh Seva Medal (UYSM).

📜 Eligibility Criteria

The Ati Vishisht Seva Medal may be awarded to personnel across all ranks of the military services who display distinguished service of an exceptional order:

Eligible Military Services

  • Indian Army: Senior command staff, corps commanders, division leads, and operational logistics officers.
  • Indian Navy: Fleet commanders, naval dockyard directors, submarine squadron leads, and naval aviators.
  • Indian Air Force: Air Officers Commanding (AOC), fighter squadron leaders, and flight safety directors.
  • Reserve & Auxiliary Forces: Territorial Army officers, Auxiliary Forces, and Military Nursing Service (MNS).

Service & Performance Benchmarks

  • Exceptional Leadership: Extraordinary devotion to duty during non-combat strategic operations, disaster response, or defense R&D.
  • Subsequent Awards (Bar): A silver bar is attached to the ribbon for each subsequent award of the AVSM.
  • Post-Nominals: Recipients are entitled to append the post-nominal initials "AVSM" after their names.

🎨 Medal Design & Insignia

The Ati Vishisht Seva Medal is crafted with official military heraldry and standardized dimensions:

🎖️ Medal Obverse

Circular silver medallion (35mm diameter). Features a prominent five-pointed star embossed in the center, overlaid with the State Emblem of India (Ashoka Lion Capital with Satyameva Jayate).

⭐ Medal Reverse

Features a five-pointed star embossed in relief with the Hindi inscription "अति विशिष्ट सेवा मेडल" (Ati Vishisht Seva Medal) embossed along the upper rim.

🎗️ Ribbon & Bar Spec

Suspended by a 32mm golden-yellow silk ribbon divided into three equal parts by two vertical dark blue stripes. Subsequent awards are marked by a silver bar with a central star.

🛡️ Armed Forces Service Coverage

The AVSM recognizes exceptional contributions across all arms of the Indian Armed Forces:

🪖 Indian Army

Conferred on General Officers and staff for outstanding corps command, high-altitude border infrastructure development, counter-insurgency command, and tactical operational management.

⚓ Indian Navy

Awarded to Flag Officers and Commanders for maritime fleet operations, blue-water exercises, indigenization of naval warships, and strategic ocean surveillance.

✈️ Indian Air Force

Honours Air Marshals and Commanders for air defense modernization, aerial logistics support in remote regions, precision air exercises, and flight safety innovations.

📋 Selection Process Workflow

Recommendations for the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal undergo rigorous multi-level military and defense screening:

1
Unit Recommendation
Initiated by Commanding Officer or Flag/Air Officer Commanding.
2
Command Review
Evaluated by Regional Army, Naval, or Air Command HQs.
3
Service HQ Board
Screened by Chiefs of Staff Committee & Service HQ Boards.
4
MoD Screening
Ministry of Defence conducts vigilance & integrity audit.
5
Presidential Sanction
Approved by the President of India (Supreme Commander).
6
Investiture
Announced on Republic/Independence Day & presented at Rashtrapati Bhavan.

📅 Historical Timeline

Key historical milestones of the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal:

1960

Medal Institution

Instituted on January 26, 1960 by President Rajendra Prasad as "Vishisht Seva Medal, Class II".

1967

Re-Designation as AVSM

Re-titled as "Ati Vishisht Seva Medal" (AVSM) on January 27, 1967, establishing the modern name.

1980

Wartime Equivalent Pairing

With the institution of the Yudh Seva Medal series, AVSM was formally established as the peacetime equivalent to the Uttam Yudh Seva Medal (UYSM).

2020s

Tri-Service & Modern Integration

Selection criteria expanded to encompass cyber defense, joint theater commands, and indigenized military tech R&D.
