Captain Lakshmi Sahgal Explorer

Discover the story of Captain Lakshmi Sahgal — physician, commander of INA’s Rani of Jhansi Regiment, and a pioneering voice for women's leadership in India’s independence movement.

About the Hero

Medicine, leadership, and the all-women Rani of Jhansi Regiment

Captain Lakshmi Sahgal was one of the most influential female figures in the INA. She combined her medical training with bold military leadership, inspiring women across India to join the fight for freedom.

Early Life & Training

Born in Madurai, she became a physician before moving to Rangoon. Her medical work brought her close to the INA and its larger struggle against British colonial rule.

INA Leadership

In 1943, Subhas Chandra Bose appointed her commander of the Rani of Jhansi Regiment, the Indian Army's first all-women combat unit.

Women's Empowerment

Her command showed the world that Indian women were ready to serve, sacrifice, and shape the nation's destiny in battle as well as in politics.

Indian National Army

Leading INA's women soldiers

Captain Lakshmi Sahgal joined the Indian National Army after meeting Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose in 1942. She became Minister of Women’s Affairs for the Azad Hind government and earned respect as a commander who empowered volunteers from across India.

She worked to build training, uniforms, and a shared sense of purpose for women who were determined to fight not only for independence, but also for greater equality in the new nation.

INA Facts

  • The Rani of Jhansi Regiment was named after the warrior queen of Jhansi.
  • It was formed in 1943 as an all-women infantry regiment of the Azad Hind Fauj.
  • Lakshmi Sahgal's leadership helped boost support for the INA among Indian women.
Rani of Jhansi Regiment

A pioneering all-women combat unit

Formation

1943 — Subhas Chandra Bose announced the formation of an all-women regiment to prove that Indian women could lead and fight alongside men.

Training & Discipline

Women were trained in drill, first aid, and military discipline. Captain Lakshmi Sahgal emphasized courage, unity, and respect for the INA ideal.

Symbolism

The regiment carried the legacy of Rani of Jhansi and became a powerful symbol of female patriotism during the freedom struggle.

Timeline

Major milestones in Lakshmi Sahgal's freedom journey

Legacy

A lifetime of service and inspiration

Captain Lakshmi Sahgal remained a voice for social justice long after independence. She continued to champion women's rights, healthcare, and nonviolent progress while becoming a beloved cultural symbol of bravery and equality.

Her story continues to inspire women in India and around the world who seek leadership roles in politics, defence, and public service.

Quote

"Freedom is not a gesture. It is the result of persistent struggle and sacrifice."
