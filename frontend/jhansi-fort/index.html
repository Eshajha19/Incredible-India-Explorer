Fort overview

A fort of courage, resistance and freedom memory

Jhansi Fort is remembered as a symbol of India's First War of Independence and the bravery of Rani Lakshmibai. This explorer combines fort architecture, Bundela history and 1857 heritage in one page.

Rani Lakshmibai legacy

The Queen of Jhansi made the fort a powerful national memory site. Her courage and leadership remain central to the story of Jhansi.

Revolt of 1857

The fort is closely tied to the 1857 uprising and the broader story of anti-colonial resistance.

Hilltop architecture

Walls, gates, bastions and elevation made the fort strategically important.
