Amul The Cooperative Giant that Fueled the White Revolution

Born out of a farmers' revolt against monopolistic middlemen in Kaira, Gujarat, Amul (Anand Milk Union Limited) sparked the White Revolution (Operation Flood), turning India into the world's leading milk-producing nation powered by 3.6 million farmer-owners.

Brand Origin & History

The 1946 Spark in Kaira: From Revolt to Revolution

In the 1940s, dairy farmers in Kaira (Anand), Gujarat were severely exploited by the monopoly Polson dairy, which procured milk at depressed prices while reaping hefty profits from the Bombay Milk Scheme. Frustrated farmers turned to freedom fighter Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, who advised them to establish their own cooperative and withhold milk until their demands were met.

On December 14, 1946, the Kaira District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union was registered under the visionary leadership of Tribhuvandas Patel. Soon after, young engineer Dr. Verghese Kurien joined Anand, teaming up with dairy technologist H. M. Dalaya to pioneer producing skimmed milk powder and butter from buffalo milk for the first time in global dairy history.

"India's place in the sun would come from the partnership between wisdom of its rural people and skill of its professionals."
— Dr. Verghese Kurien, Architect of Operation Flood

H. M. Dalaya: The brilliant technologist who invented the process of making milk powder and baby food from buffalo milk.
Cooperative Excellence

The Anand 3-Tier Cooperative Model

How Amul returns over 80 paise of every consumer rupee directly into the hands of dairy farmers.

Tier 1
🏡

Village Dairy Societies (VDCS)

18,600+ Village Societies collect milk twice daily directly from 3.6 million dairy farmers with instant electronic fat testing and transparent payment.

Tier 2
🏭

District Cooperative Milk Unions

18 District Unions operate state-of-the-art automated chilling, pasteurization, and manufacturing plants, providing veterinary care and cattle feed.

Tier 3
🌐

State Cooperative Federation (GCMMF)

Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation manages national & global marketing, brand stewardship, cold-chain distribution, and retail networks.

Advertising & Brand Identity

The Legendary 'Amul Girl' (Utterly Butterly Delicious)

Created in 1966 by Sylvester da Cunha and Eustace Fernandes of daCunha Communications, the blue-haired, polka-dotted dress Amul Girl is one of the world's longest-running outdoor advertising campaigns.

With its witty, pun-filled commentary on politics, cinema, cricket, and pop culture, Amul topical hoardings have mirrored India's social history for nearly 60 years with humorous warmth and fearless creativity.
