Biography

A young revolutionary from Sindh

Hemu Kalani was a young Indian freedom fighter from Sukkur in Sindh, then part of British India. He became associated with revolutionary resistance during the Quit India period.

He is widely remembered for an attempt to derail a train carrying British troops and military supplies by removing railway track fastenings. The plan was discovered, and he was arrested before the intended sabotage could succeed.

After a colonial trial, Hemu Kalani was executed in 1943. His story remains a powerful example of youth participation and sacrifice in India's freedom struggle.
