Prime Minister's Award for Excellence in Public Administration

Discover India's highest national honour for civil servants — recognizing innovative and impactful governance initiatives, transformative scheme implementation, and public service that moves the nation forward.

2006 Established Year
21 Apr Civil Services Day
10 Awards Per Year

📜 History of the Award

The Prime Minister's Award for Excellence in Public Administration was instituted in 2006 by the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Government of India. It is the highest national honour conferred upon civil servants for outstanding performance in public administration.

Genesis and Vision

The award was conceived to promote excellence in public administration by recognizing officers and organizations whose work reflects innovation, integrity, and impact. It aimed to create role models within the civil services and to encourage replicable models of governance across districts and states.

Evolution Over the Years

Originally operated with a focus on the implementation of flagship programmes, the scheme has evolved over time to place greater emphasis on innovation in governance. In 2020, the award framework was revamped into three categories with a focus on innovation, transparency, and measurable impact, with nominations now invited through a fully online portal.

Significance and Impact

Winners are honoured with a medal and a scroll of honour presented personally by the Prime Minister of India on Civil Services Day (21 April). The award has become a benchmark of excellence for district administrations, states, and individual civil servants, inspiring governance innovations across the country.

Institutional Framework

The award is administered by the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG), which coordinates the nomination process, screening, and the final selection committee chaired by the Cabinet Secretary.

🎯 Purpose of the Award

The award serves as a cornerstone of India's efforts to build a more efficient, transparent, and people-centric administration.

🏅

Encourage Excellence

Motivate civil servants and government organizations to strive for the highest standards of professionalism and performance in public service delivery.

💡

Reward Innovation

Celebrate creative, out-of-the-box solutions that solve real governance challenges — from last-mile delivery of welfare schemes to digital transformation of services.

🔁

Promote Replicable Models

Showcase successful initiatives so that proven governance models can be adopted and scaled by other districts and states across the country.

👥

Strengthen People-Centric Governance

Reinforce the principle that the purpose of public administration is citizen welfare — fostering transparency, accountability, and responsiveness.

🌟

Build Role Models

Create visible heroes within the civil services who inspire younger officers to take on challenges with commitment and creativity.

🇮🇳

Strengthen Nation Building

Align public administration with the vision of a Viksit Bharat (Developed India), rewarding efforts that accelerate national development.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

🏛️ Category I & II — Districts

Officers and organizations of the Central and State Governments working at the district level, implementing priority programmes and demonstrating innovation in governance, are eligible for the district categories of the award.

🏢 Category III — States / UTs

The state/UT category recognizes officers and organizations at the state level for outstanding work in specific sectors, priority programmes, or areas of exceptional governance impact.

👥 Individual or Collective

Entries may be submitted on an individual or collective basis, recognizing both the leadership of individual officers and the achievements of teams and departments.

📝 How to Apply

Nominations are invited annually through the online portal of DARPG, routed through State/UT governments, which conduct the initial scrutiny and forward recommended cases to the Government of India.

Core Evaluation Themes

Applications are assessed on the basis of innovation, efficiency, transparency, measurable impact, sustainability, and replicability of the initiative in transforming public service delivery.

🏷️ Award Categories

Under the revamped framework, 10 awards are conferred every year across three categories, honouring both districts and states for excellence in governance.

🏆

Category I

District Level

  • Two awards for districts
  • For innovation in governance and implementation of priority programmes
  • Recognizes districts that set the benchmark for others
🥇

Category II

District Level

  • Six awards for districts
  • For innovation and implementation of priority programmes
  • Honours a wider pool of high-performing districts across India
🥈

Category III

State / UT Level

  • Two awards for States and Union Territories
  • For outstanding work in a specific sector or priority programme
  • Recognizes individual or collective state-level contributions

What Winners Receive

Recipients are presented a medal and a scroll of honour (Sanad) by the Prime Minister of India during the Civil Services Day celebrations held at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi on 21 April.

⚙️ Selection Process

The selection process is designed to be transparent, merit-based, and rigorous, ensuring that only the most deserving initiatives receive the honour.

1

Online Application & State Scrutiny

Districts and state departments submit their nominations through the DARPG online portal. State/UT governments scrutinize the entries and recommend the most promising cases to the Government of India.

2

National-Level Screening

Shortlisted nominations are examined by a national screening committee, which assesses each initiative against the evaluation themes of innovation, efficiency, transparency, impact, sustainability, and replicability.

3

Final Selection Committee

A final selection committee, chaired by the Cabinet Secretary with senior government secretaries as members, reviews the shortlist and recommends the winners.

4

Prime Minister's Approval

The final list of awardees is placed before the Prime Minister for approval, after which the awards are conferred at the Civil Services Day ceremony.

Evaluation Matrix

  • Innovation: Novelty and creativity of the approach in solving governance challenges
  • Impact: Tangible, measurable improvement in public service delivery and citizen welfare
  • Transparency: Open, accountable, and citizen-friendly processes
  • Sustainability: Long-term viability of the initiative beyond the initial effort
  • Replicability: Potential of the model to be adopted by other districts and states

⭐ Success Stories

Over the years, the award has celebrated remarkable governance transformations across India. These themed stories capture the spirit of the initiatives that earn national recognition.

🏔️

Aspirational Districts Transformation

District administrations recognized for pulling their districts out of backwardness through coordinated multi-departmental action, data-driven governance, and focused improvement of health, education, nutrition, and infrastructure indicators.

🏥

Last-Mile Health Delivery

Innovations in tele-medicine, mobile health units, and community health programmes that brought quality healthcare to remote villages — from COVID-19 vaccination drives reaching every doorstep to screening camps in far-flung regions.

💻

Digital Governance

Initiatives that digitized public services, reduced corruption and delays, and empowered citizens — online service delivery, e-governance platforms, and transparent administration that rebuilt public trust.

🏗️

Flagship Scheme Implementation

Excellence in implementing national flagship programmes such as PM Awas Yojana-Gramin, PM-KISAN, PM SVANidhi, and PM Vishwakarma — ensuring welfare benefits reach the intended beneficiaries efficiently and transparently.

🌾

Agriculture & Livelihood

District-level innovations that boosted farm productivity, promoted Farmer Producer Organizations, and created sustainable livelihood opportunities for rural communities.

♻️

Sustainable Development

Governance initiatives around water conservation, renewable energy, waste management, and climate resilience that demonstrated how public administration can drive sustainable development.

Why These Stories Matter

Each recognized initiative proves that committed public servants can transform lives. By sharing these models nationally, the award ensures that a breakthrough in one district becomes a blueprint for governance across the country.
