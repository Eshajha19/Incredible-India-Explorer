A MOVING PALACE

Royal travel, reimagined for the railway age

Palace on Wheels was launched in 1982 as a joint initiative of Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation and Indian Railways. Its design draws on the private saloons associated with the former princely states of Rajputana, turning a rail itinerary into an immersive heritage experience.

The signature journey travels overnight, arriving each morning at a new destination. That rhythm makes the train itself part of the destination: breakfast can begin in one landscape while the next day's fort, wildlife reserve or desert city is already waiting outside.

HERITAGE

From princely saloons to a modern luxury icon

The visual language of the train recalls royal carriages once associated with rulers of India's princely states.

Before 1947

Royal railway saloons

Palace-style coaches were used by rulers and dignitaries, inspiring the later concept of a train that could carry the atmosphere of princely travel.

1982

Palace on Wheels launches

The inaugural luxury service begins in association with Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation and Indian Railways.

2000s onward

Luxury tourism grows

The service evolves with refreshed interiors and modern hospitality while retaining a distinctly Rajasthani aesthetic.

7 NIGHTS • 8 DAYS

Trace the royal circuit

The official itinerary runs New Delhi → Jaipur → Sawai Madhopur → Chittorgarh → Udaipur → Jaisalmer → Jodhpur → Bharatpur → Agra → New Delhi.

ON BOARD

Life between destinations

♛

Accommodation

Air-conditioned saloons offer twin-bedded chambers with attached baths; the current official site lists Deluxe, Super Deluxe and Presidential Suite accommodation.

✦

Dining

Restaurant cars serve Indian, Rajasthani, Continental and Chinese cuisine, making meals part of the journey rather than a pause from it.

☕

Lounges & service

Common lounges, bars, attentive attendants and onboard refreshment service create a social, hotel-like atmosphere while the train moves overnight.

✧

Spa & assistance

The Rajasthan Tourism description includes a royal spa and assistance such as wheelchairs and attendants, subject to current service availability.

DESTINATIONS

Eight cities, eight different moods

CULTURE

The itinerary is the experience

Fort & palace heritage

Jaipur's Hawa Mahal and royal architecture, Chittorgarh Fort, Udaipur's lake city and Jodhpur's Mehrangarh landscape connect the train to Rajasthan's courtly history.

Desert & wildlife

Ranthambore brings a wildlife dimension, while Jaisalmer introduces the Thar Desert, camel safaris and golden sandstone architecture.

Birds & Mughal heritage

Bharatpur connects the journey to Keoladeo National Park, while Agra closes the circuit with the Taj Mahal and Mughal heritage.

DID YOU KNOW?

Five details worth remembering

01

The inaugural run began on 26 January 1982.

02

The signature itinerary is a 7-night, 8-day circuit.

03

The train travels by night so passengers can spend daylight hours exploring destinations.

04

Its route combines Rajasthan's forts, desert and wildlife with Bharatpur and Agra in Uttar Pradesh.

05

The train's coaches and interiors deliberately evoke the visual language of princely Rajasthan.
