National Bravery Awards

Honouring spontaneous, extraordinary acts of courage, valor, and selflessness displayed by brave children across India under perilous conditions.

1957 Instituted
ICCW & GoI Organized By
5 Categories Sub-Awards
1,000+ Heroes Recognized

⏳ History & Genesis

The National Bravery Awards trace their inception back to October 2, 1957. During a Gandhi Jayanti performance at Ramlila Ground in Delhi, a fire broke out in a shamiana. 14-year-old scout Harish Chandra Mehra promptly used his pocket knife to slash the burning canvas tent, rescuing hundreds of trapped attendees in the presence of Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru.

Deeply moved by Harish's selflessness, PM Nehru asked the Indian Council for Child Welfare (ICCW) to establish an annual national award scheme to recognize brave children across India. Harish Chandra Mehra became the first recipient in 1957. Over the decades, special sub-awards were instituted in memory of iconic child heroes such as Sanjay Chopra, Geeta Chopra, and Bapu Gaidhani.

🎯 Purpose & Core Objectives

The National Bravery Awards fulfill three foundational national missions:

🌟 1. Public Recognition

To confer high-profile national recognition on children who distinguish themselves through acts of spontaneous, selfless courage in the face of grave risk.

🔥 2. Inspiring Youth Valor

To set inspiring examples for the nation's youth, encouraging presence of mind, empathy, and courage during fires, floods, animal attacks, or terror threats.

🎓 3. Educational Sponsorship

To provide complete financial support and scholarships through the ICCW Indira Gandhi Scholarship scheme, enabling recipients to complete school and university degrees.

📜 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must satisfy the following official eligibility benchmarks established by the ICCW:

Age & Incident Timeframe

  • Age Limit: Must be an Indian citizen aged between 6 and 18 years on the date of the incident.
  • Timeframe: The act of bravery must have occurred between July 1 of the previous year and June 30 of the award year.

Act Benchmark Requirements

  • Spontaneous Selflessness: Act must be a spontaneous, selfless deed performed under grave personal risk or life-threatening crisis.
  • Police & DM Verification: Verified by District Magistrates, Police Superintendents, or State ICCW councils.

🏅 Award Categories

The National Bravery Awards encompass 5 specialized sub-categories honoring varying levels of courage:

👑 1. Bharat Award

Instituted in 1987. The highest category presented for an exceptionally supreme act of courage in saving human lives against overwhelming odds.

🛡️ 2. Sanjay Chopra Award

Instituted in 1978 in memory of Sanjay Chopra, who fought off armed kidnappers in Delhi. Presented to boys for outstanding acts of bravery.

🌺 3. Geeta Chopra Award

Instituted in 1978 in memory of Geeta Chopra. Presented to girls for displaying extraordinary bravery and resistance in dangerous situations.

🌱 4. Bapu Gaidhani Award

Instituted in 1988 in memory of Bapu Gaidhani, who sacrificed his life rescuing cattle from a burning shed. Presented for extraordinary courage in saving lives/nature.

🎖️ 5. General National Bravery Award

Presented to children across India who display spontaneous courage and presence of mind during river rescues, animal attacks, or fires.

📋 Selection Process Workflow

Selection for the National Bravery Awards follows a rigorous multi-tier verification process:

1
Nomination
Submitted by State ICCWs, District Magistrates, Police, or School Heads.
2
Verification
Police FIR reports & DM investigation verify facts & incident timeline.
3
High-Powered Committee
Joint Committee of Ministries (MWCD, Defence, Home) & ICCW reviews cases.
4
Final Sanction
Selection of awardees across the 5 sub-categories finalized.
5
PM Presentation
Conferred by the Prime Minister of India on Republic Day eve.
6
Parade & Honors
Awardees participate in the Republic Day Parade on Kartavya Path.

📅 Historical Timeline

Key historical milestones of the National Bravery Awards:

1957

Medal Inception

Instituted by ICCW inspired by PM Nehru following Harish Chandra Mehra's Ramlila Ground tent rescue.

1978

Sanjay & Geeta Chopra Awards

Instituted to commemorate the heroic resistance of the Chopra siblings against kidnappers in Delhi.

1987

Bharat Award Instituted

Created as the highest category award for supreme acts of valour.

1988

Bapu Gaidhani Award Instituted

Created to honor acts of bravery in saving lives or environment.
