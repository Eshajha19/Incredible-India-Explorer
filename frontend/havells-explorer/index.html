Origin • 1958–1971

A trader becomes a manufacturer

Havells' story begins before the modern consumer brand: Qimat Rai Gupta started trading electrical goods in Delhi in 1958 and acquired the Havells brand in 1971.

Founder lineage

Qimat Rai Gupta's starting point

Gupta moved from Punjab to Delhi and began as an electrical-goods trader in Bhagirath Palace. The trading business gave him a close view of the market and its distribution networks.

The evolution of Havells

Made in India

The manufacturing story

Havells repeatedly invested in Indian production capacity and in-house manufacturing as it expanded beyond switchgear into consumer categories.

Manufacturing footprint

Company material has cited state-of-the-art plants across locations including Haridwar, Baddi, Sahibabad, Faridabad, Alwar, Ghiloth and Neemrana, with the exact footprint changing as the group evolves.

Why it mattersIn-house manufacturing and testing helped Havells control quality while expanding from industrial switchgear into consumer-facing products.
Brand evolution

From electrical specialist to consumer ecosystem

Havells' portfolio has broadened through organic expansion and acquisitions, bringing multiple brands and categories under one group.

Havells

Core consumer electrical brand spanning fans, switches, lighting, appliances, water solutions and more.

Lloyd

Consumer-durables play centered on air conditioners and a broader appliance portfolio.

Crabtree

Premium modular switches and electrical accessories within the group's brand architecture.

Standard

Electrical and consumer products including switchgear, cables, modular switches and fans.

Brand building

Distribution, showrooms and service became part of the product

Havells developed exclusive Havells Galaxy showrooms and service initiatives alongside its product portfolio, helping turn electrical goods into a recognizable consumer brand.
