Rajasthan Folk Art

Mandana Painting

The decorative wall and floor art of Rajasthan — drawn by women to mark festivals, weddings, and the sacred threshold of the home.

The Threshold Art of Rajasthan

Traditional Motifs

Mandana motifs are geometric and stylised, drawn freehand without preparatory sketches. Each motif carries ritual meaning and is drawn for a specific occasion.
