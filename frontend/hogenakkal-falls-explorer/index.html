🌊 Kaveri River · Dharmapuri District, Tamil Nadu

Explore Hogenakkal Falls

Known as the "Smoking Rocks" (Hoge = Smoke, Kal = Rock in Kannada), Hogenakkal is where the mighty Kaveri River bifurcates across ancient carbonatite bedrock, creating roaring cascades, natural plunge pools, and world-famous circular coracle (parisal) journeys.

🏞️ Carbonatite Rock Formations (2.4 Ga) 🛶 Parisal Coracle Boat Rides 🌿 Herbal Oil Massages & Medicinal Waters 📍 Tamil Nadu – Karnataka Border

Main Smoke Cascade (Hogenakkal)

Primary 20m Multi-Stream Plunge

Where the river drops 20 meters into a deep rock funnel, generating a dense vapor fog resembling smoke that gives the falls its historic Kannada name.

Location: Pennagaram Taluk, Dharmapuri
Water Body: Kaveri River (Cauvery)
Highlights: Smoking mist, natural rock potholes, roaring sound
Geological Heritage

World's Oldest Carbonatite Rocks & Fluvial Potholes

Hogenakkal is home to rare carbonatite igneous rocks dating back over 2.4 billion years (Paleoproterozoic Era), studied worldwide by geologists.

01

Carbonatite Intrusion (2.4 Ga)

Rare igneous rock containing over 50% carbonate minerals crystallized along the Dharwar Craton rift zone, ranking among the oldest recorded carbonatites on Earth.

02

Differential Hydraulic Erosion

Swirling pebbles and sediment carried by the fast-moving Kaveri carved deep, smooth cylindrical potholes (kettle basins) directly into the hard bedrock.

03

Gorge Bifurcation

The Kaveri splits into dozens of interwoven channels across rocky ledges before converging in a dramatic canyon funnel known as the "Smoking Chasm".

04

Medicinal Water Enrichment

As the river meanders through virgin herbal forests and Melagiri medicinal hills, the waters absorb beneficial plant minerals, traditionally prized for rejuvenating hydrotherapy.

Post-Monsoon Peak & Coracle Safaris (October – February)

The ideal time to explore Hogenakkal. River flow is robust yet calm enough for licensed parisal (coracle) boat rides to navigate through the deep gorge. Weather is pleasant, fish markets are vibrant, and all viewpoints and footbridges are accessible.

Flow: Moderate, Crystal & Roaring Boating: 100% Operational (Full Gorge Circuit) Highlights: Parisal rides, Oil massage & Fresh river fish
Viewpoints & Excursions

Key Viewpoints & Local Attractions

Top spots to visit around the Hogenakkal falls complex.

🌉

Hanging Footbridge

Suspension pedestrian bridge offering thrilling panoramic aerial vistas directly above the roaring gorge and carbonatite chasms.

🛶

Parisal Coracle Ride Circuit

Traditional circular boats crafted from bamboo and waterproof hide take visitors into tranquil gorge coves and right up to gentle spray zones.

🎬

Cine Falls (Film Shooting Gorge)

Famous shooting location for classic cinema (e.g. Mani Ratnam's *Roja* and *Raavan*) featuring dramatic perpendicular rock walls.

🌳

Melagiri Hill Forests & Wildlife Sanctuary

The adjoining forest sanctuary connects the Western and Eastern Ghats, sheltering wild Asian elephants, leopards, and medicinal flora.

🐟

Fresh Catch River Market & Fish Fry Stalls

Local fishermen sell fresh Kaveri catch (Katla, Rohu, Keluthi) freshly marinated in regional spices and prepared beside the riverside.

🌿

Traditional Herbal Oil Massages

Generational masseurs use herbal oils infused with forest herbs, followed by natural hydrotherapy showers under gentle waterfall streams.

Eco Guidelines

Responsible Tourism & Safety Protocol

Help preserve the pristine Kaveri ecosystem and ensure a safe, sustainable journey.

🦺

Strict Life Jacket Mandatory Rule

Always wear official ISI-marked life jackets before boarding coracles. Never stand or rock circular boats in deep currents.

🚯

Zero Plastic in the Gorge

Hogenakkal is an eco-sensitive zone. Carry back all water bottles, food wrappers, and avoid single-use plastics.

🎫

Official Ticket Counters Only

Book coracle rides and massage tokens exclusively through official Tamil Nadu Tourism Development Corporation (TTDC) counters.

🏊‍♂️

Respect Restricted Bathing Zones

Submerged rock crevasses and sudden vortex currents can be hazardous; bathe only in designated shallow ghats with lifeguards.
