Simlipal National Park Explorer

Immerse yourself in one of India's largest biosphere reserves in Mayurbhanj, Odisha — famed for roaring 399-meter waterfalls, wild elephant herds, 94+ orchid species, and the world's only wild melanistic black tigers.

Spectacular Cascades

Waterfalls & Deep River Gorges

Simlipal's high plateau feeds majestic waterfalls that plunge into forested abysses across the Eastern Ghats.

World Exclusive

The Melanistic (Black) Tigers of Simlipal

Simlipal is the only place on Earth where wild pseudo-melanistic black tigers exist. A rare recessive gene mutation (Transmembrane Peptidase Q) causes their stripes to broaden and merge, giving them a striking dark coat.

🧬 60%+ Population Trait: Genetic research by NCBS confirms that over 60% of Simlipal's tiger population carries this unique coat mutation.
Rich Biodiversity

Mayurbhanj Elephants & Wildlife

Simlipal forms the core of the Mayurbhanj Elephant Reserve, providing sanctuary to hundreds of wild Asian Elephants, Sambar deer, and Hill Mynas.

Botanical Paradise

Forest Types & 94+ Orchid Species

Named after the red silk cotton tree ('Simul'), Simlipal's varied elevations nurture moist Sal forests and 94 wild orchid species.

Indigenous Stewardship

Tribal Communities & Cultural Heritage

Simlipal has been protected for centuries by indigenous tribes who live in harmony with the forest.

History & Biosphere Timeline
