🔍 Mysore vs. Tanjore Painting Distinction

Mysore Painting

  • Gesso Relief: Subtle, low-relief Gacho embossing.
  • Embellishments: Pure gold foil only; no heavy beads or glass stones.
  • Color Palette: Soft, delicate, muted natural shades.
  • Expressions: Graceful, serene, and intricate facial contours.

Tanjore Painting

  • Gesso Relief: High, heavy 3D gesso plaster relief.
  • Embellishments: Gold leaf embedded with cut glass, pearls, and precious gems.
  • Color Palette: Vivid, primary, and saturated color tones.
  • Expressions: Bold, larger-than-life iconography.
